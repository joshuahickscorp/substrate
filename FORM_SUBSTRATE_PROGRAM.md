## 0. The lineage, honestly

This project began as a biology-grounded V-JEPA augmentation program. It became Mixture of
Perspectives: many cheap thinking modes over one frozen perceptual substrate, scored on density. It
then became a multiple-substrate, multiple-cognitive-currency program. Each era left assets: a
falsification engine that survived everything, a small set of hardened positives, a large and
valuable map of dead mechanisms, and a caching economy that makes a laptop and a Mac Studio real
scientific instruments.

Each era also left a shape in the imagination. First the V-JEPA pipe: 64 frames, pooled latents, a
tiny shell breathing through inherited geometry. Then the router: perspectives as arms of one
arbitration problem. Those shapes did work. They are no longer the center.

The new ontology:

```text
any observation form
-> referent-aligned form substrate
-> mode ecology and memory
-> performance-measured operation
-> falsified capability
-> controlled substrate rewrite only when inherited forms fail
```

The old project is the fossil record of the new one. Nothing is deleted. Everything is reinterpreted.

## 1. The object: a form substrate

A form substrate is a referent-preserving, form-receiving, memory-bearing, performance-measured
computational medium. It can ingest multiple forms of data, bind them to shared referents, preserve
task-relevant factors, align perspectives, route among internal modes, retrieve memory across forms,
predict action consequences, and, only when licensed by evidence, rewrite its own internal
representation under controlled plasticity.

It is not merely an LLM, a JEPA, a multimodal encoder, a fusion head, a vector database, an MoE, a
router, a symbolic system, a world model, a blank neural network, or a philosophical metaphor. It is
the operational medium where forms become usable.

It is judged on exactly eight axes: alignment, transfer, memory, consequence, plasticity, density,
performance, falsifiability. And it is disqualified by simple failures:

- A substrate that accepts many tensors but cannot bind referents is not a substrate.
- A substrate that aligns forms but cannot transfer is not general.
- A substrate that remembers only within one form is not referent-bound.
- A substrate that updates but forgets its old forms is not developmental.
- A substrate that wins only by more compute is not dense.
- A substrate that cannot fail is not science.

## 2. The primitive: form

A form is an observation family plus its machine-readable contract. Video, dense visual tokens,
image features, audio, speech, text, language hidden states, symbolic programs, equations, graph
states, code, action traces, proprioceptive states, reward histories, environment states, telemetry,
memory episodes, predicted futures, counterfactual variants, agent logs, tool traces. Any of these
can be a form. None of them is privileged.

The contract is enforced at intake (substrate/form.py): tag, kind, source, feature shape, objective
family, referent ids, optional factor labels, matched control when evidence-bearing, provenance,
license, trainable flag, and (in the manifests) byte cost and construction cost.

Without referents, a form is a pile of vectors. Without controls, a form is a story. Without
transfer, a form is only a format. Without cost accounting, a form is an unpriced luxury.

## 3. The anchor: referent

A referent is the shared thing multiple forms are about: a clip, an object, a scene, an event, an
episode, an utterance, an equation, a program, a trajectory, a goal, an action, a memory, an
environment state, a counterfactual variant, a task instance, a before-and-after intervention.

The referent is the anti-hallucination anchor. If video, audio, text, graph, and action all point at
the same referent, the substrate can be TESTED on whether they describe the same world state. If they
do not share referents, alignment is fake. Referent discipline is the first layer of intelligence
this program will admit.

## 4. What we inherit as known

The fossil record is not decoration. It constrains every F-series design:

- Pretraining is real: a frozen inherited encoder beats a random-init same-architecture control on
  nuisance-heavy decoding (+0.28 class of effect, direction hardened by bootstrap; single-split
  significance held at HOLD pending multi-seed). Inheritance is currently stronger than blankness.
- Compositional factoring exists off-ceiling in inherited features (held-out combos ~= seen), and it
  breaks at three factors and at the vision-to-language boundary. The boundary is mapped, not vibed.
- Structure wins that survive controls are architectural, not mystical: sparse gated heads halve
  forgetting and survive the projection control.
- Routing does not win for free: the trained router lost to a tuned single reader and to a
  compute-matched homogeneous bank on the real cache. Heterogeneity headroom must be created by the
  task and proven by the matrix, not assumed.
- Iterative latent compute equals unrolled depth at matched FLOPs at this scale; there is no free
  reasoning inside the frozen geometry.
- Learned codes so far are idiolects: they do not recur across seeds. A private language is not a
  language.
- The strongest binding constraint in the entire record was the TEST BED: additive synthetic content
  and one vacuous control nearly poisoned an era. Hence the difficulty-calibration gate and the
  non-vacuous control law in FORM_SUBSTRATE_DOCTRINE.md.
- The frozen substrate reveals; it does not form. The one sanctioned path to formation is the gated
  plastic branch (F7, F8, F16 through the process_c license pattern).

## 5. The architecture stack

The active architecture, each layer with the baseline it must beat before it is believed:

```text
Layer 0  Evidence Event Plane    immutable referents, events, branches, clocks, lifecycle, hashes
Layer 1  Form Intake            refuse missing referents, duplicate tags, missing controls
Layer 2  Referent Binder        beat shuffled-referent, raw-feature, single-form baselines
Layer 3  Form Feature Store     report bytes, load latency, cache hit rate, mmap viability
Layer 4  Canonical Substrate    beat concat, matched-capacity MLP, wider single-form, random map
Layer 5  Mode Ecology           beat best single mode, uniform ensemble, homogeneous copies, matched monolith
Layer 6  Cross-Form Memory      beat same-form kNN, random retrieval, recency, form-local memory
Layer 7  Operational Awareness  beat random routing, fixed routing, single-confidence, oracle-gap report
Layer 8  Action Closure         beat action-shuffle, reactive, non-action predictor, horizon baseline
Layer 9  Density Monitor        every result reports capability, cost, density
Layer 10 Plastic Rewrite Gate   opens only when frozen inherited forms plus larger shells fail
Layer 11 Execution Verifier     adaptive resources, exact resume, independent recomputation, rollback
```

Code homes for every layer are in FORM_SUBSTRATE_CODEMAP.md. Wave E0 now implements Layer 0 across
F23/F29/F39 mechanics with independent mutation verification. The P6 stream extends that plane with
disk-backed identity, exact resume, lifecycle deletion, replay controls, and long-stream endpoint
schemas at mechanics scope. P7 now implements Layer 8's rendered action, exact intervention,
eight-arm transition, horizon, calibration, planning, and cost mechanics, with a negative fixture
record to beat on external units. Layers 1 to 3 exist. Layer 5 exists as shell machinery with a
negative routing record to beat. P9 now implements Layer 7's causal telemetry, calibration, and
bounded relief mechanics, though broadcast and natural-workload validation remain. Layers 4 and 6
remain controlled experiment surfaces. Layer 9 has P4/P5/P9 instruments and P9 end-to-end workload
accounting, with energy correctly unmeasured. Layer 10 has fail-closed rewrite machinery
but still waits on valid natural evidence. Layer 11 now includes the adaptive governor and durable
atlas validator. The first owned trainable substrate completed five seeds locally; its objective
regime closed with a null, while its portable interfaces remain the successor platform seam.

## 6. The north star

Old: perceive -> remember -> predict -> surprise -> adapt -> consolidate -> abstract -> transfer.

New:

```text
receive any form
-> bind referents
-> preserve factors
-> align perspectives
-> remember across forms
-> act and predict consequences
-> know its own operational state
-> adapt the interface when frozen form fails
-> compress into dense capability
```

The research target is Level 3 operational awareness (OPERATIONAL_AWARENESS.md): a system that
detects missing forms, missing memory, uncertainty, task difficulty, and substrate failure well
enough to route, retrieve, act, or request rewrite. Not sentience. Not a person. A measurable
artificial substrate that knows enough about its own operation to manage it.

## 7. The perfect slate branch

The most dangerous and most beautiful branch. Dangerous because it can become cargo cult. Beautiful
because it asks whether the substrate can grow the structures inherited models already carry.

A perfect slate is a minimal trainable substrate initialized without pretrained perceptual
inheritance, trained on the same form curriculum as the inherited encoders, and accepted only if it
beats inherited frozen features plus larger shells at matched total compute. It must beat V-JEPA,
DINO, audio SSL and language hidden states where relevant, random-init same architecture, larger
shell on frozen features, and a matched-compute monolith. It must not win by more parameters, more
data, more compute, easier tasks, ceilinged synthetic factors, missing controls, language leakage,
or old-form forgetting.

If it fails: "the perfect slate is not live at this scale." Not "the dream is dead." A null means
inheritance is still stronger than blank plasticity under the tested conditions. A positive means
objective and curriculum matter enough to justify custom developmental pretraining. Either answer is
a result. F16 is this branch made runnable; it stays behind every gate in section 5's Layer 10.

## 8. The Substrate Is the Laboratory

Early AI mistook the model for the mind. It mistook text for thought. It mistook video for world.
It mistook fusion for alignment. It mistook benchmarks for development. It mistook weight updates
for learning. It mistook scale for plasticity.

The form substrate begins elsewhere:

```text
Before intelligence, there is form.
Before form matters, there is referent.
Before memory matters, it must bind referents.
Before plasticity matters, it must preserve what came before.
Before awareness matters, it must know what it is doing operationally.
Before the blank slate matters, it must beat inheritance.
```

Every poetic claim in this program carries an experimental translation, or it is deleted:

- Poetic: the substrate does not know vision; it receives vision as one form among others.
  Translation: vision-only transfer is not enough; cross-form transfer must beat single-form
  baselines and shuffled-referent controls (F1, F2).
- Poetic: the substrate dreams not to imitate sleep, but to protect referents.
  Translation: dream replay must improve cross-form retention at matched compute without corrupting
  held-out factor combinations (F11).
- Poetic: the substrate becomes aware of its own insufficiency.
  Translation: crisis detection must predict when frozen forms plus larger shells fail, and license
  rewrite only then (F20 feeding F8).
- Poetic: memory is the shape referents leave behind.
  Translation: storing through one form and retrieving through another must beat form-local nearest
  neighbor at matched slots (F5, F19).
- Poetic: the substrate grows.
  Translation: growth must beat a matched-final-capacity control, and a new form must arrive without
  remapping old memory (F7, F14).

This is the scientific-fiction layer: the fiction is allowed exactly as far as the falsifiers reach.

## 9. What would refute this paradigm

The form-substrate direction is refuted if:

1. forms cannot align beyond shuffled referents (F1),
2. held-out form transfer ties single-form baselines (F2),
3. cross-form memory ties form-local memory (F5, F19),
4. action-shuffle ties true action (F6, F15),
5. operational-awareness metrics tie fixed or random routing (OA suite, F17, F20),
6. performance-density gains disappear under matched compute (F13),
7. plastic rewrite ties frozen inherited features plus larger shells (F8),
8. the perfect slate ties inherited substrates (F16),
9. new forms cannot be added without remapping old memory (F14),
10. all positives collapse under seed stability or difficulty calibration.

If these happen, the right conclusion is not to mythologize the substrate. The right conclusion is:
"the current form-substrate implementation is not more than controlled multimodal infrastructure."
That would still be useful. It would not be a discovery, and this program will say which one it got.

## 10. Manifesto (one page)

We are done thinking in the shape of one encoder.

For a year this project poured discipline into a narrow pipe: one frozen video model, one pooled
latent, one tiny shell, and a null hypothesis for every hope. The discipline worked. It killed
twenty-odd beautiful mechanisms, caught its own vacuous control, mapped the exact boundary where
inherited vision stops factoring the world, and left two dozen negative results that are worth more
than most positives. The pipe also worked on us. Every ambition arrived pre-shaped: 64 frames, 1024
dimensions, video in, probe out. That shape is now the fossil record.

The new object is the form substrate: a medium that receives ANY observation family, binds every one
of them to shared referents, and is judged, coldly, on eight axes: alignment, transfer, memory,
consequence, plasticity, density, performance, falsifiability. Vision is one form. Language is one
form. Audio, symbols, telemetry, code, action, memory, counterfactuals: forms. None privileged. A
form without referents is a pile of vectors. A substrate that cannot fail is not science.

We are not claiming a mind. We are building operational awareness: the measured ability of a system
to know which forms it has, how confident it should be, what memory it can reach, which computation
to spend, and when its own representation is failing. Level 3 on our ladder. Every word of that is a
metric with a baseline and a null, gated by a rail that refuses grandiosity at render time.

We are not worshipping scale. Performance is science here: every result ships capability, cost, and
density, and an architecture is accepted only if it wins at matched cost or costs less at matched
capability. Density is the axis a small lab can win, and we intend to win it on Apple Silicon we can
touch: encode once, cache forever, route only when routing pays.

We are not deleting the past. V-JEPA remains a strong inherited arm. The negatives remain load
bearing. The controls remain sacred. The blank slate remains behind its gates, and if it cannot beat
inheritance at matched compute we will write that down too, in the same ledger, with the same
receipts.

We are changing paradigms without losing discipline. Increasing ambition while increasing controls.
Dreaming harder and measuring harder. The dream is now precise enough to fail. That is the whole
point. Substrate first. Referents always. Density or it did not happen.
