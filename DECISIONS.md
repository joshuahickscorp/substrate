# Decisions

Maintained architectural decisions:

- Preserve scientific mathematics and evidence; delete repeated lifecycle machinery.
- Use one experiment engine, evidence authority, controller, registry/configuration authority, and CLI.
- Keep producer and independent-verifier mathematics structurally separate.
- Treat nulls, negative findings, controls, units, SESOI, multiplicity, stop rules, and claim ceilings as
  first-class retained data.
- Count relocation, archival, generation, addition, deduplication, and elimination separately.
- Protect the live General Run as an immutable external boundary.
- Keep current documentation small and recover superseded narratives through the sealed historical index.

Historical decision prose is recoverable through collapse/MOP_HISTORICAL_DOCUMENT_INDEX.json.
