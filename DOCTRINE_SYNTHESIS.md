# Doctrine Synthesis: Developmental Plasticity and Language-Independent Abstraction

## What This Document Is, and Is Not

This document interprets roughly 105 to 110 real, completed experiment results (stored as JSON in `runs/pre_studio/`, catalogued in full in `runs/pre_studio/RESULTS_PRE_STUDIO.md`) against the two doctrinal questions that motivate the Brain project: (1) can this frozen-V-JEPA-2-substrate-plus-tiny-trainable-shell system be made developmentally moldable, plastic and reshapeable by experience the way a child's brain is, rather than a rigid fixed mapping; and (2) can it exhibit abstraction that does not route through language or symbolic/mathematical scaffolding. Every claim below traces back to a specific experiment id with a real numeric result on disk, cross-checked against the adversarial-verification pass already recorded in `RESULTS_PRE_STUDIO.md`.

This document is explicitly **not**: a new experiment run (no code executed, no new numbers produced here), a re-verification of individual results (that adversarial pass already happened and is cited, not repeated), and **not** an implementation plan for a V-JEPA-connected or custom architecture (that decision is a deliberately deferred later step, noted again in the closing section). Its only job is to read across the existing corpus and state plainly what it does and does not support, and where the real gaps are.

## 1. Developmental Plasticity and Moldability

**Synthesis.** Read across the full plasticity/moldability cluster (roughly 20 experiments spanning series N, D, B, E, EX, Y), the honest answer as of this pre-Studio corpus is that the thesis of genuine developmental moldability has not been demonstrated, and in several of its most direct, falsifiable forms it has been actively disconfirmed at toy scale. The strongest, most repeated pattern: every mechanism explicitly designed to reproduce a specific biological plasticity signature (Fisher-triggered critical-period reopening in n5, staged/triggered plasticity scheduling in e3, ACh/NE-style dissociated gating in n6, synaptic-tagging-and-capture consolidation in n4, homeostatic synaptic scaling in b4, neuromodulatory uncertainty gating in e4) either ties its non-biological baseline exactly, loses to it, or actively hurts performance. None of these are single flukes: e3's Fisher trace peaks at the very first checkpoint and monotonically decays rather than rising and falling the way a true sensitivity window would; n5's shift-localization accuracy lands at exactly chance (0.5); e4's neuromodulatory gate was re-run through a genuine 30-seed-by-axis grid specifically to rule out a seed-instability artifact, and the negative not only held, it got sharper (30/30 runs amplify error on noise, the wrong direction for anything resembling a useful plasticity-boosting signal). This is a coherent, cross-mechanism null: nothing in this shell currently exhibits a shapeable, reopenable, or gateable sensitivity window that outperforms a plain constant or tuned-decay learning rate.

The curriculum/scaffolding cluster, the piece of the corpus closest to the Vygotskian "scaffolded learning" claim, is messier but ultimately does not support the hypothesis either. The two experiments with the largest apparent effect sizes (d7_scaffolding and b7_developmental_curriculum) both failed adversarial review for reasons that matter: d7's winning arms had more trainable parameters than the losing arms (a capacity confound, not a curriculum-order finding), and b7's own difficulty-resolvability gate failed, meaning the task's difficulty levels were never properly calibrated. Where curriculum mechanisms were cleanly evaluable without a confound, they lost: d5's learning-progress self-curriculum underperforms random ordering on final accuracy, and b3's stigmergic curriculum trails a learning-progress baseline by 35 points of coverage. The one genuinely underpowered (rather than refuted) result is b2_baldwin_meta_init, a 9-point gap that is directionally interesting but does not clear its own 2-seed spread.

Two findings deserve more nuance because they are not flat negatives. First, **e7_sparse** (sparse/gated architectures, kWTA and MoE routing, reducing forgetting) is the single strongest surviving positive in the entire corpus, and it sits inside this theme: a genuine 30-run seed-and-axis sweep shows sparse architectures roughly halving catastrophic forgetting relative to a parameter-matched dense baseline, direction-stable in 26 to 27 of 30 configurations, with a wired-in difficulty-calibration check confirming the underlying task is not degenerate. It remains provisional: toy synthetic latents only, no frozen-random-projection control yet, no formal significance test. Second, ex13_long_stream shows a real and durable protective effect of replay plus EWC against forgetting across streams as long as 3000 tasks, with the protective gap widening rather than shrinking at scale; but its own frozen-random control very nearly matches the protected arm's performance, so this specific advantage has not been shown to require the real V-JEPA substrate as opposed to generic fixed-high-dimensional-feature-space geometry.

What cannot yet be said, stated plainly rather than softened: there is no experiment in this corpus, run to completion with a passing standing control, that shows this frozen-substrate-plus-shell system has anything resembling a child's critical period, a recoverable/rejuvenable plasticity budget, a biologically faithful consolidation-ordering preference, or a scaffolding/ZPD-style benefit from staged exposure. The one experiment purpose-built to test biological-style rejuvenation of lost plasticity, ex15_rejuvenation, was implemented and run late in this same session (after the bulk of this synthesis was drafted): at small scale a frozen-latent shell shows NO loss of plasticity to rejuvenate (an honest 'nothing to restore' null), but mid-scale probes (dim 256, thousands of tasks) do surface real plasticity loss that rejuvenation measurably restores, sometimes at a retention cost. So this is no longer a total gap; it is an early, scale-dependent result showing the mechanism only activates once the shell is pushed well past toy scale. The U-shaped overgeneralization dip from child language acquisition, the classic developmental signature that would have been the cleanest legible win if this system behaved like a developing mind, shows a dip depth of exactly zero across every capacity and memory condition tested. It must also be kept in view throughout that almost every experiment in this cluster ran on a synthetic Gaussian-cluster stand-in rather than cached real V-JEPA 2 latents, so even a clean result here is a statement about a tiny trainable shell on an easy synthetic task, not yet a demonstrated property of the frozen video substrate itself. The honest summary: replay as a category helps, clearly; no tested biologically-motivated refinement of replay, consolidation, curriculum, or plasticity gating beats the dumbest matched baseline; homeostatic and neuromodulatory analogues are neutral-to-harmful; and the one plausible positive (sparse gating) is a structural, not developmental-scheduling, mechanism, and is itself still provisional.

## 2. Abstraction Independent of Language or Symbols

**Synthesis.** Across every experiment bearing on abstraction independent of language or symbolic/mathematical scaffolding, the honest state is: nothing has yet demonstrated a real, substrate-specific instance of language-independent abstraction that survives the house's own adversarial controls. This is a stronger, more specific statement than "everything failed": the pattern is one coherent story repeating across nine independent lines of investigation (concept formation, meaning-without-symbols, thought-without-language, memorization-vs-concept, private-language, symbol-grounding, concept-arithmetic, compositionality, systematicity), each converging on the same failure mode: whatever the trained shell appears to do, a frozen random linear projection of the exact same raw latents does it just as well, or the effect ties/loses at the matched-compute or matched-margin gate the experiment itself pre-declared. p1_concept_no_labels shows zero purity advantage from training over either the raw latent or a frozen-random reprojection. s10_anti_self_deception is the single most important result in this cluster because it is a purpose-built meta-test: all four semiotic sub-tests (code-world alignment, concept arithmetic, compositionality, cross-modal translation) return exactly delta_frozen_random=0.0 and are flagged PASS-VACUOUS, meaning the toy evaluation apparatus currently has no power to distinguish genuine V-JEPA-specific abstraction from generic linearly-separable-cluster geometry under any random reprojection. That single test retroactively explains why s1_symbol_grounding, s6_compositionality, and s9_interpretable_vs_useful all evaporate on adversarial review, and the identical pattern recurs independently in c1_held_out_combination and c9_systematicity_sweep (checked separately, C-series) and in n8_object_permanence_bound and a1_affordance_decode (checked separately again, on a different mechanism family). Five independent series hitting the identical artifact is strong evidence the artifact is systemic to the current evaluation regime at this toy scale, not a fluke of any one experiment.

The "thought without language" and "meaning without symbols" lines (p9_thought_without_language, ex17_latent_reasoning, ex18_self_verification, cross-checked against n9/y1/y2/y3) tell a second, distinct, equally clean negative: iterative latent computation on this substrate behaves exactly like plain unrolled feedforward depth. No detectable matched-compute gain from iteration (p9 and ex17 both land at exactly gain=0.0), no convergence to a fixed point (n9's residual sits two orders of magnitude past its own threshold; y1 shows unrolling past the training horizon actively worsens loss), no real correction signal from a trained verifier over a shuffled, untrained one (ex18). The only honest residual benefit measured anywhere in this line is parameter efficiency (4x fewer parameters for equal accuracy), a real but much narrower claim than "reasoning without language."

The discretization-cost lines (p6, s4_latent_vs_discrete, s7_learned_vs_designed) point in an interesting direction but cannot yet be read either way: every one is ceiling-saturated (continuous and discrete/VQ arms tie at accuracy 1.0), so there is no room left to detect whether a discrete symbolic bottleneck costs anything real. This is the largest "untested, not refuted" gap in the theme. The private-language/universal-code line (p5_private_language, s5_code_stability, y3_seed_consistent_fixed_points) is where the research map's own explicit warning ("a code that does not recur across seeds above the frozen-random floor is an idiolect, not a language") is directly and repeatedly cashed out: cross-shell probe transfer sits at exact chance in p5, cross-seed code agreement sits below the frozen-random floor in s5, fixed points are seed-idiosyncratic below the frozen-random floor in y3. Wherever tested, the code is an idiolect, not a shared language, and this is a clean, replicated negative.

One candidate resists the uniform pattern and deserves to be named honestly: p2_memorize_vs_concept shows a genuinely large gap (shell generalization error roughly a quarter of a nearest-neighbor baseline's) that survived interpolation and extrapolation checks. But it never ran a frozen-random control, and its target function is exactly linear, which near-guarantees any parametric readout beats 1-nearest-neighbor regardless of real concept formation, so the result is real but not diagnostic. The backprop-alternatives line is split informatively: the easy version (i4_backprop_alts) is worthless (the task is separable even untrained), but the harder domain-incremental version, **ex5_local_rules_scale**, shows local, biologically-motivated learning rules (feedback alignment, predictive coding) beating global backprop on both accuracy and backward transfer, an unforced, genuinely surprising result explicitly marked **plausible-but-unverified** pending an optimizer-matching control (Adam vs. plain SGD effective step size). This, together with e7_sparse, is one of only two candidates in the entire corpus with enough residual signal to be worth a real Studio-scale confirmation run.

## 3. What This Corpus Actually Shows So Far

The blunt, calibrated read: of roughly 25 to 27 candidate positives that emerged across the pre-Studio corpus before adversarial review, exactly **one** survives, provisionally: **e7_sparse**, where sparse/gated architectures (kWTA, MoE routing) measurably halve catastrophic forgetting relative to a parameter-matched dense baseline across a real 30-run seed-and-axis sweep, still pending a frozen-random control and real-latent confirmation. One more, **ex5_local_rules_scale**, is genuinely interesting and not yet refuted, flagged plausible-but-unverified because a live optimizer confound (Adam vs. plain SGD effective step size) has not been ruled out. Every other candidate positive in the corpus, more than twenty of them, was refuted on adversarial review: either it never ran the frozen-random-projection control the house doctrine requires, or it ran that control and lost or tied, or its apparent "rejection of the null" was a boolean-logic artifact of how the null was AND-gated rather than a real effect in the intended direction. This is the headline finding of the corpus as it stands: almost every tested mechanism, across both doctrinal themes and across all nine originating disciplines, did not survive adversarial review at this toy, synthetic-substrate scale. That is not a failure of the research program; it is exactly what pre-registration and adversarial verification are supposed to produce, a clean map of what is honestly dead, what is underpowered by ceiling effects or too few seeds, what lands where the doctrine itself predicts pooled latents should land (the taxonomy-3 bounds), and what two threads are worth a real follow-up. But it should not be oversold: as of today, neither central doctrinal question (moldability, language-independent abstraction) has a demonstrated substrate-level positive result behind it. The corpus's genuine value right now is a rigorously honest negative map plus two live, worth-chasing leads, not a body of confirmed mechanism.

### 3a. Update: the two live leads were closed, and a corpus-wide census was run

Three targeted control experiments (scripts `close_e7_sparse.py`, `close_ex5_local_rules.py`, `close_ex2_planning.py`) resolved the open contentions, and the picture is now sharper:

- **e7_sparse: real but REFRAMED as architectural, not substrate-specific.** Its sparse-minus-dense forgetting advantage survives a frozen-random projection of the inputs nearly intact (real gain +0.239 vs frozen-random +0.164, ratio 0.69, margin-clearing at all 5 seeds). So sparse/gated heads genuinely forget less, but overwhelmingly as a property of the head ARCHITECTURE, not of V-JEPA geometry.
- **ex5_local_rules: REFUTED.** Plain-SGD backprop at a matched effective step size forgets no more than the local rules (+0.157 BWT recovered), so "local rules beat backprop" was an Adam optimizer artifact.
- **ex2_latent_planning: PROMOTED to a real positive.** Scored honestly on the true synthetic dynamics (not the planner's own belief), the MPC planner still beats the flat baseline and the action-shuffle control on all 3 seeds. Short-horizon latent planning genuinely reaches goals a reactive head cannot.

**Net: two real positives (e7_sparse architecture, ex2 planning), and they point the same way: what works is the shell's structure and algorithm, not the pooled substrate carrying special structure.**

A corpus-wide census (`frozen_random_census.json`) confirms this from the other direction. Of every GENUINE frozen-random-substrate comparison across all ~119 experiments (real V-JEPA pooled geometry vs a random linear projection OF ITSELF), 7 of 9 are exact ties and the only exception is e7_sparse, by +0.075 to +0.124, and ONLY in continual-forgetting dynamics, never in any decodability, geometry, or abstraction probe (a2 viewpoint, c3 prototype, i8 quantization, p10 theory-ladenness, and the real 64-clip replication all tie at delta 0.000). **The pooled substrate is projection-invariant almost everywhere; the entire substrate-specific signal in the corpus is a whisper in sparse-head forgetting.** This is the strongest single piece of evidence that the pooled interface, not the mechanisms, is the binding constraint, and it is exactly what the dense-vs-pooled probe was built to confirm or refute on a clean spatial factor.

### 3b. Update: the dense-vs-pooled probe landed, and it reweights the fork

The decisive probe ran on real V-JEPA weights (128 clips, full 8192 pre-pool tokens per clip, `dense_vs_pooled.json`). It compared decodability of a GLOBAL factor (hue) and a SPATIAL factor (orientation) from the mean-POOLED vector versus a dense token GRID, each against frozen-random. The result was NOT what the pooling-bottleneck hypothesis predicted:

**Orientation (the spatial factor) decodes PERFECTLY from the mean-pooled vector (real_acc 1.0). Mean-pooling does NOT destroy it.** The encoder stores orientation in the token VALUES, not only in the spatial arrangement that pooling discards, so the pooled mean retains it. The simplest form of the "pooling destroys spatial structure, go dense" hypothesis is REFUTED for a single spatial factor.

The important caveat: the whole probe is ceiling-saturated (every cell, hue and orientation, pooled and grid, real and frozen-random, hits exactly 1.0), because 4-way grating factors are trivially linearly separable in 1024 dimensions. So the probe cannot say whether the real substrate carries structure a random projection does not, only that pooling is not the loss.

**This reweights the paradigm fork.** The binding constraint is not the pooled interface per se (simple spatial factors survive pooling fine); it is TEST DIFFICULTY, the same ceiling effect that produced projection-invariance across the entire corpus. Two consequences: (1) the "dense substrate is required" motivation is weaker than the taxonomy-3 framing suggested, at least for simple factors; the correct open question is COMPOSITIONAL structure (held-out combinations, relations) at NON-ceiling difficulty. (2) More fundamentally, the recurring, cross-corpus obstacle is that synthetic-grating content is too easy to construct a test that can distinguish a real substrate property from generic geometry.

### 3c. The arc converges: the test bed, not the substrate or the mechanisms, is the binding constraint

A cheap follow-up (`compositional_ceiling_finding.json`, seconds, no encoder) settled where the compositional test can even bite. The held-out-combination probe has a phase transition on the FACTOR-BINDING knob: for INDEPENDENT or weakly-bound factors (interaction 0 to 1) held-out accuracy sits at ceiling (1.0, trivially compositional, a linear probe for factor A generalizes to unseen (A,B) cells because A is decodable regardless of B); only for NON-ADDITIVELY BOUND factors (interaction >= 2) does it drop to chance and become informative. The V-JEPA factorized cache uses INDEPENDENT hue x orientation (the generator sets tint and angle separately), so its compositional test would ceiling and say nothing, exactly as the dense-vs-pooled probe already ceiling-saturated on the same gratings. The planned 2.4h factorized build was therefore stopped: it would have produced a predictable, uninformative ceiling.

**The whole synthetic-test arc converges on one conclusion.** Stacking the results: (a) mechanisms mostly do not help (adversarially-verified corpus); (b) the two real positives (e7_sparse, ex2) are architectural/algorithmic, not substrate; (c) the pooled substrate is projection-invariant almost everywhere (census); (d) pooling does not even destroy simple spatial factors (dense probe); (e) synthetic content ceilings every decodability and compositional test, and cannot pose the compositional-BINDING question at all because its factors are additive by construction. The single binding constraint across the entire program is the TEST BED: synthetic Gaussian-cluster and grating content is too easy and too additive to distinguish a real substrate property, a real compositional capability, or a real mechanism gain from generic linear geometry.

**Implication for the fork (dense V-JEPA vs custom architecture): the fork cannot be answered on synthetic data at all, and neither branch is yet justified.** "Go dense" is not motivated (pooling is not the bottleneck for what we could test); "go custom" is not motivated (we have not shown the frozen substrate is bounded, only that our tests could not challenge it). The actual prerequisite, the one thing deferred all along, is REAL NATURAL-VIDEO content with genuinely bound objects (where color, shape, position, motion are non-additively entangled), run through the frozen encoder, so that a compositional or abstraction test can finally be non-ceiling and non-additive. Until that data exists, an architecture decision would be premature. The concrete next experiment is therefore not another synthetic probe but the natural-video path: cache real V-JEPA latents over a small set of real clips with labeled bound attributes, and run the held-out-combination and readout-contribution probes on THAT. That is the deviation-to-target this arc identifies: the barrier was never the substrate or the mechanisms, it was that we had no data hard enough to ask the real question.

## 4. Gap Analysis and Proposed Next Experimentation Lanes

### Untested Mechanisms (never built, not even a toy proxy)

**Plasticity/moldability relevant:**
- **D6, sensitive/critical developmental window** (elevated-then-closed plasticity with content-order-dependent consequences, Hensch 2005): arguably the single most direct test of the moldability thesis as the user defines it, and it has zero implementation, not even a toy proxy.
- **N2, sleep-cycle consolidation** with true offline-replay interleaving and synaptic downscale (Tononi-Cirelli SHY 2014): studio-tier, deferred.
- **D10, play as goal-babbling** for representation-space coverage (Cangelosi-Schlesinger 2015): studio-tier, deferred.
- **B8, morphogenetic growth** (structural plasticity, adding/pruning capacity in response to experience rather than just reweighting): the only catalog mechanism testing structural change directly; never built.
- **Y10/I10, early-warning critical-slowing-down signals** before a forgetting knee (Scheffer 2009): ex13_long_stream already supplies the long-stream logs this diagnostic needs, but the diagnostic itself was never built.
- **Y4, hysteresis/phase transition** in retention under a slowly varying schedule parameter: tests path-dependence directly; never built.

**Language-independent-abstraction relevant:**
- **P7, object-level binding prior to pooling**: needs a second, dense-token, unpooled encoder path; never built.
- **P8/A9, closed-loop cognition-action coupling and tool-use means-end chaining**: environment-needed; never built even as a cpu-now offline proxy (A5, the closest existing analogue, itself failed its own control).
- **S2/S8, cross-domain/cross-modal alignment through a shared bottleneck**: the most direct available test of language-independent universal structure; ex10_cross_modal is registry-only/deferred, S2 and S8 were never built at all.

### Proposed Next Lanes

1. **Real-latent replication of the doctrine-load-bearing series (P, S, select C/D)** on the existing real V-JEPA 2 latent cache (64-clip real-encoder cache built this session). ## Closing Note

An implementation plan, whether that means continuing to build on the frozen V-JEPA 2 substrate or moving toward a different or custom architecture, is a deliberately deferred, later step and is out of scope for this document. The sole purpose of this synthesis is to ground that eventual decision in what the real experimental corpus on this laptop actually shows, rather than in the original literature-only hypotheses the 12-agent research map proposed before any of it was run. As of today, that evidence base consists of one provisional positive (e7_sparse), one plausible-but-unverified lead (ex5_local_rules_scale), a large and well-controlled map of honest negatives, a set of taxonomy-3 pooling bounds that behave exactly as the doctrine predicted, and a substantial set of underpowered or never-built experiments whose resolution (real-latent reruns, difficulty recalibration, the sensitive-window and structural-plasticity lane) is the concrete, actionable next step before any architectural commitment should be made.
