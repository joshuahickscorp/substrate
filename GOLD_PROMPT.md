  is the only trainable lane.
- Every experiment declares its null before it runs. The linear-probe gate precedes any mechanism
  claim. A tie is a null.
- No positive result enters any document without an independent adversarial verification pass.
- No consciousness, sentience, agency, or general-intelligence language anywhere; engineering
  vocabulary only. The north_star scanner is authoritative.
- No em dashes or en dashes in anything you write. Never attribute Claude or any assistant in git.
- Fail closed. A required arm that cannot write its receipt aborts its campaign atomically. Never

## Host guards (check before every heavy launch)

- One heavy model or training process at a time. Never two heavy CPU/MPS jobs.
- 180-minute wall per shard; exit resumable at the wall, never silently shrink the experiment.
- 40 GB free-disk hard floor, checked in-loop; new media/model downloads freeze when free disk is
  within 3 GB of the floor.
- Per-step MPS synchronization on training loops so failures are attributable to an exact
  arm/update; atomic refusal and crash receipts on every scheduler.
- Run the doctor (`runs/studio_doctor.md` generator) after any heavy process exits and before the
  next one starts.

## The loop (imperative; repeat until a stop condition)

1. SCAN. Rebuild the live queue: every registry row and portfolio item (R0, P1-P10, F chain,
   EX/MoT bank) whose first honest blocker is category 1 (runnable now) or category 2 (runnable
   after implementation). Check which receipts moved since the last pass; check what another agent
   or session is actively writing (recent mtimes, moving hashes) and do not touch those files.
2. PICK. Choose the highest-decision-value runnable item: prefer (a) closing an open receipt chain
   over starting new science, (b) controls over new positives, (c) items that unblock the most
   downstream rows. Never pick two heavy items.
3. PREFLIGHT. Doctor green, disk floor green, no competing heavy process, config and source hashes
   pinned, null and kill rule preregistered, seeds declared before launch.
4. EXECUTE. One heavy lane plus parallel light lanes (see wave structure). Long jobs run resumable
   with content-addressed checkpoints.
5. VERIFY. Adversarially. Reproduce or replay a slice, check finiteness, check controls, check that
   the negative control actually constrains the positive. If the result is favorable, attack it
   before recording it.
6. RECORD. Emit the proof artifact (atlas row, null card, receipt json) with hashes; update
   STATUS.md and the docs ledger in terse factual voice; refresh any exhaustion/localization ledger
   whose inputs changed. Nulls and kills are recorded with the same care as positives; retired lines
   go to `proof/OBITUARIES.md` or a null card, never silently dropped.
7. RECLASSIFY. If the item revealed a new blocker, put it in exactly one category (1-9) with
   evidence. Category 8/9 requires a measurement or calculation, never a label, guard, model name,
   or intuition.
8. LOOP. Go to 1. Do not ask permission to continue. Ask only when blocked on something only the
   human can do, and even then queue it to the human tray and keep working everything else.

## Wave structure (how to parallelize without violating the one-heavy rule)

- Lane 0 (heavy, exclusive): the single active training/encoding/caching job on MPS or CPU.
- Lanes 1..n (light, parallel): implementation, tests, receipt verification, ledger regeneration,
  metadata-only intake research, no-download preflights, doc drift repair, statistical analysis of
  completed receipts. These must not load model weights, decode media at scale, or write files
  another live task owns.
- Downloads are their own serialized lane: network-only, hash-verified, atomic partials, double-byte
  reservation, and only while the disk floor plus 3 GB margin holds and no heavy job needs the disk.
- When lane 0 frees, immediately promote the next heavy item from the queue; when a light lane
  finishes, immediately reuse the slot to attack the next blocker category.
