# PARADIGM MIGRATION

The consolidation and wipe order: what survives from the V-JEPA, biology-lever, and
Mixture-of-Perspectives eras into the Form Substrate Program, in what role, and what stops being the
organizing frame. The wipe is conceptual, not reckless: no evidence, receipt, result, or code is
deleted. Provenance is preserved. The ACTIVE language changes.

House style: no em or en dashes. Companions: FORM_SUBSTRATE_PROGRAM.md (the new center),
MIGRATION_PHASES.md (execution order), LEGACY_INDEX.md (per-document archive index),
FORM_SUBSTRATE_EXPERIMENTS.md (per-experiment migration map), FORM_SUBSTRATE_CODEMAP.md (per-module).

---

## 1. The wipe (what is no longer the center)

Removed from active doctrine as ORGANIZING FRAMES (they remain as evidence and instruments):

1. V-JEPA as the mental shape. It is one inherited perceptual arm. Video is one referent type.
   Pooled latents are one feature geometry.
2. "Frozen substrate plus tiny shell" as the master frame. It survives as the DEFAULT REGIME and as
   the control against which any trainable substrate must win, not as the ontology.
3. Mixture of Perspectives as the final paradigm. The mixture becomes Layer 5 (mode ecology) of a
   larger stack, and its H1-H5 hypotheses remain live within that layer, carrying their negative
   router record with them.
4. Biological-lever testing as the top-level program. The biology levers are a closed, mostly
   negative evidence corpus. Individual instruments (replay, consolidation, sparsity) survive as
   machinery where they won on architecture grounds.
5. "Multimodal fusion" as the substrate story. Fusion without referents is fake alignment. The A6
   record (alignment carried by nuisance geometry, not semantics) is the standing warning.
6. The MoT name. The program is MoP historically and Form Substrate Program actively. GO.md's stale
   docs/mixture_of_thinking/ path is a known defect to fix in Phase 0.

## 3. Reconciliation with the standing brakes

The form paradigm raises ambition; it does not relax a single gate:

- The readout-vs-formation wall stands: a frozen substrate reveals structure, it does not form it.
  F7/F8/F16 are the only doors, and they inherit doc 15's triggers, the process_c license gate, and
  the PR9 kill-switch semantics unchanged.
- The DR1 acceptance gates (caption recoverability, A6 residual guard, perspective-matrix receipt,
  adversarial verifier) become the intake standard for ALL real paired forms, because DR1 is the
  first real multi-form referent corpus this program will own.
- The router null (EX-ROUTER-DENSITY) travels with Layer 5: any mode-ecology positive must beat the
  same matched-compute homogeneous-bank control that killed the first router claim.
- The A6 lesson travels with Layer 2: alignment survivors must be residualized against nuisance
  factors before any cross-form claim uses the word semantic.

## 4. Naming policy (two stages, binding)

New work imports the form interface; old imports stay valid.

tests green, (b) the registry and EXPERIMENTS.md render stable, (c) grep shows no orphan references.

MIGRATION_PHASES.md Phase 6 and none is chosen today.
