# Experiments

The executable experiment inventory is mop.experiments.REGISTRY; this document does not duplicate its
counts or status fields.

An experiment must declare its question, null, source identity, split, independent unit, treatment and
controls, metric direction, SESOI, multiplicity policy, budget, stop rule, claim ceiling, and independent
verifier. Ordinary execution belongs to mop.science. Unique mathematics stays in small providers.

A result may remain null or negative. Removing a framework must not remove its hypothesis, controls,
evidence class, or recovery path. Graded production and independent recomputation remain structurally
separate.

STARSS23 retains its scientific rationale in docs/ESCS_DEEP_RESEARCH.md; current code and sealed
preregistrations are the operational authority.
