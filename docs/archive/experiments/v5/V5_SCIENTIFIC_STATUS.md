## Interpretation boundary

The admitted environments are deterministic seeded desktop and 3D sandboxes
plus a frozen synthetic multimodal developmental generator. The 13 modules are
hand-specified deterministic Python fixtures with bounded synthetic competence
and no training data. They are not downloaded pretrained checkpoints and do not
establish general-world model capability.

The terminal result supports the frozen engineering/scientific classification
ladder and licenses external review of the package. It does not establish
consciousness, phenomenal experience, sentience, feeling, suffering, desire,
personhood, life, or moral status. “Model organism” is an engineering metaphor
only, and no result licenses uncontrolled external action.
