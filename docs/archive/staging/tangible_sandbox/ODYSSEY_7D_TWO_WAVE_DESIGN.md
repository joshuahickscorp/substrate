# Substrate Odyssey — 7D Two-Wave Edition

**Program:** substrate-odyssey-7d-two-wave-v1
**Status:** draft authority; inactive
**Activation:** false

This is a scientific transition plan, not a launch receipt. It preserves all
eight paired frontier histories and the full seven-day developmental sequence
for each history. It does not shorten a history, reduce the frontier set, or
turn the two execution waves into independent scientific units.

The transition is admissible only after the bounded local quantized
preservation attempt is retained as evidence and no candidate honestly admits
the frozen width-eight contract. Its required transition reason is:

> The local host can maintain four paired frontier cells at the required scientific fidelity, but does not admit eight simultaneous paired cells under the frozen width-eight inference contract.

## Campaign shape

The campaign lasts 336 hours (14 days) as two sequential 168-hour waves. A
paired frontier block consists of one candidate history and its matched control
history. The eight paired frontier history blocks are the independent units;
the waves are operational epochs used to fit the local host and must not be
treated as independent experiments or naively compared.

| Wave | Paired frontier blocks | Reason for the allocation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A — integrated continuity and persistent work; B — formal mathematics; F — sound, speech, and auditory grounding; G — vision, motion, spatial/3D, body, and active perception | Mixes formal and sensory work, lighter and heavier work, and media/non-media load. |
| 2 | C — formal logic; D — code and software systems; E — philosophy, epistemology, and functional self-model; H — science, causality, and multimodal inference | Retains the same mixed workload profile without concentrating expected media, storage, or model demand in one wave. |

This assignment is preregistered and must be frozen before any hidden seed is
materialized. All eight frontier manifests, task commitments, curriculum,
weights, and evaluator commitments are also frozen before Wave 1 begins.

## The non-compressible seven-day arc

Every frontier receives the complete 168-hour history below. A wave is not a
3.5-day treatment period.

| Day | Required sequence |
|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline and project-state formation |
| 2 | Guided practice / matched exposure |
| 3 | Near transfer and revision |
| 4 | Interference, interruption, and recovery |
| 5 | Far and cross-modal transfer |
| 6 | Long-horizon integrated project work |
| 7 | No-new-instruction cold retention and blind examination |

Day 7 permits no new instruction. Candidate and control remain matched on
schedule, observations, tools, compute, wall time, token/instruction budget,
stimulus family, and scoring rubric; only the sealed developmental intervention
may differ.

## Frozen cross-wave controls

One model, runtime, quantization, candidate, control, tool panel, chat
template, prompt policy, context policy, reasoning policy, structured-output
policy, and scoring policy are selected and frozen for both waves. No model
update, quantization change, runtime upgrade, prompt change, or tool revision
is allowed between them.

Wave 1 scores remain hidden from the execution system and custodians who could
change Wave 2 until both waves are sealed. Wave 1 results may not alter Wave 2
tasks, difficulty, composition, curriculum, weights, or evaluation. Account
for calendar order, host aging, software drift, model-service drift,
environmental differences, and operator interventions before any cross-wave
aggregation.

The host, runtime, and model identities are recorded at least every 12 hours.
A small public anchor canary is repeated before Wave 1, between the waves, and
after Wave 2. The anchor contains no hidden Odyssey content. If anchor behavior
or the pinned identities exceed the frozen drift tolerance, cross-wave
aggregation is invalidated.

At the midpoint, the operator must first independently verify and seal the
Wave 1 trace. Only ephemeral execution state may be reset. Immutable
source/model/tool identities remain. A fresh resource preflight accounts for
the sealed Wave 1 evidence, then the already committed Wave 2 hidden task
commitments may be materialized and Wave 2 may begin without scientific edits.

## Local resource admission

The preserved width-eight G06 is historical refusal evidence under this
transition; it is not silently reinterpreted as a pass. The admission gate for
this program is **G06-TW**, calibrated at widths 1, 2, and 4 under a frozen
scale factor. Width four is required.

G06-TW admits only if four paired frontier cells are actually running and all
candidate/control 64-token arm outputs are valid JSON; there are zero new
pageouts, no sustained swap growth, slowdown no greater than 1.35×, a sustained
soak consistent with scaled calibration, no missed cell work/event/checkpoint,
no cross-lane context leakage, and candidate/control parity. Client-side
queueing that defeats the frozen simultaneity definition is a refusal. The
scale factor cannot be tuned according to which value passes.

The hard resident ceiling remains 85 GiB. The broker thresholds remain 75 GiB
(deny new work), 80 GiB (P2 checkpoint), 82 GiB (P1 pause), and 85 GiB (safe
hold). The active wave has a 120 GiB private-write cap unless the measured
rehearsal justifies a lower frozen cap; the dynamic filesystem guard and
30-minute Telegram reporting remain required. Unused width-four memory must
not automatically be spent on a larger model: select the strongest locally
available model that passes width four with comfortable headroom and seven-day
stability.

The storage rehearsal projects separately Wave 1 growth, Wave 2 growth,
retained sealed Wave 1 evidence, Wave 2 active writes, terminal clean-clone
and publication needs, and a protected floor. After Wave 1 verification its
evidence may be compressed and made read-only, but it must not be deleted
before Wave 2 finishes. Wave 2 admission includes that retained evidence.

## Custody and blindness

All eight frontier custody identities are retained. Each wave uses separate
candidate/control roots, UIDs, model contexts, and random streams. Evaluator
mounts are absent from execution roots; denied-access probes are real rather
than asserted; traces lock before any answer reveal. The Day-7 examination for
each frontier remains blind.

No hidden seed, answer material, or Odyssey evaluator content is materialized
by this draft. The preservation tournament uses only public construction
canaries and public synthetic arm-shaped JSON work.

## Authority and terminal classifications

The transition invalidates the former one-wave gate set. New G01–G15 evidence
must bind the new source commit, with G06 replaced by G06-TW. This design and
its companion draft authorities are not evidence that any gate has passed.

- **Outcome A:** all eight blocks and both seven-day waves are valid; candidate
  beats the matched control under the frozen threshold; Day-7 cold retention is
  positive; no critical drift or leakage occurs; zero mutation survivors; and a
  clean clone passes.
- **Outcome B:** both waves are valid but the advantage is below threshold; the
  exact null is published.
- **Outcome C:** the preservation test and width-four admission fail, or
  cross-wave drift, storage, memory, custody, isolation, or a required frontier
  invalidates the campaign.

Neither a shortened campaign nor a reduced-frontier campaign may be labeled a
full Two-Wave Odyssey.
