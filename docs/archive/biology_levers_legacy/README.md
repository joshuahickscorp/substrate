# Archive: biology-levers legacy

Pointer index only. The biology-lever program (testing neuroscience-inspired mechanisms as levers
over frozen latents) is closed as a top-level ontology; its experiment banks stay runnable in code
and preregistered in the registry, and its verdicts stand as the negative map.

The record this archive points at (verdicts in registry rows, null cards, and DOCTRINE_SYNTHESIS.md):

- neuromodulation gating: refuted (error amplified on noise, 30 of 30 runs)
- staged plasticity, critical periods, fisher reopening, ACh/NE dissociation: refuted
- homeostatic scaling and LR loops: refuted or short of margin
- biologically ordered replay: no ordering beats uniform rehearsal cleanly
- U-shaped overgeneralization: dip exactly zero (clean non-ceiling null)
- local learning rules at scale: optimizer artifact (matched-SGD closes the gap)
- rejuvenation: nothing to repair at this scale
- the survivors are architectural, not biochemical: sparse gated heads (e7 lineage) and
  replay-as-category

Why kept: these negatives are priors that every F-series design must not re-lose to, and the
instruments (replay buffers, consolidation, plasticity controllers) remain load-bearing machinery
inside the shell.
