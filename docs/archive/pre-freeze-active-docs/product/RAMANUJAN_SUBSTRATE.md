# Ramanujan mathematical substrate

A mathematical Substrate would persist conjectures, failed proof paths, lemma
libraries, numerical experiments, verifier results, strategy competence, and
an active research agenda while using replaceable mathematical organs.

The future body could combine approved mathematical literature, Lean or other
formal verifiers, symbolic algebra, numerical experiments, and critic workers.
Every proposed result would remain a conjecture until independently verified;
the entity's history would preserve both success and informative failure.

No Ramanujan-derived model, theorem-discovery system, or long-running research
agent is implemented or claimed by this product foundation.
