# ESCS X1 counterfactual dispatch preregistration audit

**Status:** runnable scaffold, preregistered, intentionally unexecuted  
**Evidence ceiling:** generated structured-observation mechanics only  
**Candidate activation:** disabled  
**Scientific promotion:** blocked regardless of outcome

X1 is Experiment 1 from `docs/mixture_of_perspectives/18_event_sourced_coalition_substrate.md`.
It asks whether a temporary coalition can be selected from a future-blind structured hypothesis header
using predicted causal marginal value of computation. It does not test raw event formation; X0 remains
mandatory for any integrated event-driven efficiency claim.

No official X1 producer receipt or verification artifact exists at scaffold time. Unit tests use explicit
exploratory EDCM fixtures and tiny generated rows. Those fixtures are mechanics tests, not evidence.

## Entry gate and route separation

Official execution requires two immutable EDCM-1 inputs:

1. a terminal, self-hashed `mop-edcm1-receipt/v3` producer receipt with `all_ok=true`, no problems, and a
   complete complementarity gate; and
2. a self-hashed `mop-edcm1-verification-artifact/v1` whose full deterministic verifier is terminal and whose
   byte receipt binds the exact producer file supplied to X1.

Missing, partial, mismatched, diagnostic-only, or unhashed EDCM evidence blocks execution. A valid EDCM
complementarity failure creates an X1 `invalid_bed` stop with no dispatch rows; it is not a learned-routing
null. X1's own generated complementarity gate also precedes held-out inference. It requires off-ceiling
single actors, oracle headroom, unique niches for all four EDCM-aligned core actors, and a cold-start pair
whose members have zero individual value but positive joint value.

Terminal routes are disjoint:

- `positive`: every registered held-out criterion passes;
- `null`: the bed is valid but at least one scientific criterion does not pass;
- `invalid_bed`: the verified EDCM or X1 complementarity bed cannot test routing;
- `failed`: a schema, exact-rate, cap, accounting, leakage, regeneration, or verifier invariant fails; and
- `blocked`: prerequisite authority is missing or nonterminal before execution.

The campaign must never reinterpret `invalid_bed`, `failed`, or `blocked` as a dispatch null.

## Future-blind boundary

Every generated case has two distinct typed products:

- `VisibleHeader`: event/world identity, current clock interval, factor scope, routing shards, current change,
  novelty and uncertainty summaries, deadline slack, prior public-history digest, payload bytes, and explicit
  idle/storm flags; and
- `EvaluatorTruth`: niche, actor values, pair interactions, redundancy groups, irreducible-noise identity,
  hidden-state digest, and future consequence.

`DispatchPolicy.select` accepts exactly one `VisibleHeader`. It cannot receive `EvaluatorTruth`, inactive
actor outputs, the future consequence, hidden state, or an oracle coalition. The primary critic receives
only delayed exact fork records during tune/calibration. Those records contain empty/singleton/pair utility
effects and a public fork-authority digest derived from event, world, and prior-public-history identity; the
evaluator's hidden-state digest never crosses the fit boundary. Records become available after the source event. The policy is
frozen before gate, held-out, and fresh deployment. Deployment oracle-fork allowance is exactly zero.
Irreducible noisy-TV cases reuse ordinary plausible factor scopes and carry high novelty/uncertainty; no
`noise` scope, evaluator niche, or semantic noise flag appears in event or world identity.

The header is deliberately structured and can name bounded factor scopes. This is the narrow premise EDCM
can license. It is not evidence that factor scopes can be learned from raw sensing.

## Arms and exact controls

The fixed arm order is authority-bound:

1. interaction-aware learned value-of-computation dispatch;
2. individual-only exact credit;
3. learned dispatch without cold-start exploration;
4. exact-rate random exploration;
5. the EDCM-aligned hard sentinel;
6. aggregate-outcome bandit routing;
7. uncertainty and novelty gates;
8. full activation;
9. reactive/cheapest and fixed-sparse controls;
10. periodic, random, and shuffled per-actor exact-rate schedules;
11. whole-coalition exact-multiset shuffle;
12. gate-selected homogeneous best-single computation at exact total actor work;
13. tuned best single;
14. an equal-or-greater-work visible-history recurrent control; and
15. an evaluator-only oracle, permanently nonpromotable.

Rate controls derive schedules only after the learned schedule is frozen. Periodic, random, and shuffled
controls preserve every actor's exact call count. The coalition shuffle preserves the complete coalition
multiset. The homogeneous control preserves effective total calls and is padded to the learned actor-work
floor. The recurrent control is padded to the learned full-work floor without receiving evaluator state.

The primary critic estimates individual and pair terms from factor scope plus coarse current change and
joint novelty/uncertainty bins. Surprise does not independently trigger work: the bins matter only through
their calibrated same-state decision value. Candidate retrieval is capped at
`K`, coalition size at `C`, and considered coalitions at `B`. Coalitions are content-addressed, temporary
objects; no coalition state persists across events. A generated scaling assay registers 0, 64, 256, and
1,024 dormant actors at three subscription densities. It queries a precomputed top-`K` scope index without
a global scan while separately reporting and charging population-dependent index construction, retained
index byte-time, and bounded query work. Bounded-central dispatch is the implemented candidate in this
scaffold. Sharded-subscription and peer-nomination execution remain an explicit implementation gap; no
decentralization, ecosystem, or society claim is licensed.

## Exact credit fixtures

For each calibration state the scaffold evaluates the empty coalition, all singleton forks, and all pair
forks. It reports:

```text
individual_i = U({i}) - U({})
interaction_ij = U({i,j}) - U({i}) - U({j}) + U({})
difference_i(C) = U(C) - U(C without i) - resource_debit_i
```

Every fork and critic target is charged. The generated bed includes:

- a binder pair with zero singleton effects and a positive joint effect;
- substitutable retrieval actors with a negative pair interaction;
- four EDCM-aligned unique niches;
- a contradiction niche with a message-dependent planner/verifier interaction;
- a regime-shift actor that pays stale-reactivation work;
- idle headers, irreducible noisy TV, event storms, and disjoint world tokens.

Higher-order exact credit is an oracle diagnostic only and is not available to the learned policy.

## Full boundary accounting

Every arm reports the same nonnegative work vector:

- structured header intake;
- explicit idle-header floor;
- candidate retrieval and readiness bids;
- bounded dispatch search and exploration;
- actor execution;
- message operations and encoded bytes;
- exact counterfactual forks and critic training;
- stale reactivation; and
- receipt serialization.

`WorkCharges.as_escs_work_vector` losslessly projects those operation counters into the chassis'
`WorkVector` categories, and each compact study coalition can project its selected actor set into the live
runtime's immutable `DispatchDecision`. The compact receipt does not invent discarded beam members that it
did not retain.

Retained-state byte-time and total encoded bytes remain separate dimensions rather than being silently mixed
with abstract operations. Training, failed coalition trials, exact forks, control-plane work, idle work, and
deployment work are all inside the lifecycle boundary. These deterministic counters are not measured
energy, hardware operations, latency, or wall time. The Studio governor remains responsible for host
telemetry and safe admission.

Three correlated storm arrivals share one created tick. A deterministic FIFO service assay has an exact
events-per-tick capacity and queue cap, records peak depth, deadline misses, and drain time, and requires the
queue to recover without dropping or silently postponing work.

## Verdict contract

Across five paired seeds, the preliminary producer result is favorable only if all registered confidence
bounds and every-seed directions pass:

- learned utility is within `0.01` of full activation;
- total lifecycle work is at least `25%` lower;
- the learned arm contributes a positive Pareto point over the hard sentinel, individual-only credit, all
  rate/coalition-matched controls, homogeneous computation, tuned best single, and equal-work recurrence;
- predicted marginal value is calibrated on unseen world tokens;
- the cold-start pair is discovered without deployment oracle access and more often than random exploration;
- irreducible noise causes no excess activation;
- high-credit actor and pair-message lesions selectively reduce utility;
- queue/deadline, candidate, beam, no-global-scan, and dormant-population slope constraints hold; and
- every future/evaluator leakage check remains closed.

The producer is not final. Verification regenerates every producer gate and held-out row byte-for-byte from
the sealed code/config, then executes the same registered aggregate on five disjoint fresh seeds and a
disjoint fresh world family. A `positive` result requires both producer and fresh aggregates to be positive.
A mismatch routes to implementation failure, not a scientific null.

Even a fresh-positive result remains a disabled Gate-A candidate at generated structured-observation scope.
Natural/session-disjoint confirmation, X0, integrated ESCS controls, independent implementation, current
governor authority, and every Gate-B condition remain unmet.

## Resume and authority

The serial CPU runner checkpoints atomically after each complete phase/seed row. A checkpoint binds config
authority, implementation-manifest identity, exact EDCM entry-gate evidence, row digests, and seed prefixes.
Changed code, config, prerequisite evidence, row order, or output identity fails closed. Config, manifest,
EDCM producer, EDCM verifier, checkpoint, producer output, and verification output must be distinct files.

The canonical implementation manifest binds the exact byte length and SHA-256 of the config, study module,
runner, tests, and this audit. Its review status is `preregistered-scaffold-unexecuted`; the manifest records
identity and does not bypass `GOV_1`, EDCM verification, or heavy-lane admission.

## Known gaps

- The environment, actors, scopes, and counterfactual forks are generated.
- Bounded-central dispatch is implemented; sharded and peer-nomination organizations are not yet runnable.
- The critic is a compact scope-conditioned fixture, not a general learned dispatcher.
- The verifier shares the frozen implementation; an independent implementation path would strengthen the
  evidence.
- Abstract work and encoded bytes are deterministic accounting, not empirical energy or latency.
- The structured header assumes factor scopes and therefore cannot support raw event-formation or natural
  factor-discovery claims.
- Candidate activation stays disabled, and scientific promotion stays blocked, under every result.
