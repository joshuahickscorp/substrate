## Question and evidence boundary

X3 asks whether rare actor birth, differentiation, merge, pruning, and repair can improve online
adaptation and post-lesion recovery beyond fixed final capacity, the same final genotype installed
from the first event, same-grammar random search, mutation-disabled topology, fixed spare capacity,
restart at the current stream position, and full retraining. An oracle topology arm is a labeled
nonpromotable ceiling.

This implementation does not answer that question. It is a runnable counterfactual mechanics bed
whose official configuration has `activation.enabled=false`. It never mutates a live
`CoalitionRuntime`, and every arm reports zero factual topology mutations. The fixture can rehearse
proposal, shadow, canary, commitment, consequence, and rollback records while the effective topology
remains byte-identical to the base snapshot. It cannot support claims about morphogenesis, actor
invention, capability, computational efficiency, developmental learning, biology, or natural data.

## Fixed experimental contract

The config is `configs/experiment/escs_x3_topology_adaptation.json`. It freezes five paired producer
seeds, five disjoint fresh-verifier seeds, four neutral-initialization conditions (canonical,
actor-ID permutation, scope-label permutation, and topology permutation), and streams containing:

- recurring niches and genuinely new regimes;
- abrupt and gradual drift;
- selective actor lesion and sensor failure periods;
- held-out post-lesion recovery;
- return of an old regime;
- an untouched future-learning phase.

All non-oracle arms are matched to the registered final capacity. The same-final-genotype control
uses the candidate's exact final genotype from event zero. The restart control begins at the current
stream position and does not replay the original stream. Full retraining is a non-efficiency upper
bound and is charged for original-stream replay. The oracle is always nonpromotable.

## Transaction and accounting boundary

Every counterfactual structural arm reports candidate-generation trials, mutation search,
proposals, shadow events/work, canary events/work, failed candidates/work, counterfactual commit
candidates, consequence evaluations, rollbacks/work, genotype receipt bytes, retained genotype
bytes and byte-time, replay bytes, and topology churn. Failed candidates, shadow evaluation,
canaries, rollback, serialization, and retention are never free.

The same run also reports the complete ESCS `WorkVector`: raw intake, event formation, graph/index
maintenance, dispatch/exploration, actor execution, messages, counterfactual credit, learning,
archive/erasure, retained byte-time, and idle floor. Retained byte-time remains separate from
abstract operation work; they are not added into a unitless score.

The generated G0 mechanics fixture creates an immutable lesion observation, inferred repair
hypothesis, topology-transaction commitment to refuse-and-rollback, and a consequence that confirms
the base topology was preserved. The event ledger and lifecycle ledger must replay exactly. This is
governance mechanics, not a successful topology change.

## Verdict routing

Terminal scientific routes are exactly `positive`, `null`, `invalid_bed`, and `failed`:

- `failed`: a transaction, identity, ledger, authority, control-coverage, accounting, or
  no-factual-effect invariant fails;
- `invalid_bed`: drift, lesion, oracle headroom, off-ceiling, or future-learning difficulty is
  absent; this is not a mechanism null;
- `null`: the bed is valid but any mandatory fixed/random/restart/retraining comparator matches the
  online-utility/recovery frontier, full charged work or peak state is worse, old-function
  regression exceeds the SESOI, future learnability fails, topology churn is excessive, or roles do
  not recur after neutral initialization and registered permutations;
- `positive`: producer and fresh verifier both satisfy positive paired confidence bounds over every
  registered comparator on online utility and recovery with no extra work/state and every protected
  gate passing. Even this route is only a disabled feature-flag candidate pending a separately
  enabled, independently reviewed campaign revision.

A complete producer receipt is self-hashed and seed-prefix resumable. The verifier first regenerates
every producer row exactly, then runs disjoint fresh seeds and sessions with its own atomic,
self-hashed checkpoint and receipt. Positive requires both producer and fresh routes to be positive;
any failed or invalid route propagates, and all other valid combinations route to null.
