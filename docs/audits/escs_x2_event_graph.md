# ESCS X2 Event-Graph Scaffold Audit

Status: preregistered, activation-disabled, unexecuted scaffold
Evidence ceiling: deterministic generated mechanics only
Scientific promotion: forbidden

## Question and boundary

X2 asks whether event-sourced causal state improves held-out intervention ranking or produces a
noninferior action-value result with materially lower fully charged work than fixed recurrent state,
bounded raw history, episodic key-value memory, archive-only retrieval, and exact global history. It
also retains reactive, periodic-summary, action-blind, referent-shuffled, random-graph,
action-shuffled-graph, and oracle-state controls.

This implementation is a tiny generated mechanics bed. It does not test natural perception, learned
event formation, a learned world model, or natural action benefit. It makes no prediction-only claim:
the primary quality endpoints are held-out intervention ranking and realized action value. A positive
fixture result is at most a feature-flagged candidate pattern and cannot promote ESCS.

## Fixed contract

The config is `configs/experiment/escs_x2_event_graph.json`. It freezes five paired producer seeds,
five disjoint fresh-verifier seeds, all arms, lifecycle charges, and both positive routes:

- at least `0.05` intervention-ranking gain with a positive paired lower confidence bound, at no
  greater operation work or retained state than the strongest quality control; or
- noninferiority within `0.01`, at least `30%` operation-work saving, and no greater retained state.

Every graph revision, poison, deletion, branch-isolation, compaction, provenance, schema-transfer,
replay-authority, and calibrated-abstention gate must pass. An oracle failure, reactive saturation,
missing attack, schema, action, or session-disjointness condition is `invalid_bed`, not a mechanism
null. An ESCS identity/archive/accounting failure is `failed`. A valid controlled miss is
`controlled_null` and leaves the event graph as evidence infrastructure only.

## Mechanics covered

Each seed builds repository-native `ObservationEvent`, `HypothesisEvent`, `CommitmentEvent`, and
`ConsequenceEvent` records in one `EventLedger`; adds an immutable consequence revision; creates a
same-parent simulated branch; replays the hash chain; charges an independent `LifecycleLedger`; and
uses `BoundedArchive` for compaction, cold retrieval, logical deletion, dependent index/cache removal,
and explicit loss of exact replay authority. A digest-recomputed poison is rejected before replay.

The generated comparison fixture includes session-disjoint queries, stable event/referent identity,
canonical and changed schema-local factor names, stale facts plus revisions, adversarial poison,
counterfactual records that must not enter factual state, a deletion marker, irrelevant history, and
ambiguous queries requiring calibrated abstention. Schema transfer uses stable referent/event identity;
it receives no schema-name translation map. This is an identity mechanics test, not evidence of learned
semantic transfer.

## Fully charged boundary

Every arm reports raw transport/adapters, event formation, graph/index maintenance, dispatch/retrieval,
actor execution, messages, counterfactual work, learning, archive/erasure, retained byte-time, idle
floor, serialization, current retained state bytes, and serialized bytes. Retained byte-time is kept
separate from abstract operation work rather than being summed into a unitless resource score.

The graph arm pays for event intake, indexing, typed causal state, messages, archive publication,
retrieval, compaction, deletion, provenance, branch isolation, and receipt serialization. Archive-only
and global-history arms pay their full per-query scans. The oracle is nonpromotable.

## Resume, verifier, and authority

Producer and fresh-verifier checkpoints are atomic, self-hashed, prefix-ordered by the frozen seed
lists, and bound to exact config and implementation authorities. A complete producer receipt is
self-hashed and immutable. The verifier regenerates every producer row before running its disjoint
fresh sessions and seeds; it also supports seed-boundary resume and emits a separate self-hashed
verification receipt.

`proof/ESCS_X2_EVENT_GRAPH.implementation-authority.json` binds the config, module, runner, and this
audit. It intentionally binds no produced result. Official execution fails closed while activation is
disabled. The exploratory path exists for focused tests only.

## Prerequisites and remaining gaps

Official X2 remains blocked until current verified P5 context-control selection and P6 10k continual
stream receipts exist, alongside the existing ESCS mechanics and P7 branch/action-plane mechanics.
The eventual claim-bearing run must use the P5-selected context control, the P6 lifecycle, charged X0
raw event formation, independently metered resources, and a separately frozen enabled campaign
revision. It also needs natural or materially richer held-out world families before any intelligence,
adaptability, or computational-efficiency claim is considered.
