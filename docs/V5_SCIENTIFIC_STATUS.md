## What remains scientifically unearned

The frozen 5,760-unit DAG covers 80 developmental histories, 18 arms, 20 phases,
and 576,000 generated events or episodes. None of those principal, replication,
or open-world units is terminal yet. Consequently the following are not yet
established at principal scale:

- persistent-entity and integrated-entity advantage;
- replication of multimodal effects;
- generator-held-out open-world transfer;
- v4 capability preservation after full sensorium integration;
- zero surviving claim mutations;
- clean-clone double regeneration; and
- any terminal v5 classification.

The moderate admission authority allows a principal launch only after the ready
tag and exact ready-source digest gate pass. The current ready-stage status has
green admission but no `substrate-v5-sensorium-ready` tag, so automatic principal
authorization remains false.
