# One Odyssey toward semi-conscious general intelligence

18 September 2026. Primary-source research and an implementation synthesis, not a completed experiment, systematic literature review or consciousness determination.

## The target is not being lowered

**Substrate's target is semi-conscious general intelligence in a continuing developmental entity. There is one Odyssey.** Its internal chapters cover mechanisms, integration, apprenticeship, plasticity, continuity and full-target interpretation. Calibration pilots and separately locked comparisons are instruments within that one program, not successor Odysseys and not substitute destinations.

“Semi-conscious” is the project's target term, not an established numerical scale. Here it directs work toward limited but genuinely integrated access to self/world representations, recurrent cognition, metacognitive control, perspective and agency, while actively investigating whether any subjective experience accompanies those processes. Functional findings and phenomenal interpretations are reported separately. The target must not quietly become a memory agent, an eloquent self-description or success in six small worlds.

The scientific question is consequently stronger than “can this architecture imitate awareness?” It is whether development creates a causally integrated organization with broad transferable competence, internally available self/world structure and theoretically relevant consciousness properties. No release tag, signed declaration, module name or count of features answers that question.

## Research pass: what matters and what it does not establish

**Consciousness indicators.** Butlin and colleagues' 2023 report translates several neuroscientific theories into computational indicators: recurrence, workspace access, higher-order representation, predictive processing and attention-schema functions.[1] The later *Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems*, published online in November 2025 and in the June 2026 issue of *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, develops this theory-based approach.[2] These motivate differentiated evidence and causal interventions, not a checkbox certificate. A small literal implementation of an indicator may omit the organization that makes the theory explanatory.

**Access versus experience.** Block's distinction separates availability for reasoning/control from phenomenal experience.[3] This lets us specify access mechanisms without pretending they settle what, if anything, the system feels. Conversely, uncertainty about experience is not a reason to abandon a concrete engineering and experimental program. The experiment should expose where its conclusions depend on a contested bridge from computation to phenomenology.

**Competing explanatory commitments.** Seth's biological-naturalist argument challenges the assumption that reproducing abstract computation is sufficient for consciousness.[4] We retain that challenge rather than assuming computational functionalism has won. The implementation can test computational mechanisms; its success would not by itself refute biological constraints. A genuinely theory-aware report must say which interpretations remain compatible with the observations.

**A real workspace experiment.** Dossa and colleagues implemented a multimodal global-workspace agent in an embodied navigation setting.[5] This is more informative than a diagram labeled “workspace”: task behavior can be compared under architectural changes. Their particular findings do not establish universal superiority of larger workspaces. Our text/structured-data processors do not reproduce their learned audiovisual system.

**Selection rather than unlimited context.** Bengio's consciousness prior proposes sparse, low-dimensional representations selected from richer underlying states.[6] It motivates a capacity bottleneck and explicit competition. It is not a proof that a bottleneck is conscious, nor evidence that adding more context makes a system more aware. Capacity is an experimental factor, with accuracy, transfer, interference and resource consequences.

**Acquired structure.** DreamCoder learns compositional program abstractions and search guidance.[7] Its important design lesson is that development can change the language in which later problems are solved. Substrate now implements a much smaller mechanism: role-normalized numeric templates mined from distinct programs, bounded instantiation and separately qualified reuse. It does not implement DreamCoder's neural wake-sleep learner or general concept discovery.

**Generality is not file size.** Chollet's intelligence-measurement proposal emphasizes priors, experience, generalization and skill-acquisition efficiency.[8] The Levels of AGI framework similarly distinguishes breadth, performance and autonomy.[9] These motivate an evidence profile, not a single “intelligence per kilobyte” or “percentage conscious” headline. A compact memorizer, a slow novel-problem solver and a broad learner may occupy different positions on each axis.

**Responsible investigation.** Work on responsible AI-consciousness research and AI welfare argues for explicit uncertainty and preparation rather than confident anthropomorphic claims or automatic dismissal.[10,11] This charter requires review before scaling agentic experiments. It does not introduce synthetic distress, suffering rewards, shutdown avoidance or self-preservation objectives as routes to consciousness.

## The implemented perspective and workspace model

A perspective is an attributed, scoped representation:

```text
subject · viewpoint · situation/scope · claim · value
stance · evidence identity · confidence · parent representations
```

“Where I think the object is,” “where another observer thinks it is” and “where it would be under an imagined intervention” are not interchangeable records. Contradiction detection compares the same subject, viewpoint, scope and claim; imagination is not counterevidence merely because its contents differ. Confidence does not decide truth by majority vote. The current API can represent these alternatives; a general learned theory of other minds remains to be implemented and measured.

The bounded workspace admits competing representations under slot and serialized-byte limits. Observation, procedural routing, memory, self-forecasting and goal processors propose candidates. A guard critic can discover that a selected procedure cannot accept the current input. A subsequent broadcast can change routing; the self forecast can change how much verified experience is retrieved. Projected broadcasts also reach the actual configured LLM request path as context, never authority.

The cycle has recurrence because later processors consume a prior broadcast and can change the next one. It does not claim that three programmed iterations are equivalent to neural recurrent processing in every consciousness theory. Current persistence is an event-triggered, bounded sequence with durable developmental working-state summaries, not an always-running phenomenal stream.

During development, independently admitted feedback updates self-forecast calibration and channel-selection statistics. Channel credit is observational and heuristic, not a solved causal-credit-assignment algorithm. Frozen evaluation constructs ephemeral working state without modifying the entity or admitting final labels. A full trace can be retained under the existing deterministic policy; every trial retains its compact outcome and cognitive summary.

Interventions include no workspace, no recurrence, no self-model, no perspective labels and no broadcast. The perspective-label intervention currently changes the organ's access to viewpoint/stance labels; it is not a demonstrated lesion of a learned perspective-taking faculty. Report exactly which mechanism was disabled. A failed abstraction or lesion hypothesis is data, not permission to rename the same implementation a success.

Agency attribution is separately tied to signed observations of intended actions, executed actions and explicit controls. It distinguishes actor matching, prediction matching and outcome difference under intervention. Temporal sequence alone is insufficient, and this interface does not establish subjective agency.

## Does Substrate get larger as it gets more intelligent?

There is no necessary monotonic relation. The useful accounting separates five objects.

| Object | What changes | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Runtime/source kernel | Engine and supported operations | Can stay approximately fixed while the entity develops. Source LOC is not neural capacity. |
| Acquired organization | Beliefs, procedures, abstractions, competence, projects, self/world structure | Can expand, consolidate or reorganize. Fewer procedures can solve more families when reusable structure is found. |
| Developmental evidence | Episodes, failures, certificates, revisions and provenance | Usually accumulates unless an explicit retention policy archives it. More evidence is not automatically more usable intelligence. |
| Neural artifacts | Weights, adapters, embeddings, routers and representation payloads | Learning may alter values without changing parameter count; new modules may grow the artifact; compression may reduce bytes at some fidelity/cost. |
| Active physical state | Working memory, recurrent state, caches, resident model pages and device buffers | Depends on task, scheduling and hardware. Serialized file bytes do not measure residency. |

Consider a hypothetical progression: 100 task-specific solutions become ten reusable methods. The history may grow because it retains the discovery and its failed attempts, while the active procedural representation becomes smaller. Alternatively, an entity can gain a new perceptual capacity by adding a larger neural organ. Neither change can be interpreted from size alone.

The implementation measures entity-reachable current objects and historical closure, raw and packed payload bytes, namespace counts and native instruction counts. Shared objects are counted once under a disclosed stable allocation rule. It reports external model bytes, resident process memory and device residency as unknown unless an actual instrument supplies them. It does not substitute source-code size for the deployed organism's footprint.

A growth comparison is valid only for declared checkpoints and lineage. Its capability counterpart needs independently assessed transfer, retention, calibration, task quality and acquisition cost. A larger checkpoint can be less capable; a compressed representation can be faster but lose important behavior. Measure quality and cost jointly and report failures.

The new code does **not** delete old procedures or evidence merely to display shrinking intelligence. General semantic compaction, forgetting policies, automatic safe garbage collection and neural architecture growth remain research work. Content addressing removes byte-identical duplication; candidate abstraction supports reuse; neither proves an optimal representation.

## How intelligence is physically manifested

The proposed artifact is a hybrid organization, not a single neural tensor:

```text
engineer.substrate/
    organization.nr.json       whole-organization identity and dependency contract
    entity.bundle              durable records, lineage, event history and provenance
    state.safetensors          optional actual learned numeric sufficient statistics
    assets/<content hash>      explicitly supplied neural/other artifact payloads
```

The supplied runtime remains a separate, pinned dependency. Missing organ artifacts are listed. Export and restore do not install credentials, grant tool permissions, restart a writer or qualify a new host. Safetensors provides a tensor format, not the semantics of a cognitive organization or its executable runtime.[12] The optional writer serializes actual learned statistics; it does not emit random weights and call them a brain.

The important transition contract is more like `(state, observation) → (state', action)` than merely “the same prompt produces the same text.” A whole-entity port must preserve projects, scopes, uncertainty, state updates, source attribution, invalidation behavior and relevant resource constraints, not just a response benchmark.

### Safetensors, NR and NX are related but not equivalent

Safetensors can carry learned arrays inside an organ or the wider entity. A portable NR-style representation can describe arrays, graphs, programs, schemas, state, provenance and dependencies. An NX-style realization specializes accepted semantics for a machine: executable regions, layout, kernels, state residency, scheduling and calling contracts.

The user's Hawking functional-compilation plan requires exact NR binding, full payload and semantic provenance, machine/runtime compatibility, and independent fidelity and cost assessment. It also distinguishes immutable executable identity from explicitly mutable external state.[13] Substrate follows that distinction rather than silently rewriting a qualified NX as it learns.

This drop implements a Substrate-owned whole-organization NR-like package and a machine/runtime-bound closed-DAG image for currently qualified native skills. The latter loads and checks serialized instructions, then runs them through the existing bounded interpreter under a separate image qualification. It is not machine-code generation, FPGA lowering, a whole neural-model compiler or proof of compatibility with Hawking's current ABI.

There is **no architectural cap restricting Hawking to one small organ**. A future qualified adapter may accept all relevant organization or inherited runtime machinery. The immediate lowering implementation is narrower than the intended eventual port. All residual runtime, neural artifacts, tokenizer/configuration, transport, state and kernel dependencies must be counted rather than hidden outside the “tiny executable.”

Development can continue while a particular executable stays immutable: mutable entity state evolves under the declared transition contract; a changed procedure or fitted neural organ creates a new candidate artifact, which must be qualified before replacement. A frozen NR snapshot can produce multiple machine realizations without becoming multiple entities by filename convention alone.

## One Odyssey: questions, chapters and closure

One charter owns the target, while individual comparisons remain locked, independently authorized and auditable. This avoids two opposite errors: pretending one tiny test covers the full ambition, and perpetually deferring the ambition to another Odyssey.

| Internal chapter | Its obligation within the one Odyssey |
|---|---|
| Mechanisms | Establish verified development, guarded reuse, reference-oracle validity and complete cost accounting. |
| Integration | Challenge recurrence, broadcast, perspective and self-monitoring with information-routing and lesion experiments. |
| Apprenticeship | Develop in independently designed unfamiliar proof, repository, simulation and perception domains; test transfer rather than memorized cases. |
| Plasticity | Compare remembering, compiling and neural changes; measure retention, interference, acquisition costs and representation growth. |
| Continuity | Challenge organ/host changes, interrupted projects, action attribution and controlled same-identity continuation. |
| Closure | Interpret generality and theory-specific consciousness evidence on the same qualified lineage; include counterevidence and remaining uncertainty. |

The program ledger admits complete, independently reviewed chapter evidence with exact design, code, artifacts, histories, domains, controls, costs and outcome hashes. It retains supported, contradicted and inconclusive endpoints separately. Expired or revoked authority does not erase history but cannot supply current qualification. Findings from different artifacts are not automatically combined into a qualified “super-entity.” A completed jobs table is not a full-target verdict.

The initial world runner remains six bounded families. General external apprenticeship now has prospective inquiry/instrument/action contracts, but real formal, repository, browser and perception dispatchers are not supplied by those contracts. Full multi-domain integration studies, open-ended environment generation, learned recurrent neural models and neural fitting still require implementation. Those are obligations **inside this Odyssey**, not renamed later projects.

## What this drop actually adds

`workspace.py` owns attributed representations, capacity-limited recurrent selection, behavioral routing/retrieval effects, developmental self-forecast feedback and qualified action attribution. `language.py` and `cognition.py` own bounded acquired-template reuse and its separate qualification path. `research.py` owns the one-Odyssey charter, evidence profile and prospective apprenticeship contracts. Existing `handoff.py` now owns migration, training preparation, physical accounting, tensor inventory, complete organization export/restore and native-region images. `odyssey.py` and `organs.py` connect the new mechanisms to the actual worker/request path.

All implementation is source-delivered and unrun. There are no fabricated outcomes, lifespan, trained weights, measured speedups or consciousness determinations. The target remains semi-conscious general intelligence; the release describes exactly which machinery is now available for pursuing it.
