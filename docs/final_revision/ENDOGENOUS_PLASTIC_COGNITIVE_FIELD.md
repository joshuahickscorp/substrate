# Endogenous Plastic Cognitive Field Foundation

## Status and claim boundary

This document specifies a pre-freeze foundation and feasibility workstream for
the Substrate Final Revision. It does not change the current campaign’s frozen
historical null, hypotheses, primary endpoints, strongest baseline, S2 resource
controls, hidden challenge answers, or classification rules. Foundation
results receive zero current endpoint and classification credit. External
activation remains false.

The full field architecture, meaningful-scale native training, large candidate

## Thesis

An Endogenously Plastic Cognitive Field is a persistent cognitive material
whose topology, connection strengths, precision, procedures, attractors,
memories, and active state are the accumulated, compressed, verified
consequence of its developmental history.

Thoughts are transient trajectories through the field. Learning is selective,
verification-gated deformation. Concepts are stable but revisable structures.
Skills are compiled pathways. Unresolved competing structures represent
uncertainty. Temporary shadow fields support imagination and counterfactual
work. An exact constitutional shell protects identity, evidence, permissions,
claim boundaries, and irreversible commitments. Replaceable models, sensors,
tools, and bodies are organs and interfaces; none owns the entity.

No final physical form is selected here. Monolithic, graph-dynamical, cellular,
state-space, continuous-time, event-sourced, hybrid, and new Substrate-native
forms remain open.

## Formal state

The executable foundation represents:

- `Θ`: slow shared cognitive laws.
- `P_t`: fast and intermediate plastic connection state.
- `G_t`: current topology.
- `Z_t`: active cognitive field state.
- `E_t`: exact constitutional and epistemic state.
- `A`: append-only developmental archive.
- `C_t`: compiled procedures and stable cognitive pathways.
- `M_t`: model, body, tool, and sensor competence state.

The transition contract consumes all eight state regions plus observations,
goals, body state, and elapsed time. The implementation distinguishes a
monotonic-clock attestation from an explicitly simulated test clock. Plasticity may propose changes to
`P_t`, `G_t`, precision, and `C_t`; durable changes require independent
verification.

Callers receive detached snapshots of the plastic, topology, cell, and exact
stores; durable changes route through receipt-bearing methods. The approximate field cannot directly rewrite the exact shell. The shell owns
identity, lineage, goal commitments, evidence provenance, permissions, claim
boundaries, activation state, checkpoint integrity, and irreversible
commitments.

## Timescales and plasticity

The shared interfaces distinguish momentary activation, fast plasticity,
intermediate plasticity, slow consolidation, rare kernel revision, and
topological development. Runtime changes are sparse, bounded, local where
possible, reversible, receipt-bearing, and verification-gated.

Low-bit rewrite proposals use the native set `{-2, -1, 0, +1, +2}`. A
model-generated thought can propose or simulate a rewrite but cannot commit it.
Held-out improvement and retention are computed from sealed evaluation cases;
callers cannot provide pass booleans. Evaluator identity, batch digest, raw
outcomes, provenance, and rollback state are retained in receipts.

Metaplastic relations carry value, precision, stability, scope, provenance,
last verification, and contradictions. The states are new, provisional,
supported, consolidated, reopened, and refuted. Consolidation resists isolated
noise; repeated verified contradiction can reopen it.

## Native precision

The foundation implements binary, ternary, quinary, seven-state powers-of-two,
4-bit, and 8-bit alphabets plus architecture-neutral classes for learned codebooks, sparse
outliers, vector quantization, and adaptive mixed radix. Packing is exact;
quinary triplets use seven bits.

Precision promotion runs a sealed precision ablation. It requires raw
held-out improvement attributable to the target alphabet, measured added-bit
cost, a minimum future-utility-per-added-byte threshold, and preserved shell
integrity. Demotion must preserve sealed future utility; continued rent is
auditable. Frozen precision can reopen on contradiction. A tiny projected
ternary training loop is executable, but meaningful-scale native training is
not complete.

The governing rule is:

> Every additional bit must earn its continued existence through verified
> future cognitive value.

No global five-gigabyte objective exists. The microfixture harness covers 512 MB,
1 GB, 2 GB, 5 GB, 10 GB, and an unconstrained reference. It reports raw utility,
actual serialized payload and checkpoint bytes, deterministic latency/energy
operation proxies, rare-case accuracy, reconstruction error,
retention, recovery, and Pareto frontiers. It does not claim full-process RSS,
hardware energy, or calibrated probabilistic uncertainty. Volatile wall-clock
codec observations are kept in the ignored run ledger rather than embedded in
content-addressed authorities.

## Topology, attractors, and shadow fields

Topology operations allocate, connect, disconnect, split, merge, compile,
prune, archive, restore, and rollback. Every change is evaluated against
sealed topology queries and records its trigger, prior and proposed structures,
evaluator-computed expected value, measured serialized byte delta, enforced
resource envelope, affected beliefs and procedures, and complete rollback
state. Unbounded or rent-free growth is refused.

Generic cognitive cells can represent objects, events, concepts, beliefs,
goals, procedures, bodies, tools, models, places, causal relations, and
unresolved hypotheses. A shared local rule or activity receipt is not evidence
of useful self-organization.

Bounded leaky-competition attractor microfixtures cover coherent settling, preservation of unresolved
alternatives, escape from false interpretations after evidence, and recovery
after corruption. Recovery never fabricates lost evidence.

Shadow fields copy only relevant sparse regions and reject queries outside the
copied region. Fork, perturb, run, compare, discard, and promote operations
preserve the distinctions among thinking, believing, knowing, and learning.
A single-relation foundation promotion must pass the ordinary sealed
PlasticityPropose/Verify/Commit route; promotion claims verified learning, not
knowledge.

## Cognitive compiler and real elapsed time

The compiler observes traces, proposes and verifies procedures against sealed
cases, performs a bounded peephole compilation, executes bytecode, monitors failure, invalidates unsafe procedures, and
decompiles back to flexible reasoning. Instructions include `OBSERVE`, `BIND`,
`RETRIEVE`, `COMPARE`, `INFER`, `SIMULATE`, `VERIFY`, `REVISE`, `DEFER`,
`COMMIT`, and `ROLLBACK`; the compiler may emit the internal fused `EQUAL`
opcode. Every admitted opcode has executable semantics in the microfixture.

Compiled procedures retain inputs, executable `truthy:<binding>` assumptions,
scope, branch and failure conditions, verification method, measured
instruction cost, and provenance. Metadata must match the verified proposal.
Novel exceptions reopen flexible reasoning.

Continuous-time contracts provide a caller-independent local
`time.monotonic_ns` path and a separately labeled deterministic fixture clock
for scheduled observations, bounded consolidation, goal deadlines, prediction
expiry, and memory-decay proposals. Each advance processes at most 64 events.
The field does not generate meaningless activity to appear continuous and
makes no operating-system scheduling guarantee.
