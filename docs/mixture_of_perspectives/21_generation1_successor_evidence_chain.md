## 2. Canonical taxonomy

The scientific vocabulary from document 20 remains binding: `M`, `T+`, `N`, `F`, `I`, `P`, and `B`
describe evidence. The labels below describe a node's state inside this execution chain. They must
not be substituted for scientific evidence classes.

| Chain state | Meaning | Allowed transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `terminal-verified` | The named result and required verifier are complete, sealed, and clean. | May satisfy an exact declared dependency. |
| `bounded-candidate` | A positive generated pattern survived its registered bounded gate. | May enter a fresh or confirmatory design, never activation by itself. |
| `mechanics-only` | Plumbing, controls, and favorable/null discrimination work as constructed. | May justify a larger mechanics or robustness epoch. |
| `tuning-only` | Data were used to choose a design or hyperparameter. | Freeze the winner before untouched evaluation. |
| `verified-null` | A valid bed supports the registered null or fails the positive gate. | Prune, retire, or preserve as a control according to the frozen route. |
| `invalid-bed` | The task bed cannot answer the registered mechanism question. | Repair or replace the bed; do not call the mechanism null. |
| `pending-terminal` | Work may exist, but no clean terminal authority is available. | Wait, resume, or retry under the same frozen authority. |
| `dependency-gated` | Upstream evidence required by the DAG is absent or negative. | Hold without weakening the gate. |

Every executable node has one role:

- `adopt`: validate and bind an immutable upstream authority without rewriting it;
- `produce`: generate the preregistered result on its declared seed partition;
- `challenge`: repeat a frozen design on a disjoint same-code partition;
- `verify`: recompute receipts or aggregation through separately authored verification logic;
- `classify`: apply frozen thresholds and choose only a declared route;
- `expand`: run the next disjoint robustness epoch when the prior classification permits it;
- `synthesize`: aggregate terminal authorities without changing their evidence class;
- `hold`: stop a branch while allowing independent siblings to continue.

The only route verbs are `continue`, `prune`, `redesign`, and `hold`. A result may select a verb, but
it may not rewrite earlier seeds, thresholds, controls, claim scope, or receipts.

## 3. Program ownership

### 3.1 Existing consolidated authority

`generation1-consolidated-final-campaign-v1` remains responsible for consuming the frozen D1 and
extended successor-mechanics terminal authorities, regenerating their source evidence, applying its
declared fresh stress, and emitting one consolidated result. Its same-code regeneration and fresh
cycles are robustness evidence, not independent scientific confirmation.

The v4 parent must wait until that result exists and validates. Partial predecessor receipts and a
`running` or `waiting` status are not adoptable scientific evidence.

### 3.2 Adopting parent

`generation1-successor-evidence-chain-v4` is the active one-command owner. Its bounded contract is:

1. Bind its own implementation and the exact sealed horizon manifest authority.
2. Adopt the exact live D1, successor-mechanics, and consolidated-v1 parents by PID, create time,
   process-group identity, working directory, status schema, and immutable observation-only receipt.
3. Never signal adopted legacy work; restart a queue only after exact absence, no terminal result,
   and no residual worker, resource tracker, or ambiguous process identity.
4. Bind the consolidated result as the immutable prerequisite of
   `generation1-successor-horizon-v1`.
5. Start or resume the horizon without changing any completed predecessor artifact.
6. Wait for the horizon aggregate, independent artifact-verification receipt, and generated report.
7. Emit terminal parent status only after every required child authority is clean or explicitly
   classified through a frozen null-safe route.

The parent is an adopting orchestrator, not a new source of efficacy evidence.

### 3.3 Bounded five-epoch horizon

`generation1-successor-horizon-v1` adds five ordered robustness epochs, H01 through H05. They use
disjoint generated seeds and continue the existing cycle series with cycles 2 through 6. Each epoch
has the same high-level order:

1. Run the eligible D1 shards for that epoch.
2. Run the eligible successor-mechanics shards for that epoch.
3. Classify the epoch with frozen routing rules.
4. Seal the epoch authority before admitting the next epoch.

The horizon is deliberately finite. H05 is the end of this sealed program. Any later work requires
a separate append-only authority; horizon v2 supplies that separate authority without rewriting v1.

The sealed horizon contains 74 capsules: one admission boundary, 65 compute shards, five epoch
classifiers, one aggregate, one separately authored verifier, and one report receipt. Its maximum
planned work is 843,938 seconds, or 234.43 serial compute-hours. At the registered idle-host width
of eight workers that is 29.30 ideal worker-hours. While Hawking is active each compute capsule
downshifts to one worker, so wall time may be much longer. Frozen null and warning routes can reduce
the executed total by pruning only later eligible work.

## 5. Frozen horizon routing rules

The horizon may adapt future composition only through these preregistered routes:

| Observation | Current epoch classification | Later-epoch action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 satisfies the frozen candidate criteria | `bounded-candidate` | Continue D1 on the next disjoint epoch. |
| D1 is mixed or seed-sensitive | bounded mixed result | Continue D1 so the predeclared horizon can measure stability. |
| D1 supports the registered stable null | `verified-null` at this robustness scope | Prune D1 from every later horizon epoch. |
| D1 bed or receipt is invalid | `invalid-bed` or `pending-terminal` | Hold D1; repair under a new authority rather than relabeling it null. |
| A successor-mechanics lane passes its frozen lane gate | `mechanics-only` robustness pattern | Continue that lane on the next disjoint epoch. |
| A successor-mechanics lane fails its frozen lane gate | failed mechanics lane | Prune that lane from every later horizon epoch. |
| A receipt, seal, source binding, or verifier check fails | `pending-terminal` | Retry within policy or hold; never count the item as evidence. |

Pruning saves future computation but does not erase the failed or null receipts. Mixed or candidate D1
continues because the horizon was specifically added to measure robustness. Failed mechanics lanes do
not continue merely to consume the remaining budget.

## 6. Aggregate, verification, and report boundary

After H05, the horizon must emit one cross-epoch aggregate that preserves each epoch's inventory,
classification, seed authority, pruning decision, retry history, and artifact hashes. A separately
authored verifier must reconstruct receipt identity and aggregation, reject semantic mutations, and
confirm that pruned work was neither executed nor silently omitted.

This verifier is independent at the artifact and aggregation level. It is not automatically an
independent scientific replication because the horizon's producer, challenge, and robustness epochs
remain generated and substantially same-code. The generated report must preserve that distinction.

The report may conclude that a pattern repeated, mixed, weakened, or became null across the bounded
horizon. It may recommend a separately authored scientific confirmation. It may not authorize live
ESCS activation, topology mutation, an architecture-level claim, or Stage 3.

## 8. Operating invariants

- One admitted heavy lane remains the default unless an exact manifest authorizes safe independent
  work.
- The chain may pause or resume only processes it owns and identifies exactly.
- User work and externally owned workers are never signaled, suspended, or reclassified as chain
  capacity.
- Atomic work-item receipts are the resume boundary. A partial file is not evidence.
- Retries preserve the same scientific authority and are bounded. Exhausted retries enter a hold.
- Early results may prune only the future branches named in section 5. They cannot change thresholds,
  seed membership, controls, evidence language, or completed artifacts.
- Additional hours or an additional day are an operational envelope, not a promotion rule.
- Every artifact keeps `activation_allowed: false` and `scientific_promotion: false` unless a future,
  separately authorized scientific program changes those gates.

## 9. Completion condition for this scaffold

The successor evidence chain is complete only when the parent has adopted a valid consolidated-v1
result, H01 through H05 have each sealed all eligible work or frozen pruning decisions, the
cross-epoch aggregate validates, the separate artifact verifier is clean, and the generated report
preserves the claim boundary.

Until then, the exact description is: **the one-command successor evidence chain is waiting on its
adopted legacy authorities**. The future horizon is sealed and queued, but it is not scientific
evidence until its eligible capsules and independent artifact verification complete.
