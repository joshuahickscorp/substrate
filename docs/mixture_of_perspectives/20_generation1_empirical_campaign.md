The immediate job is deliberately broad and local. It reruns the existing programmatic cognitive experiment registry across many fresh outer seeds to discover stable candidate traces, stable nulls, seed-sensitive effects, missing measurements, and cross-capability niches. It does not activate the 31 ESCS perspective slots, install a learned dispatcher, mutate topology, download external benchmark packs, or make a natural-world intelligence claim.

The governing distinction is:

> Make entry into the registry cheap, reversible, and evidence-labeled. Make runtime activation selective and causally earned. Make scientific promotion difficult.

A low registry gate is not a low truth gate. It lets the project preserve many heterogeneous possibilities without paying for all of them on every event or pretending that inclusion proves efficacy.

## 1. Exact position of the project

The project has an implemented organizational chassis and a large body of controlled programmatic experiments. It does not yet have a demonstrated active cognitive substrate.

What exists now:

- Four immutable ESCS event types, event and referent identity, counterfactual branch separation, provenance, lifecycle accounting, bounded archives, typed messages, coalition runtime mechanics, and commitment/consequence authority.
- A content-addressed registry and assembly containing all 31 requested perspective facets. Every slot is disabled, the assembly is quiescent by default, and scientific promotion is unavailable.
- A finite G0 genotype representation and counterfactual evaluator with bounded state, operators, messages, costs, and refusals.
- Deterministic resource and causal monitoring, atomic checkpoints, resume boundaries, proof receipts, and null-safe campaign routing.
- Many local synthetic experiments spanning perception, abstraction, memory, plasticity, causality, planning, uncertainty, curiosity, communication, compression, and topology.

What does not exist yet:

- A learned event former that preserves decision utility while saving lifecycle work.
- A validated task bed with enough complementary actor niches to justify learned dispatch.
- A continual-learning mechanism that jointly improves retention and future learning.
- A learned simulator whose prediction quality produces action or intervention benefit.
- Runtime-authorized perspective activation.
- Enabled topology mutation or a frozen G0 construction authority.
- Natural or independently generated confirmation supporting an architecture-level claim.

The current substrate is therefore best described as a safe, inspectable, inert organizational contract plus a broad experimental inventory. Generation 1 must turn that inventory into a competence atlas and use the atlas to decide what, if anything, should be activated.

### 2.2 X0 event formation: verified strong null with large oracle headroom

The official X0 producer and disjoint fresh verifier both returned `strong_null_not_rejected` on valid generated asynchronous beds.

On the five producer held-out seeds:

- The learned event former saved a mean 37.85 percent of fully charged lifecycle work relative to always-on processing.
- It lost a mean 96.70 percent utility relative to always-on processing.
- It failed utility noninferiority, Pareto contribution, queue/deadline integrity, and the registered every-seed direction.
- Its noisy-TV activation exceeded the exact-rate shuffled control in the producer interval.
- The nonpromotable semantic oracle saved a mean 72.86 percent of lifecycle work and slightly improved utility over always-on processing.

The five fresh verifier seeds reproduced the important pattern: approximately 37.93 percent work saving with approximately 96.81 percent utility loss. The producer rows regenerated exactly.

This is not evidence against event-driven cognition in general. It is evidence against the current visible-packet plus delayed-value learner and against raw sparsity as a sufficient event principle. The oracle gap says the bed contains substantial event-selective efficiency, but the useful events depend on temporal and relational structure that the current learner does not recover.

Generation 1 consequence:

- Raw delta, novelty, surprise, entropy, or uncertainty cannot independently authorize compute.
- The next event-former revision must represent changes in relations, temporal phase, persistence, causal consequence, deadline, and task state.
- It must be compared with always-on, periodic, raw-delta, exact-rate shuffled, novelty, uncertainty, relational-state, temporal-state, and semantic-oracle controls.
- Rare consequential-event recall and weighted misses are primary. Activation rate alone is not success.

Source evidence: [X0 audit](../audits/escs_x0_event_formation.md), `proof/ESCS_X0_EVENT_FORMATION.json`, and `proof/ESCS_X0_EVENT_FORMATION.verification.json`.

### 2.3 EDCM-1: the coalition bed failed complementarity before routing

The official EDCM-1 receipt stopped at a verified `complementarity_gate_failed`. No held-out learned-routing result was produced.

The gate showed:

- Reactive-spatial cognition had a positive niche advantage of 0.06998, with a positive 95 percent interval.
- The short-horizon planner had a smaller positive niche advantage of 0.03053, also with a positive interval.
- Episodic retrieval had a negative niche advantage of -0.07511, with the entire interval below zero.
- Oracle routing headroom was positive but small: mean 0.03850.
- The verifier helped on disagreement by approximately 0.00120 and had exactly zero measured effect on agreement.
- The bed failed the requirements that every proposer have enough unique wins and a positive niche advantage.

This is an invalid bed for X1 dispatch, not a learned-dispatch null. Learning a router on a population without validated complementary competence would only optimize assignment among weak or harmful actors.

Generation 1 consequence:

- Measure individual actor competence before coalition training.
- Require at least two reproducible, context-disjoint positive niches and material oracle headroom before learned arbitration.
- Preserve episodic retrieval as an optional, abstaining actor until a task family demonstrates positive value.
- Trigger verification by disagreement or calibrated expected correction value, not continuously.
- Treat actor eligibility and actor usefulness as empirical quantities, not names assigned in advance.

Source evidence: [EDCM-1 audit](../audits/edcm1_event_triggered_coalition.md), `proof/EDCM1_EVENT_TRIGGERED_COALITION_V3.json`, and its verification artifact.

### 2.4 P6 continual learning: retention without future-learning benefit is a null

The independently verified 10,000-event P6 rung completed all 30 cells: five seeds by abrupt and gradual schedules by replay, no-replay, and fresh-init controls. The strict joint endpoint was null, so the null-safe router correctly refused the 100,000-event rung.

Against no replay, replay improved mean final retention by 0.15 on abrupt drift and 0.20 on gradual drift, but future first-window learning gain was exactly zero. Against fresh initialization, replay retained more old performance, but on abrupt drift its future-learning delta was -0.9375 in every seed. Gradual replay tied fresh initialization on future learning. No schedule and control combination satisfied the required joint gain.

Generation 1 consequence:

- Replay volume is not plasticity.
- Retention and future learning remain separate mandatory endpoints.
- Fresh initialization is a serious control because it exposes when stable state is preventing adaptation.
- The next candidate is a stable core plus transient actors, selective consolidation, local retirement, and recurrence-aware reactivation.
- Scaling a null to more events is prohibited unless a new mechanism and new preregistration change the causal premise.

Source evidence: [P6 audit](../P6_CONTINUAL_MILLION_EVENT_AUDIT_2026_07.md), `proof/P6_CONTINUAL_10K.json`, and `proof/P6_CONTINUAL_10K_INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.json`.

### 2.5 P7 action world model: prediction quality did not produce planning value

P7 established a reusable rendered action and branch mechanics surface, but the learned transition arms failed as planners. Across three layouts, the reactive rendered policy reached the goal on 1.00, 0.75, and 1.00 of episodes. Both primary learned transition arms reached 0.00 on all three. The object-centered predictor was better than the compact predictor on one-step exact agent-cell accuracy, but neither converted its prediction advantage into planning benefit.

Generation 1 consequence:

- One-step prediction, multistep rollout calibration, counterfactual ranking, plan validity, action value, and realized goal success must be reported separately.
- A simulator or imagination actor earns activation only through intervention ranking or action benefit.
- True-dynamics, reactive, model-free recurrent, action-blind, action-shuffled, matched-depth, and oracle controls remain mandatory.
- Forecast quality can be a diagnostic but never the planning verdict.

Source evidence: [P7 audit](../P7_ACTION_WORLD_MODEL_AUDIT_2026_07.md) and `proof/P7_ACTION_WORLD_MODEL_PREFLIGHT.json`.

## 3. Immediate Generation 1 local corpus

### 3.1 Frozen configuration

The immediate corpus is configured by `configs/experiment/generation1_cognitive_corpus.json` and executed by `scripts/generation1_cognitive_corpus.py`.

Its exact configured scale is:

- 24 distinct outer seeds, `20260801` through `20260824`.
- 128 eligible `cpu-now` experiment classes from the live registry.
- 3,072 logical experiment-seed coverage cells before retries.
- 3,003 actual experiment executions before retries: 125 inferential experiments by 24 effective seed authorities, plus one fixed-case and two mechanics-only experiments executed once and referenced by the remaining seed receipts.
- Inclusion of the F-series and the wrapper-smoke flag in the frozen scope.
- 15 overlapping capability packs containing 158 memberships and covering all 128 eligible classes at least once.
- No excluded experiment IDs.
- A minimum of 20 completed outer seeds for each experiment's aggregate classification.
- An 80 percent directional stability threshold.
- A top-level boolean `null_supported` value for every effective inferential observation, with at least 20 observations, before directional classification.
- A frozen five-seed variation canary using the first five outer seeds. Every inferential experiment must have five structurally distinct effective seed authorities; invariant scientific output is retained but quarantined as descriptive evidence.

The result tag is `generation1-exploratory-corpus-v2`. The claim scope is fresh-seed programmatic and synthetic capability census only. Natural-world, biological-equivalence, substrate-superiority, and scientific-promotion claims are prohibited.

The outer seed is not treated as evidence merely because it appears in a manifest. Generation 1 derives every consumed inner seed or seed list with a domain-separated SHA-256 authority keyed by campaign, experiment, outer seed, config path, list position, and original value. The resolved config, experiment config, implementation sources, manifest, attempt receipt, and effective seed authority are hash-bound for each executed cell. Experiments whose scientific path is fixed or whose wrapper exposes mechanics only are explicitly non-inferential and cannot vote in candidate-versus-null classification.

Three composite experiments deliberately retain matched within-cell control substreams: P5 varies the data stream while shell, clustering, and frozen-projection indices remain fixed; Y3 varies the data stream while the refiner ensemble indices remain fixed; F12 varies its declared seed list and base seed while split, probe, and shuffle controls retain their fixed internal indices. Their authority therefore supports variation over the declared data/configuration axis, not a claim that every nested random choice was resampled. The exact resolved configs and implementation hashes make this limitation inspectable, and the scientific-fingerprint canary still blocks directional voting if the declared variation has no observable effect.

### 3.3 Per-experiment classifications

Each experiment is classified from its fresh outer-seed manifests:

| Classification | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `stable_candidate_trace` | The experiment defeated its own registered null on at least 80 percent of available qualifying outer seeds. It remains synthetic exploratory evidence. |
| `stable_null` | The registered null was supported on at least 80 percent of qualifying outer seeds. |
| `mixed_or_seed_sensitive` | Neither direction reached the frozen 80 percent threshold. No promotion or architecture dependency is allowed. |
| `descriptive_only` | The experiment lacks an exhaustive boolean `null_supported` value across at least 20 effective inferential observations. This is an instrumentation or contract limitation, not a positive result. |
| `descriptive_seed_invariant` | Effective seed authorities are distinct, but the scientific metric fingerprint is invariant across the frozen five-seed canary. The row is valid descriptive evidence but cannot vote directionally. |
| `descriptive_seed_adapter_unverified` | Effective seed authority, evidence class, execution count, or canary structure does not match the frozen policy. The row is quarantined. |
| `descriptive_fixed_case` | The experiment is an intentional single fixed case. It is reported once and cannot be replicated by repeating its receipt. |
| `mechanics_noninferential` | The wrapper validates execution mechanics only. Any internal `null_supported` field is ignored for scientific voting. |

The aggregate records boolean rates, a non-promotional 95 percent Wilson interval for the registered null direction, and numeric mean, population standard deviation, minimum, and maximum for finite scalar metrics flattened from the experiment manifests. It also records each experiment's frozen contract, effective observation count, distinct authority count, variation canary, capability-pack summaries, retries, wall time, peak worker RSS where available, and manifest bytes.

The corpus can mark all 128 experiments complete only when every varied experiment has all 24 valid effective authorities and each fixed or mechanics row has its exact single reference execution. The independent verifier is stricter: every seed, cell, manifest, resolved config, attempt receipt, implementation authority, execute-once reference, experiment summary, and corpus seal must match an independent reconstruction. It separately rejects plan, seed-authority, evidence-class, attempt, classification, seed-order, and promotion mutations. The intended final verified corpus therefore exposes all 3,072 logical cells while charging exactly 3,003 real executions before retries.

### 3.4 What the local corpus can and cannot answer

It can answer:

- Which prior programmatic directions are stable across substantially more outer seeds.
- Which effects are fragile, mixed, or missing an explicit null contract.
- Which capability packs tag several recurring candidate traces from distinct implementation-source families, without treating tag overlap as independence or complementarity.
- Which classes repeatedly favor cheap specialized controls over mixtures or loops.
- Where the next mechanistic bed should be built and where further scaling is unwarranted.

It cannot by itself answer:

- Whether ESCS perspectives cooperate in one integrated runtime.
- Whether an actor selected by name has competence on a new task.
- Whether event-driven activation preserves natural-task utility.
- Whether the system has general intelligence, understanding, consciousness, or biological equivalence.
- Whether a programmatic candidate transfers to natural data or an independent generator family.
- Whether runtime energy or latency improves unless separately metered.

## 6. Unified controls and metrics

### 6.1 Controls required across applicable mechanism epochs

Every mechanism should face the strongest relevant subset of this common control library:

1. Best single actor.
2. Cheapest eligible actor.
3. Full or all-on activation.
4. Exact-rate random activation.
5. Periodic activation.
6. Shuffled per-actor schedule preserving exact call counts.
7. Whole-coalition multiset shuffle.
8. Fixed empirical competence map.
9. Learned dispatcher.
10. Evaluator-only oracle dispatcher, permanently nonpromotable.
11. Equal-compute homogeneous model or actor bank.
12. Equal-or-greater-work recurrent control where temporal state is relevant.
13. Coalition with and without verification.
14. Verify-all, verify-none, random rate-matched, disagreement-triggered, and oracle verification.
15. Actor, message, memory, simulator, and topology lesions.
16. Wrong-message, stale-message, shuffled-rollout, action-blind, and action-shuffled controls.
17. Fresh initialization, restart-at-current-position, spare capacity, and full retraining for adaptation.
18. Noisy-TV and irreducible-surprise controls for any novelty, uncertainty, or event trigger.
19. Random initialization and conventional engineered baselines for representational or biologically named mechanisms.
20. Negative leakage controls, structural hashes, and evaluator-boundary mutations.

No single task needs every control. The preregistration must explain which controls apply and why an omitted stronger control is genuinely irrelevant.

### 6.2 Outcome metrics

The campaign keeps endpoint families separate rather than collapsing them into one score:

| Family | Required examples |
| --- | --- |
| Task quality | Accuracy, return, goal success, exact proof or action sequence, intervention ranking, calibrated abstention, task-specific utility. |
| Complementarity | Per-actor niche gain, unique wins, oracle-routing headroom, coalition synergy over best single, marginal contribution, redundancy. |
| Event formation | Consequential-event precision and recall, weighted misses, detection delay, nuisance activation, deadline misses, queue stability. |
| Planning and simulation | One-step prediction, horizon calibration, counterfactual ranking, plan validity, replans, action value, realized success. |
| Memory | Retrieval precision, provenance, stale-memory harm, revision and deletion correctness, projection rebuild, future decision utility. |
| Continual learning | Acquisition, backward transfer, retention, future first-window learning, recurrence recovery, update count, replay bytes. |
| Uncertainty | NLL, Brier, ECE, adaptive and classwise ECE, risk-coverage, AURC, OOD AUROC/AUPRC, abstention utility. |
| Verification | Corrected errors, introduced errors, correction per unit compute, disagreement selectivity, verification regret. |
| Cooperation | Message causal effect, synergy, bytes, link marginal value, delay cost, lesion and Byzantine/noisy-actor robustness. |
| Robustness | Shift, poison, retraction, deletion, schema change, event storm, lesion, restoration, and neutral-label permutation sensitivity. |
| Interpretability | Exact event, source, branch, actor, message, memory, simulation, topology, commitment, and consequence lineage. |

### 6.3 Resource metrics

Every claim about efficiency requires the full applicable resource vector:

- Abstract operations by component.
- Actor executions, model calls, decoder calls, updates, and active multiply-accumulates where measurable.
- Message and serialized bytes.
- Current retained state bytes and retained byte-time.
- Replay, archive, deletion, compaction, failed candidate, retry, and receipt work.
- CPU time, wall time, peak resident memory, and accelerator allocation when available.
- Queue depth, deadline misses, pauses, resumes, and work replayed after the last checkpoint.
- Energy only when directly measured with a documented boundary. Abstract work must not be relabeled as joules.

Primary efficiency reports should show a Pareto surface over utility, work, latency, state, and communication. A weighted scalar may be included for sensitivity analysis, but it cannot replace the component ledger.

### 6.4 Unified future task record

Future local mechanism and external benchmark epochs should normalize each episode or case into a record containing:

- Campaign, epoch, generator, code, config, schema, seed, and structural identities.
- Capability tags, task family, context, difficulty, shift, split, and contamination boundary.
- Input modalities, action space, deadline, exact oracle, and event labels.
- Eligible and activated actors, abstentions, arbitration scores, and activation reasons.
- Typed messages, encoded bytes, provenance, age, lesions, and restorations.
- Memory reads, writes, revisions, deletions, consolidation, and rebuild operations.
- Simulation branches, rollouts, counterfactuals, commitments, actions, and consequences.
- Quality, calibration, causal, robustness, communication, and resource endpoints.

The current 24-seed local corpus predates this unified episode schema and aggregates existing experiment manifests. It is the discovery census that tells the project which mechanisms deserve conversion into this stricter substrate-level protocol.

## 7. Three non-interchangeable gates

### 7.1 Gate R: low-cost registry admission

The registry accepts breadth while preserving evidence status. A candidate may enter as an inert slot when:

- Its interface, effect boundary, trigger authority, and resource dimensions are explicit.
- Its source evidence and exact scope are recorded.
- Its implementation is bounded and deterministic enough for shadow use, or it is marked as a stub.
- It cannot silently gain factual, activation, or promotion authority.
- Nulls remain controls, failures remain excluded, and blocked mechanisms remain non-claiming stubs.

Gate R permits mechanics, bounded toy positives, null controls, pending candidates, and research stubs to coexist. It does not require a global positive effect and does not activate anything.

### 7.2 Gate A: selective runtime or shadow activation

Activation is much stricter than registry admission. It requires:

- A reproducible positive niche on visible information.
- Off-ceiling task difficulty and positive oracle headroom.
- Bounded harm or reliable abstention outside the niche.
- Measured expected value of computation after trigger, communication, state, and verification cost.
- Relevant matched controls and exact-rate schedules.
- No sole reliance on raw novelty, uncertainty, surprise, entropy, or a semantic evaluator label.
- A new content-addressed assembly revision naming the exact authorized scope.

An actor may be active in one context and inert everywhere else. Activation authority is an edge between an actor and a validated context, not a permanent property of the actor's name.

### 7.3 Gate P: strict scientific promotion

Promotion requires all applicable conditions from the one-shot campaign plus Generation 1 confirmation:

- Independent or materially different generator families, or natural/session-disjoint data where the claim needs them.
- Frozen train, selection, test, and fresh verification boundaries.
- Strongest matched, tuned, random, shuffled, oracle, simpler, and equal-compute controls.
- Registered effect sizes and confidence intervals clearing a nonzero SESOI.
- Disjoint fresh replication and an independent verifier, preferably through a separate implementation.
- Full lifecycle and empirical resource measurements.
- Noisy-TV, poison, revision, deletion, schema, storm, lesion, restoration, abstention, and relevant adversarial tests.
- Demonstrated action or intervention benefit for planning, prediction, causal, or simulation claims.
- Wording bounded to the tested population, task families, substrates, horizon, and interventions.

The immediate corpus is permanently below Gate P. A stable candidate trace is a map coordinate, not a promoted mechanism.

## 8. Null-safe routing and injectability

### 8.1 Terminal routes

Every scientific epoch must publish one of these terminal interpretations:

| Route | Meaning | Required next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `positive` | All registered criteria pass on a valid bed. | Advance only to the exact bounded next node. |
| `null` | The bed is valid and a control matches or wins. | Retain the control, retire or narrow the candidate, and continue elsewhere in the DAG. |
| `invalid_bed` | Complementarity, difficulty, oracle headroom, task validity, or leakage boundary makes inference impossible. | Repair or replace the bed under a new authority. Never count it as a mechanism null. |
| `failed` | Code, schema, identity, accounting, replay, or verification failed. | Repair and rerun the same scientific question under a new sealed implementation. |
| `blocked` | Required rights, artifact, authority, implementation, or external state is absent. | Wait or pursue independent safe work. Do not weaken the prerequisite. |
| `mixed_or_seed_sensitive` | Direction varies across outer seeds. | No promotion. Add a targeted fresh epoch or identify the moderating context. |
| `descriptive_only` | The experiment lacks a directional null surface. | Improve instrumentation before using it as an architectural dependency. |

Nulls are productive terminal results. The campaign should continue to independent branches, update the control registry, and avoid spending larger compute on a killed premise.

### 8.2 Injection contract

The detached workflow may accept later work without mutating an active or completed epoch. Every data-producing injection is a new child epoch containing:

- A unique epoch ID and parent corpus digest.
- Exact code, config, generator, dependency, and license identities.
- A declared task schema and capability-pack mapping.
- New disjoint seeds or a seed-commitment digest.
- Expected CPU, accelerator, memory, disk, wall, and network requirements.
- Explicit controls, null, SESOI, output paths, retry semantics, and terminal routes.
- A statement of whether it is discovery, mechanism, confirmation, or promotion evidence.
- A separate aggregate and verifier artifact.

A read-only derived-analysis injection does not invent new empirical cells merely to satisfy the data-epoch shape. It instead depends on the completed source aggregate and verifier, binds their exact bytes when it executes, declares every scientific boundary it cannot test, and emits its own sealed result plus an independent verifier. The accepted `inj-000001-bc8051b78fba` child follows this second form: `g1_evidence_synthesis` depends on `g1_empirical_report`, and `g1_evidence_synthesis_verify` depends on the synthesis. It adds no seeds, experiment classes, or activation authority.

An injection may add an experiment family, independent generator, metric, actor implementation, or confirmation test. It may not change the 24 local seeds, 128-class set, stability thresholds, completed manifests, or interpretation of the immediate corpus. If a new insight changes the question, it creates a new epoch rather than editing history.

### 9.3 Contamination rules for future packs

- Generate claim-bearing cases only after code and config freeze.
- Commit the seed set or seed digest before evaluation and hide exact evaluator seeds from actors.
- Hold out generator families, operators, compositions, and complexity regimes, not just random examples.
- Deduplicate by canonical structural representation.
- Use public fixed evaluation sets once after freeze when possible.
- Label fixed public-set results diagnostic if any pretrained component may have seen them.
- Use two independently implemented generators for high-value causal, symbolic, temporal, or compositional claims.
- Keep task success and mechanism attribution separate. A coalition solving a task does not prove message value, perspective cooperation, or event-driven efficiency without lesions and matched controls.

## 10. Local resource and detached execution model

### 10.1 Host envelope

The planning host at this snapshot is an Apple M3 Ultra Mac Studio with:

- 103,079,215,104 bytes of unified memory, approximately 96 GiB.
- 28 physical and 28 logical CPU cores.
- Approximately 280 GiB free on the active data volume at the snapshot.

These numbers describe the host, not an unconditional allocation. Live memory pressure, swap, thermal state, power, disk reserve, foreground work, and unmanaged heavy processes remain authoritative at each admission boundary.

### 10.2 Immediate corpus process model

The local corpus is CPU-only. Each experiment runs in an isolated subprocess with these thread limits set to one:

- `OMP_NUM_THREADS`
- `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS`
- `MKL_NUM_THREADS`
- `VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS`
- `NUMEXPR_NUM_THREADS`

The configured runner defaults are:

- Sixteen concurrent experiment subprocesses inside one seed orchestrator, subject to the sealed measured canary and live process-tree cap.
- A 1,200-second timeout per experiment attempt.
- A 17,400-second, four-hour-fifty-minute wall budget per seed invocation.
- Atomic per-attempt and per-seed receipts.
- Resume only after rediscovering a valid manifest, resolved config, outer attempt receipt, seed authority, implementation authority, and result tag for the exact effective cell.

Across 3,003 effective executions, the individual timeout ceiling is 1,001 CPU-hours. Perfectly filled 16-way batches would convert that pathological ceiling to about 62.6 wall-hours; the stricter serial seed-capsule ceiling is 116 wall-hours before supervisor-level retries. Real wall time should be much lower for short experiments, but the detached workflow is prepared for retries and several days of operation.

One seed orchestrator at 16 workers is the maximum authorized baseline for this epoch. The resource canary uses the exact sorted batch containing the historically largest worker, records aggregate and per-child RSS, pressure, swap, load, thermal, power, disk, source authority, and safety events, then applies an explicit measured margin. No unmeasured extrapolation above 16 workers is permitted even though the host has 28 cores and 96 GiB.

The final sealed canary completed 16 of 16 workers in 85.266 seconds. Aggregate process-tree RSS peaked at 5,455,855,616 bytes, the maximum individual worker reached 1,999,978,496 bytes, host-available memory never fell below 77,757,972,480 bytes, memory-pressure free capacity remained at least 95 percent, swap remained zero, CPU utilization peaked at 66.8 percent, thermal state remained normal, and AC power remained present. Its frozen rule recommends a 10,000,000,000-byte process-tree declaration for 16 workers: 1.5 times measured aggregate peak plus 1 GB, rounded upward in 2 GB steps. The receipt explicitly forbids extrapolation beyond the measured concurrency.

### 10.3 Admission and coexistence

The existing local throttle principles remain binding:

- One heavy lane maximum.
- No second MPS owner because reliable global per-process Metal allocation is unavailable.
- Never signal or suspend a user-owned or externally owned process.
- Require several healthy samples before launch or resume.
- Stop and retry only the exact scheduler-owned process group on critical memory, disk, swap, thermal, power, identity, or declared process-tree-RSS pressure; never signal an inexact identity.
- Preserve at least the declared 40 GB disk floor plus atomic-write and uncertainty reserve.
- Treat missing telemetry as a refusal, not as proof the host is idle.
- Do not treat the campaign's own intended CPU saturation as foreign load after admission.

Because this corpus is CPU-only, it can fill genuinely idle host gaps while an external queue exists. A known-heavy Hawking child or another project blocks fresh admission; if such work appears after admission, the supervisor records the resource transition, stops only its own exact child group, preserves valid completed attempts, and retries later. It never signals Hawking. Seeds, class scope, timeouts, scientific thresholds, and claim language remain unchanged.

### 10.4 Resume and watchdog behavior

The detached workflow should remain event-driven:

- It checks admission at a seed or retry boundary.
- It acquires an atomic shared local-throttle lane reservation, launches one bounded seed batch, and holds the reservation through exact child termination.
- While that child is live, it samples process-tree RSS plus memory availability and pressure, swap, disk forecast, thermal status, and AC power at the policy interval; every sample updates durable extrema and a bounded event log.
- It writes atomic receipts and does not poll scientific output every few minutes.
- It retries only missing or invalid experiment cells under the same authority.
- It skips verified cells and never overwrites a successful attempt.
- It retries a failed capsule at most three times, then enters a durable failure hold instead of looping indefinitely.
- After a supervisor crash it observes an exact still-live child without signaling it, retries an exact-gone child within the same bound, and enters an integrity hold for an ambiguous identity.
- It aggregates only after the full seed set is complete.
- It verifies only after the aggregate has landed.
- Operator-requested inspection reads the durable status and receipts; inspection is not required for progress.

The operator-facing path is deliberately two-step and non-mutating until submission. A later exploratory capsule is first built against the live queue head, then copied atomically into the governed inbox:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/build_generation1_injection.py \
  --program configs/campaign/generation1_empirical_program_v2.json \
  --capsule-json path/to/exploratory_capsule.json \
  --reason "new disjoint evidence after operator review" \
  --out runs/generation1/injections/request.json

.venv/bin/python scripts/mop_generation1_campaign.py inject \
  --program configs/campaign/generation1_empirical_program_v2.json \
  --request runs/generation1/injections/request.json
```

An active supervisor consumes a valid request only at a capsule boundary. If the base program has already completed, the same sealed supervisor is started again with explicit execution authority; it consumes the new child epoch before evaluating completion. Injection cannot rewrite a completed capsule, seed, threshold, classification, or queue history.

The scientific workflow must not adapt thresholds based on early results. Resource adaptation may change concurrency or pause timing, but it may not change experiments, seeds, controls, classification rules, or claim language within the frozen epoch.

## 12. Decision after the immediate run

The first decision is not whether to turn on all 31 perspectives. It is which bounded successor hypotheses, if any, the 24-seed census supports preregistering. The census cannot reveal context-disjoint actor competence or justify a shadow coalition; those are separate G1-C1 and G1-C2 questions.

If verified source-distinct traces and null-safe constraints identify a worthwhile successor bed, the next high-information sequence is:

1. Validate actor niches on explicit task families.
2. Establish complementarity and oracle-routing headroom.
3. Build X0-R relational and temporal event tests in parallel with memory/plasticity and action-value simulation beds.
4. Train or fit arbitration only after complementarity survives.
5. Add bounded communication, disagreement-triggered verification, contradiction repair, calibration, and abstention.
6. Search finite G0 shadow candidates with all failed work charged.
7. Compare the integrated shadow substrate with best-single, fixed-map, all-on, homogeneous, recurrent, random, shuffled, and oracle organizations.
8. Inject external procedural packs only as separately sealed epochs for independent capabilities and fresh confirmation.

If the corpus mostly returns stable nulls, mixed results, and descriptive-only rows, that is still a complete and valuable outcome. The project should preserve the ESCS chassis as infrastructure, promote the strongest specialized controls, narrow the candidate registry, and build better cognitive task beds before spending more compute on arbitration or topology.

Generation 1 succeeds by producing a trustworthy map of where heterogeneous cognition helps, where it does not, and what causal evidence is still missing. Forming the substrate means discovering and governing those relationships, not merely instantiating more modules.
