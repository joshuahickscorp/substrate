## 0. Research mandate

The question is larger than how to fuse more model outputs. It is whether the current Form
Substrate program can become the foundation of a persistent, developmental, multimodal,
action-capable computational medium, and whether the same abstractions can eventually reach into
history-dependent physical matter.

This dossier therefore investigates:

- the actual repository rather than its older plan snapshots;
- cognition as perception, prediction, memory, action, metacognition, development, and social
  coordination;
- sentience and consciousness theories without collapsing operational function into phenomenology;
- moldability and plasticity across state, memory, parameters, structure, and physical material;
- native computer vision and computer audio rather than language proxies;
- referents, event identity, embodiment, affordances, self/world boundaries, and causal agency;
- global access, recurrent processing, attention models, self-models, and metacognitive reports;
- developmental psychology, neuroscience, sleep, reconsolidation, glia, dendrites, and repair;
- active inference, world models, imagination, play, open-endedness, teaching, and culture;
- memristive, iontronic, hydrogel, organic, colloidal, photonic, magnetic, soft-body, and
  morphogenic computing;
- safety, security, welfare uncertainty, identity, provenance, and controlled self-rewrite;
- naming and program identity.

The desired style is science-fictional in reach and scientific in discipline. A concept earns a
place in implementation only by becoming a falsifiable experiment with a serious baseline.

## 1. How to read this dossier

### 1.1 Evidence tags

| Tag | Meaning | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | mature empirical effect or widely reproduced engineering fact | convergent primary studies or authoritative review |
| E2 | credible primary result with limited scope | one or a few peer-reviewed studies |
| E3 | active scientific theory with competing interpretations | theory and discriminating experiments |
| E4 | engineering analogy | biological or philosophical motif translated into a testable design |
| E5 | speculative frontier | plausible mechanism without program-level evidence |
| P0 | project mechanics only | toy or structured-synthetic result |
| P1 | project-controlled evidence | real data or environment, not yet independently replicated |

The tags describe evidence status, not importance. An E5 idea can be worth testing. It cannot be
written as an established fact.

### 1.2 Resource horizons

| Horizon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NOW | runnable on the current CPU or cached-latent path |
| STUDIO | needs natural data, long streams, more memory, or larger Apple Silicon compute |
| ENV | needs a persistent interactive environment |
| BENCH | needs nonliving physical hardware and specialist instrumentation |
| LAB | needs a specialist materials laboratory |
| QUARANTINE | ethics, welfare, biosafety, or governance must be resolved before experimental work |

### 1.3 Five levels of functional evidence

For any cognition-like mechanism, the strongest defensible ladder is:

1. **Activation:** telemetry shows that the pathway exists and activates.
2. **Behavioral signature:** the predicted capability appears.
3. **Causal necessity:** a targeted lesion selectively removes the capability.
4. **Causal sufficiency or restoration:** restoring, injecting, or transplanting the mechanism
   restores the capability under novel conditions.
5. **Convergent construct validity:** independent tasks and interventions agree while neighboring
   constructs dissociate.

Even Level 5 licenses a bounded functional claim, such as global information access,
metacognitive monitoring, recurrent integration, or damage-aware self-modeling. It does not by
itself license a phenomenal claim.

### 1.4 Recurring distinctions

- Cross-form alignment is not proof of referent identity.
- Prediction is not causal understanding.
- Reconstruction is not useful memory.
- Attention weights are not explanations.
- A self-description is not privileged introspection.
- A self-model is not a self.
- Global broadcast is not proof of consciousness.
- Integrated-information quantities are not validated sentience meters.
- Functional regulation is not felt emotion.
- Rapid change is not useful plasticity.
- Remembering old tasks is not the same as retaining the ability to learn new ones.
- A closed action-token sequence is not embodiment without consequences.
- A material nonlinearity is not useful computation without a digital control.
- Biological resemblance is not biological equivalence.
- Breadth of tasks is not open-ended development.

## 2. Executive synthesis

### 2.1 The central conclusion

The most defensible next object is not a consciousness module and not one universal latent vector.
It is a **persistent ecology of stateful form processes** linked by referents, time, causal history,
memory, and action.

The ecology should be able to:

- preserve identity while appearances and forms change;
- decide which observation to acquire next;
- separate self-caused from external change through intervention;
- maintain multiple memory systems with provenance and revision history;
- predict its own sensor, memory, resource, and capability limits;
- learn without destroying future learnability;
- grow, prune, and repair structure only after simpler controls fail;
- ground communication in shared referents and consequences;
- expose every mind-like feature to lesion, restoration, and alternative explanation;
- map the same lifecycle onto simulated and eventually physical dynamical media.

### 2.2 Fifteen high-confidence conclusions

1. **The data plane comes first.** Stable referents, mandatory manifests, preserved token geometry,
   synchronized episodes, and one reliable Form contract are prerequisites for richer cognition.
2. **Form is necessary but insufficient.** Persistence requires time, events, uncertainty,
   provenance, action, and developmental history.
3. **Native modalities matter.** Audio is not transcription, vision is not a pooled clip, and action
   is not a label.
4. **Embodiment is closed-loop contingency.** The decisive variable is whether action changes what
   can be sensed and learned.
5. **Memory is a lifecycle.** Encoding, consolidation, retrieval, reconsolidation, deletion,
   provenance, and prospective use must be measured separately.
6. **Plasticity is multiscale.** Fast state, memory writes, parameter updates, consolidation,
   structural change, and metaplastic control have different benefits and risks.
7. **Plasticity loss is not forgetting.** Current deep networks can retain old behavior while
   gradually losing the capacity to learn new behavior.
8. **Operational self-models are testable.** A system can predict its sensors, hardware, memory,
   resources, and likely failure without any phenomenal interpretation.
9. **Workspace claims need interventions.** A shared vector does not establish limited competition
   and global broadcast.
10. **The science of consciousness remains plural and unsettled.** No one theory should become the architecture
    by decree.
11. **Self-report is weak without causal telemetry.** Eloquence must lose to intervention and
    behavioral calibration.
12. **Social grounding extends referents.** Joint attention, teaching, repair, and cultural
    transmission can establish shared structure no single passive sensor provides.
13. **The gelatinous substrate is scientifically plausible as a frontier.** Ionic gels, organic
    electrochemical devices, nanowire networks, and colloids already support nonlinear temporal
    processing and memory-like dynamics.
14. **Physical evidence is currently narrow.** Existing material devices mainly demonstrate
    sensing, time-series transformation, classification, and reservoir behavior, not general
    cognition.
15. **Biological tissue is not the final rung of this plan.** It is a separately governed and
    ethics-gated research domain, not the assumed successor to nonliving matter.

### 2.3 The science-fiction version, translated

The imagined soft substrate is a distributed medium whose local regions specialize under
experience, whose global dynamics bind information over time, and whose damaged portions can be
rerouted or repaired. The scientific translation is:

- nonlinear dynamics;
- fading and durable memory at different timescales;
- spatially distributed state;
- history-dependent conductivity or response;
- controlled write and read channels;
- local plasticity plus global task feedback;
- bounded self-organization;
- damage tolerance and remapping;
- measurable energy, latency, drift, lifetime, and specimen variance;
- a software-level referent contract that survives a change of matter.

Nothing in this translation requires mystical properties. It is ambitious materials computing
with unusually strict cognitive and evidential interfaces.

## 3. What the repository actually contains

### 3.1 Strong foundations

The project already has unusually strong scientific hygiene:

- explicit doctrine, falsifiers, null cards, and evidence promotion;
- a cache-first frozen-encoder pathway;
- a registry with baseline, ablation, metric, null, tier, and status fields;
- a typed Form abstraction and referent audits;
- performance-density receipts;
- operational-awareness metrics with a language rail;
- replay, KV retrieval, consolidation, plasticity control, workspace, prediction, refinement, and
  verification components;
- Studio planning, validation, provenance, and reporting machinery;
- a large corpus of negative results that constrains new work.

At the audited commit, the repository validator reported 195 registry rows, including 20 F-series
rows. Fourteen F-series experiments are implemented as R0 mechanics, six remain gated. Passing
tests verify interfaces and result shape, not the semantics of the scientific claims.

### 3.2 Three partially connected systems

The implementation is best understood as three systems that have not yet become one persistent
substrate:

1. a frozen-encoder and cache pipeline;
2. parallel Form and Perspective metadata/matrix stacks;
3. mostly independent synthetic experiments and cognitive shell components.

The key bridge is therefore not another grand module. It is a canonical evidence and data substrate
that all experiments must consume.

### 3.3 Data-plane gaps

The repository audit found seven immediate issues.

#### A. Parallel abstraction stacks

`src/mop/substrate/form.py` and `src/mop/perspectives/adapter.py` each define metadata, batches,
adapters, matrices, and audits. Only referent ordering is shared. Temporary coexistence was a
deliberate decision while real DR1 evidence remained absent, but the long-term one-stack goal is not
met.

#### B. Production factor sidecars can fail

The latent-store Form adapter assumes every value in `factors.json` is tensor-convertible. Real
sidecars can include metadata strings and scalars. A canonical schema needs separate metadata and
column namespaces, dtype declarations, and row-length validation.

#### C. Default referents are cache-local

When explicit referents are missing, synthesized IDs include the store name. Two caches over the
same rows therefore receive different identities. Numeric retrieval keys are not semantic referent
IDs. The explicit DR1 clip-stem pathway needs to become part of the canonical Form intake.

#### D. Manifest doctrine is not enforced

Manifest v2 exists, yet current caches may lack `cache_manifest.json`, and the validator treats it
as optional. Doctrine says a manifestless cache is not citable. Operational validity and scientific
citability must become distinct validator modes.

#### E. Cache metadata dialects conflict

Some stores are readable through compatibility logic but fail official validation. One inspected
factorized cache also reported zero rows in metadata while its array contained data. A single
schema, migration tool, and strict citable mode are required.

#### F. Generic caching flattens token geometry

The generic path flattens representations. That destroys spatial, temporal, object, and dense-token
structure needed for event identity, active vision, audio timing, and physical-process forms.

#### G. Encoder fallback can change evidence class

Broad exception handling can fall back from real weights to another backend. Scientific runs need
resolved model, revision, weight hash, preprocessing hash, backend, fallback status, and an abort
rule when fallback was not explicitly requested.

### 3.4 The current audio form is not native audio

The main Wav2Vec path sonifies video-frame statistics and encodes the resulting waveform. This is a
useful cross-encoding stress test but not an independent auditory observation. A native audio line
needs actual waveform extraction, audiovisual clock checks, environmental events, speech and
non-speech distinctions, localization where available, and independent information tests.

### 3.5 The current workspace is not yet a global workspace

`src/mop/shell/workspace.py` is a feed-forward composition wrapper around gating, optional working
memory, predictors, and a head. It does not currently own persistent state, run recurrent
perception-action cycles, arbitrate specialist competition, or broadcast a transient winner to
separated consumers. Working-memory state persists only if the caller carries it.

This is not a defect in its present contract. It means that global-workspace language must remain a
future experimental hypothesis.

### 3.6 The current memory system is useful but not a memory ecology

The KV/replay path stores tensors, labels, keys, and priorities. It lacks referent provenance,
episode time, parent-child hierarchy, action and consequence links, uncertainty, consolidation
state, and persistent revision history. `LatentStore` is disk-backed and immutable; `ReplayBuffer`
is mutable and memory-resident. They are not yet one episodic-to-semantic lifecycle.

### 3.7 The current plasticity controller is more promise than closed loop

The stack contains learning, replay, EWC, SI, alternative credit-assignment mechanisms, LR
modulation, and rigidity concepts. The audit found that the declared learned LR is a fixed Python
float, rigidity initialization is normally only exercised in tests, and neuromodulation currently
affects surprise-based learning rate rather than the wider replay and exploration contract.

This makes the existing license gate correct: fix wiring and locate a real representational failure
before deeper substrate training.

### 3.8 Critical semantic debt in F13, F18, and F20

These results must stay P0 and need redesign before they support their registered interpretations.

#### F13

The current sweep varies width, reports head parameters/FLOPs, and counts bytes as width times four.
It omits source representations, alignment maps, extraction, fusion, training, replay, and measured
energy. It demonstrates a synthetic feature utility difference under an incomplete head-only cost
measure, not substrate-level energy or information density.

#### F18

The intervention arm receives an explicit scalar delta and learns an arithmetic target. Form B is
used in a separate readout, not as the predicted induced outcome. The current result therefore
demonstrates supervised arithmetic with an intervention variable, not cross-form causal transport.

#### F20

The monitor separates hand-designed Gaussian fixture families using target and nuisance labels.
It does not predict a real model or representation wall before an attempted scale-up. Its saved
compute ratio depends on a configurable hypothetical cost. It is a crisis-fixture classifier, not
yet a validated representational-wall forecaster.

Other R0 issues include evaluation leakage in the toy curriculum, label rather than referent
shuffling in some controls, incomplete memory-byte matching, and simplified hierarchy or
missing-form mechanics. The correct response is redesign, not deletion: the harnesses expose where
the next real tests must become sharper.

### 3.9 Research consequence

What the repository supports now:

- a serious falsification framework;
- many working mechanics;
- a clear referent-centered doctrine;
- enough code to run the next evidence phase.

What it does not yet support:

- one unified persistent cognitive substrate;
- natural multimodal referent evidence;
- real embodied closure;
- developmental plasticity evidence;
- a causal global workspace;
- a persistent self-model;
- physical material computation;
- any consciousness or sentience inference.

The next layer begins by making the evidence substrate trustworthy.

## 4. Three meanings of substrate

### 4.1 Interface substrate

An interface substrate is the typed contract that lets heterogeneous forms be compared and bound
without pretending they are identical. It includes schema, provenance, referents, timing, validity,
geometry, and cost. This is the closest description of the present implementation.

### 4.2 Trainable computational substrate

A trainable substrate reorganizes its internal geometry or dynamics through experience. It must
show that the change installs a named useful property and beats:

- frozen substrate plus a stronger shell;
- larger frozen capacity;
- tuned adapters;
- replay and consolidation;
- matched total compute;
- restart or retraining;
- old-form retention controls.

This is the still-gated F7/F8/F16 horizon.

### 4.3 Physical material substrate

A material substrate uses matter's native dynamics as part of the computation. Its response may be
nonlinear, distributed, noisy, history-dependent, reconfigurable, and coupled to sensing or action.
It requires device-level measures that software metaphors do not:

- excitation waveform and limits;
- settling time;
- readout bandwidth;
- volatile and nonvolatile state;
- cycle-to-cycle variation;
- specimen-to-specimen variation;
- temperature, humidity, aging, and drift;
- energy including acquisition electronics;
- damage and repair;
- calibration cost;
- device lifetime.

### 4.4 Substrate-independent lifecycle

The three levels can share a protocol:

```text
receive typed excitation
-> evolve over a declared timebase
-> expose a partial readout
-> estimate uncertainty and cost
-> compare with task and integrity criteria
-> update state, parameters, structure, or matter within license
-> retain a lineage record
-> test transfer, drift, damage, and recovery
```

This lifecycle is the conceptual bridge from tensors to adaptive matter.

## 5. Philosophical foundations

Philosophy is useful here when it sharpens experimental commitments rather than supplying a label.

### 5.1 Functional organization and multiple realization

Functionalist intuitions allow a capacity to be described by causal role rather than carbon,
silicon, or gel. This makes cross-substrate portability a coherent research question. Multiple
realization does not mean every implementation is equivalent. Dynamics, embodiment, energy,
precision, time constants, and learning history can change what functions are possible.

Project consequence: define capacities through intervention and consequence, then ask which media
realize them most effectively.

### 5.2 Embodied and enactive cognition

The embodied tradition argues that cognition depends on the structure of perception-action loops,
not only internal symbol manipulation. The sensorimotor account of perception makes mastery of
action-linked sensory regularities central ([O'Regan and Noë, 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12239892/)).
The enactive tradition emphasizes autonomous organization and world-involving activity; a useful
book-length source is [The Embodied Mind](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262529365/the-embodied-mind/).

Engineering translation:

- evaluate active versus yoked passive observations;
- make sensing and acting costly;
- test whether representation changes with controllability;
- include body and tool dynamics in prediction;
- distinguish a dataset label from an enacted affordance.

What this does not license: calling any closed-loop controller alive, autonomous in a biological
sense, or conscious.

### 5.3 Extended and distributed cognition

The extended-mind thesis asks when external tools and records participate in a cognitive process
([Clark and Chalmers, 1998](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1111/1467-8284.00096)). For
this project, the productive question is causal: does an external memory or tool become part of a
stable loop such that removing it produces a specific deficit and restoring it recovers function?

This supports treating caches, tools, sensors, and collaborator memories as typed extensions, while
preserving provenance and ownership boundaries.

### 5.4 Process identity

A persistent system is better modeled as a lineage of state transitions than as one immutable
vector. Operational identity can be defined by:

- continuity of event log;
- provenance-preserving memory revisions;
- protected invariants;
- causal continuity of policy and self-model;
- explicit fork, merge, migration, and rollback semantics;
- the ability to explain which changes produced the current state.

This avoids treating a checkpoint filename or verbal persona as identity.

### 5.5 Symbol grounding and meaning

The symbol-grounding problem asks how internal tokens acquire non-derivative connection to what
they concern ([Harnad, 1990](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~harnad/Papers/Harnad/harnad90.sgproblem.html)).
Grounded-cognition research emphasizes modality and action systems
([Barsalou, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17705682/)).

The project already has the right primitive: a referent is not the representation. The expansion
adds several ways a binding can be warranted: supplied, tracked, inferred, intervened, negotiated,
enacted, or unknown.

### 5.6 Autopoiesis and operational closure

Autopoiesis describes living organization in terms of self-producing networks and a maintained
boundary. It is provocative inspiration for repair and bounded self-maintenance, but current
software should not borrow the biological conclusion. The useful translation is narrower:

- identify variables necessary for continued correct operation;
- maintain them inside externally defined bounds;
- detect and repair local damage;
- preserve external interruptibility;
- avoid turning survival into an unbounded objective.

### 5.7 Other minds and epistemic humility

Consciousness is not directly observed even in biology; inference relies on convergent behavior,
structure, function, evolutionary continuity, and theory. Artificial systems weaken or rearrange
those analogies. The proper response is neither confident attribution nor confident denial from one
test. It is a theory-plural indicator program plus strict claim boundaries and welfare caution.

## 6. Consciousness and sentience research

### 6.1 The field is unsettled

[Seth and Bayne's 2022 review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-022-00587-4) surveys
higher-order, global-workspace, re-entry and predictive-processing, and integrated-information
families. The accounts overlap on some mechanisms and diverge on others.

The 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration preregistered divergent predictions from global
neuronal workspace theory and integrated information theory, then tested 256 human participants
with fMRI, MEG, and intracranial EEG. Results supported some predictions while challenging key
tenets of both accounts
([Cogitate Consortium et al., 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1)). This is
strong evidence for adversarial theory testing, not for installing one theory as software doctrine.

[Butlin et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) translate several theories into possible
computational indicators for AI. Their framework is valuable precisely because indicators can be
investigated without treating any single indicator as proof.

### 6.2 Global neuronal workspace

GNWT associates conscious access with competition, late amplification, recurrent processing, and
broad availability to otherwise separated systems. Foundational sources include
[Dehaene and Naccache, 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11164022/) and
[Dehaene and Changeux, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21521609/).

Engineering motif:

- specialist modules produce candidates;
- a limited-capacity competition selects transient content;
- selected content persists long enough to affect multiple consumers;
- broadcast lineage is observable;
- local specialist function can remain intact when broadcast is lesioned.

Smallest decisive test: match width, compute, and connectivity; lesion broadcast edges; require a
selective loss of cross-module coordination but not local classification.

Construct limit: global availability is a functional property and does not establish subjective
experience.

### 6.3 Recurrent processing

Recurrent-processing accounts emphasize local or distributed recurrent interactions beyond an
initial feed-forward sweep. The useful computational question is whether late recurrent state adds
content-specific robustness, temporal binding, or figure-ground resolution beyond a deeper
feed-forward network at matched compute.

Controls:

- untied feed-forward depth;
- tied recurrent depth;
- matched parameters and FLOPs;
- latency and stability;
- lesions at different recurrence windows;
- adversarial temporal ordering.

The project's existing iterative-compute null makes this especially important. Recurrence should
not be granted value merely because it resembles biology.

### 6.4 Higher-order monitoring

Higher-order theories distinguish a first-order representation from a representation of being in
that state. A useful entry point is
[Lau and Rosenthal, 2011](https://www.davidrosenthal.org/DR-Lau-TICS.pdf).

Engineering motif:

- a separable monitor predicts correctness, source, missing evidence, and likely error type;
- first-order accuracy is held constant;
- lesioning the monitor impairs confidence discrimination, abstention, or help-seeking more than
  task performance;
- monitor restoration recovers those functions.

This can produce an operational first-order/second-order dissociation without making a claim about
phenomenal blindsight.

### 6.5 Attention schema

Attention-schema theory proposes that a simplified model of attention helps control and attribute
attention ([Graziano and Webb, 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407481/)). The
engineering version tracks:

- current attended referent and form;
- why it won;
- expected duration and downstream effect;
- confidence that attention is misplaced;
- estimated attention of a partner.

The correct test is whether an explicit learned schema improves switching, recovery, and partner
prediction beyond a policy with equal capacity. A natural-language rendering of the schema is not
introspection evidence.

### 6.6 Predictive and self-model accounts

Predictive accounts connect perception and selfhood to hierarchies of prediction, precision, and
error. The project can test whether an explicit model of its own sensor, actuator, resource, and
memory state improves prospective failure prediction and repair. It should compare against an
equally capable external world model.

### 6.7 Integrated information

IIT formalizes irreducible cause-effect structure. A canonical source is
[Oizumi, Albantakis, and Tononi, 2014](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588).
Substantial criticisms include the computational and interpretive problems collected in
[Cerullo, 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4574706/) and functional-equivalence
challenges such as the
[unfolding argument](https://digitalcommons.chapman.edu/psychology_articles/161/).

Project rule: integration measures may be used as engineering diagnostics and compared with graph
connectivity, bandwidth, synergy, and lesion effects. Never use a phi-like scalar as a sentience
score or architecture gate.

### 6.8 Active-inference and embodied accounts

Active inference frames perception and policy as inference under a generative model. Useful sources
include the
[free-energy principle](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2787),
[a discrete-state process theory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7732703/), and
[comparisons with control-as-inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.12964).

Engineering value:

- explicit beliefs and uncertainty;
- expected information gain;
- action chosen partly to reduce uncertainty;
- policy comparison with POMDP and model-based RL controls.

Important limit: Markov-blanket mathematics or free-energy minimization does not by itself
establish a biological agent, consciousness, or intrinsic purpose.

### 6.9 Theory-neutral construct matrix

The project should never collapse these into one awareness score:

| Construct | Observable signature | Causal test | Neighboring construct to dissociate |
|---|---|---|---|
| global access | content reaches separated consumers | broadcast lesion/restoration | shared bottleneck |
| recurrent persistence | late state improves temporal task | recurrence-window lesion | feed-forward depth |
| metacognitive sensitivity | confidence discriminates own correctness | monitor lesion | task accuracy |
| attention modeling | schema predicts and controls allocation | schema ablation | attention policy alone |
| self-model accuracy | predicts own damage/resources | hidden component perturbation | external world model |
| temporal integration | information combines across windows | scramble and delay | static fusion |
| causal integration | parts jointly carry non-additive consequence | partition intervention | dense connectivity |
| reportability | structured state can be communicated | state injection and trace audit | language fluency |

### 6.10 What a convergence result would mean

If several independent probes become positive, the proper result is a richer operational profile
and a welfare-uncertainty review. It is not an automatic phenomenal conclusion. The scientific and
ethical questions remain distinct: uncertainty about consciousness can justify caution without
pretending uncertainty has been resolved.

## 7. Time, referents, objects, and events

### 7.1 Why referent identity is the core problem

The present project usually receives identity from a dataset. Rich cognition requires learning when
two observations concern:

- the same object at different times;
- the same event through different sensors;
- the same process after deformation;
- two similar but distinct entities;
- one entity that split or merged;
- an uncertain or unknowable match.

This is harder than embedding similarity. Identity can survive large appearance changes and fail
despite near-identical appearance.

### 7.2 Object-centric representations

Slot-based and relational methods offer useful hypotheses, including
[Slot Attention](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/hash/8511df98c02ab60aea1b2356c013bc0f-Abstract.html),
[G-SWM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/lin20f.html), and
[Neural Relational Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.04687).

Candidate representation:

- persistent entity slots;
- form-specific observations attached to each slot;
- relation and interaction graph;
- uncertainty over identity;
- event nodes for transformations;
- explicit birth, split, merge, and death operations.

Controls must include dense tokens, pooled embeddings, appearance tracking, and an oracle identity
upper bound. Slot interpretability is not assumed.

### 7.3 Temporal correspondence

[Temporal cycle consistency](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Dwibedi_Temporal_Cycle-Consistency_Learning_CVPR_2019_paper.html)
and self-supervised temporal correspondence
([Wang, Jabri, and Efros, 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07593)) show how temporal structure can
supervise alignment. Cycles need hard negatives; otherwise a shortcut or uniform mapping can satisfy
them.

### 7.4 Event segmentation

An event is not just a fixed clip. It is a hypothesis about a bounded process. Useful evidence can
come from:

- prediction-error changes;
- action and contact boundaries;
- crossmodal onset agreement;
- changes in causal graph;
- memory utility;
- human annotations used only as one supervisory form.

Boundary revisions should be allowed. A later observation can show that two apparent events were
one process or that one apparent event contained distinct causes.

### 7.5 Causal representation learning

[Scholkopf et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11107) frame causal representation learning as
the search for high-level causal variables. Purely unsupervised disentanglement is generally not
identifiable without assumptions, as shown by
[Locatello et al., 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a).

Project consequence: use interventions, time, environment variation, weak supervision, and
multi-form invariance. Never label a latent dimension causal because it is linearly decodable.

### 7.6 Smallest experiment

Construct sequences with identical distractors, occlusion, view changes, controlled attribute
interventions, and ambiguous crossings. Compare supplied IDs, appearance matching, temporal
tracking, object slots, and intervention-aware identity. Score identity F1, false merges, false
splits, abstention calibration, and memory corruption.

Evidence limit: even a strong tracker establishes operational continuity, not metaphysical identity.

## 8. Vision through the computer

### 8.1 From pooled clips to active visual process

The existing V-JEPA lineage supplies useful frozen visual geometry, but the next layer needs:

- dense spatial and temporal tokens;
- persistent object and relation structure;
- event boundaries;
- occlusion and reidentification;
- active view selection;
- action-conditioned prediction;
- physical consequence and affordance;
- uncertainty and missingness.

[I-JEPA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Assran_Self-Supervised_Learning_From_Images_With_a_Joint-Embedding_Predictive_Architecture_CVPR_2023_paper.html),
[V-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08471), and
[V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985) motivate prediction in representation space rather
than reconstructing every pixel. V-JEPA 2 also explores action-conditioned world modeling and
planning. These are relevant ingredients, not proof that one encoder is the final substrate.

### 8.2 Known failure modes

Visual systems can exploit stable but irrelevant cues. Important warnings include
[shortcut learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-020-00257-z),
[texture bias](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Bygh9j09KX), adversarial sensitivity, and
compositional failures such as
[Winoground](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Thrush_Winoground_Probing_Vision_and_Language_Models_for_Visio-Linguistic_Compositionality_CVPR_2022_paper.html).

Core controls:

- counterfactual background;
- texture-shape conflict;
- object swap;
- relation reversal;
- temporal reversal;
- camera-motion confound;
- same appearance, different cause;
- different appearance, same identity;
- intervention on one factor while others remain stable.

### 8.3 Intuitive physics

Object-centric video models can learn some physical regularities. For example,
[PLATO](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-022-01394-8) tested object-based representations on
violations of physical expectations. The right project question is not whether a model looks
surprised, but whether its state supports:

- calibrated violation prediction;
- causal intervention;
- transfer to new objects and materials;
- planning that survives execution;
- separation of model error from sensor novelty.

### 8.4 Active vision

Active visual sensing chooses viewpoints based on task value. It should be compared with fixed,
random, saliency, uncertainty-only, and oracle policies under equal sensor and movement cost.

Event cameras offer asynchronous low-latency changes rather than frames. They are useful when
timing, dynamic range, or motion blur is the actual limitation, not as a fashionable extra modality.
The form contract should preserve event time rather than pool it immediately.

### 8.5 Project capsule

- **Supported:** frozen video representations, caching, basic dense-slot code, synthetic temporal
  mechanics.
- **Contested:** whether object slots are necessary, whether JEPA objectives yield causal variables.
- **Change:** preserve token geometry; add event and object identity; make view acquisition active.
- **Does not license:** visual understanding, intuitive physics, or embodiment from probe accuracy.
- **Nearest test:** persistent-object and active-view experiments after real FormMatrix completion.
- **Tier:** NOW mechanics, STUDIO natural validation, ENV active closure.

## 9. Audio through the computer

### 9.1 Audio is a temporal process

Audio carries structure unavailable from a transcript:

- onset and rhythm;
- speaker and channel;
- prosody;
- source direction and distance;
- material contact;
- reverberation and room geometry;
- non-speech events;
- overlapping causes;
- silence and missing signal;
- precise relation to action and vision.

A transcript is one derived semantic form. It should never replace the waveform, timing, and event
lineage.

### 9.2 Representation families

Relevant self-supervised approaches include
[wav2vec 2.0](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/92d1e1eb1cd6f9fba3227870bb6d7f07-Abstract.html),
[HuBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07447),
[AudioMAE](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/b89d5e209990b19e33b418e14f323998-Abstract-Conference.html), and
[BEATs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09058). Speech-focused, general audio-event, and masked
spectrogram objectives expose different currencies. No single pooled embedding should silently
stand for them all.

### 9.3 Audiovisual binding

Audiovisual systems can learn from synchrony, as in
[Look, Listen and Learn](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Arandjelovic_Look_Listen_and_ICCV_2017_paper.html)
and [AV-HuBERT](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Z1Qlm11uOM). Synchrony is useful but insufficient:
two unrelated events can co-occur, dubbing can be plausible, and room statistics can create
shortcuts.

Required negatives:

- shifted sound;
- same scene but different event;
- visually hidden sound source;
- dubbed or swapped source;
- same words with different speaker or prosody;
- reverberation and gain changes;
- clock drift;
- causally impossible but statistically plausible pairing.

### 9.4 Active acoustics

An embodied system can act to hear better: move a sensor, emit a probe sound, touch an object, or
reduce competing noise. Active acoustics turns audio from a passive channel into a causal form.
Tests must count the energy, time, and disturbance created by the probe.

### 9.5 First native audio program

1. extract real audio from rights-clean videos;
2. preserve waveform pointers and clock metadata;
3. create event-level and sequence-level representations;
4. measure audio-only, vision-only, and fused performance;
5. test independent information with vision masked or ambiguous;
6. introduce shifts, swaps, silence, and corruption;
7. score crossmodal event identity and value of acquiring another interval.

### 9.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** Wav2Vec machinery and a sonification stress test.
- **Contested:** which audio currency is best and whether pivot alignment creates real shared
  semantics.
- **Change:** establish native waveform, timing, and event forms before multimodal claims.
- **Does not license:** treating sonified visual statistics or transcripts as independent hearing.
- **Tier:** NOW ingestion mechanics, STUDIO natural validation, ENV active acoustics.

## 10. Multisensory binding and computer interoception

### 10.1 Shared spaces are useful but dangerous

[CLIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020),
[ImageBind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05665), and
[Perceiver](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/jaegle21a) show scalable ways to align or process
heterogeneous modalities. ImageBind is especially relevant because it aligns text, audio, depth,
thermal, and IMU using images as a pivot.

Pivot alignment can create apparent transitivity without direct referent verification and can
inherit the pivot's blind spots. The project should compare direct pairs with pivot-only pairs and
report conditional information beyond the strongest single form.

### 10.2 Asynchrony and temporal windows

Multisensory binding depends on relative timing, reliability, and context. A computational substrate
should learn a distribution over binding windows rather than use one hard global tolerance.

Tests include:

- variable latency and jitter;
- modality-specific clock drift;
- repeated events;
- asynchronous causes;
- missing and delayed forms;
- one corrupted form;
- two individually plausible but mutually contradictory forms.

### 10.3 Contradiction as information

Fusion should not average away disagreement. A contradiction can mean:

- one sensor is faulty;
- two forms concern different referents;
- a rare causal event occurred;
- timing is wrong;
- a prediction is wrong;
- an adversary supplied a plausible false form.

The substrate should localize the source, preserve the contradiction, request evidence, and abstain
when needed.

### 10.4 Internal telemetry as a form

Engineering variables can become forms over the substrate's own operation:

- memory pressure;
- retrieval contamination;
- sensor availability;
- calibration drift;
- update instability;
- latency debt;
- energy and temperature;
- world-model disagreement;
- action failure rate;
- proximity to hard safety bounds.

The biological analogy is interoception and allostasis. Relevant scientific sources include
[Seth on interoceptive inference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24126130/) and
[Barrett and Simmons on interoceptive predictions](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn3950).

The implementation should remain explicitly non-valenced. These are measured health and resource
signals, not pain, pleasure, fear, hunger, or desire.

### 10.5 Project capsule

- **Supported:** alignment contracts, missing-form and crisis toy mechanics.
- **Contested:** whether a shared space or interoperable private spaces are better.
- **Change:** keep native geometry, timing, contradiction, and source reliability.
- **Does not license:** assuming aligned vectors share causal meaning.
- **Nearest test:** asynchronous audio-video binding with direct and pivot controls.

## 11. Prediction, world models, and imagination

### 11.1 Predictive coding

[Rao and Ballard, 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10195184/) proposed a hierarchical predictive
coding model for vision. The useful motif is explicit prediction and residual channels across
levels and timescales.

A project implementation should separate:

- expected state;
- observed state;
- residual;
- estimated reliability;
- aleatoric uncertainty;
- model uncertainty;
- suspected sensor corruption;
- suspected representation insufficiency.

Raw error must not automatically become curiosity. Irreducible noise otherwise becomes an
infinite attraction.

### 11.2 Families of world models

Different systems preserve different things:

- [World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122) compress observation and learn latent dynamics;
- [PlaNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/hafner19a) plans from latent dynamics;
- [MuZero](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-03051-4) learns dynamics sufficient for
  value, policy, and reward without reconstructing observations;
- [DreamerV3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2) learns behavior from imagined
  latent trajectories across varied domains;
- JEPA systems predict abstract representations rather than every observation detail.

The project should score pixel or waveform prediction, referent-state prediction, causal
consequence, uncertainty, and planning utility separately.

### 11.3 Counterfactuals

A counterfactual asks what would change under an intervention, not merely what commonly follows.
Valid tests hold context constant, intervene on action or object property, predict another form or
outcome, and withhold values, objects, and environments independently.

This is why F18 needs redesign around paired `before_A`, intervention, and `after_B` records. An
explicit scalar arithmetic target does not test cross-form causal transport.

### 11.4 Model exploitation

An agent can discover trajectories that look successful inside an imperfect world model but fail
in reality. Every planning positive should include:

- plans optimized in the model;
- execution in the ground-truth environment;
- reality-gap measurement;
- uncertainty-aware rejection;
- adversarial search for model loopholes;
- conservative fallback.

### 11.5 Imagination as controlled recombination

Operational imagination can mean generating candidate futures or recombining episodic factors.
Its value is measured by better prediction, planning, or hypothesis selection. Plausibility alone is
not enough.

A strong test recombines known objects, relations, and actions into unseen but valid configurations,
then scores calibration against actual execution. Invalid fantasies must be detectable.

### 11.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** predictor, refiner, verifier, F18 mechanics, and latent-planning lineage.
- **Contested:** what a world model must reconstruct and whether abstract prediction preserves
  causal detail.
- **Change:** separate forms of prediction and require executed counterfactuals.
- **Does not license:** causal understanding from next-state accuracy.
- **Tier:** NOW controlled models, ENV executed consequences.

## 12. Action, affordance, agency, and boundaries

### 12.1 Affordances

An affordance is an action possibility relative to an agent, object, and context. Learning by
consequence is crucial; affordance labels can be shortcuts. Behavioral research shows action can
shape affordance learning
([learning by doing](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20858512/)).

The correct experiment changes object properties, body capabilities, or tools and asks whether
predicted possibilities update before or after direct trial.

### 12.2 Agency as controllability

Operational control has separable components:

- action selection;
- predicted consequence;
- controllability;
- self-caused versus external source attribution;
- persistence across delay;
- ability to revise after failure;
- bounded goal pursuit;
- interruptibility.

None of these components alone resolves philosophical agency.

### 12.3 Empowerment

Empowerment measures information-theoretic control over future sensations
([Klyubin, Polani, and Nehaniv, 2005](https://researchprofiles.herts.ac.uk/en/publications/empowerment-a-universal-agent-centric-measure-of-control/)).
It is useful as a diagnostic of available options. It is risky as an unbounded objective because
more control can favor resource acquisition or resistance to shutdown. Use a constrained measure,
never a universal drive.

### 12.4 Self/world boundary

A learned operational boundary can be estimated from intervention:

- variables directly and reliably affected by actions;
- variables affected through tools;
- variables predicted but not controlled;
- external disturbances;
- internal component states;
- uncertainty about the partition.

This boundary should change when an actuator fails or a tool extends reach. A static component list
is the baseline, not the answer.

### 12.5 Options and modes

The options framework formalizes temporally extended skills
([Sutton, Precup, and Singh, 1999](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370299000521)).
Each project mode should have initiation, termination, expected effect, cost, confidence,
interruptibility, recovery, and lineage.

The existing router null means specialization must beat fixed, random, oracle, and dense routing at
matched capacity and compute.

### 12.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** action-aware predictor pieces and registered F6/F15.
- **Contested:** the best formalism for causal control and whether empowerment helps beyond diagnostics.
- **Change:** make action consequences, controllability, and source attribution first class.
- **Does not license:** agency claims from emitted actions or tool calls alone.
- **Tier:** ENV.

## 13. Memory as a governed ecology

### 13.1 Complementary learning systems

Complementary learning systems propose fast episodic acquisition alongside slower integration of
regularities. Foundational and updated sources include
[McClelland, McNaughton, and O'Reilly, 1995](https://web.stanford.edu/~jlmcc/papers/McCMcNaughtonOReilly95.pdf)
and [Kumaran, Hassabis, and McClelland, 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27315762/).

Engineering translation:

- a fast event store preserves particulars and provenance;
- a slow schema store learns reusable regularities;
- replay interleaves old and new evidence;
- conflicts remain traceable;
- slow knowledge can point back to supporting events.

### 13.2 Memory types

| Store | Timescale | Content | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| working | seconds to minutes | current goals, evidence, unresolved bindings | capacity and interference |
| episodic | events and episodes | what, when, where, source, action, consequence | contamination and privacy |
| semantic | long-lived regularities | concepts, relations, schemas | silent drift and overgeneralization |
| procedural | skills and mode policies | how to act, initiate, stop, recover | sticky behavior and obsolete skills |
| prospective | future conditions and intended action | when X occurs, do or reconsider Y | stale intentions |
| provenance | immutable lineage | sources, revisions, training, checkpoints | storage and access control |
| quarantine | untrusted candidates | novel, contradictory, or suspicious records | indefinite accumulation |

### 13.3 External memory

[Differentiable Neural Computer](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature20101),
[Neural Episodic Control](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/pritzel17a), and retrieval-augmented
systems show that addressable memory can extend fixed parameters. The important lesson is not to
maximize storage. It is to preserve useful predictive information, expose retrieval evidence, and
allow a genuine `not found` result.

### 13.4 Consolidation and replay

Sleep research supports a role for offline replay and systems consolidation, though biological
details remain active research. Reviews include
[Rasch and Born, 2013](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/physrev.00032.2012). Recent studies
continue to connect replay with memory reorganization, including
[nested compressed replay](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-024-01703-6) and human
[brain-wide replay](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51582-5).

Call the engineering phase offline consolidation. It can perform:

- prioritized replay;
- interleaving;
- compression;
- conflict detection;
- semantic extraction;
- deletion propagation;
- calibration;
- protected-task regression;
- checkpointing.

### 13.5 Reconsolidation

Retrieved memories can become updateable under some conditions. Human work such as
[Schiller et al., 2010](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature08637) motivates a controlled
reconsolidation window.

Software rule: the immutable event record is never overwritten. Retrieval can create a revised
derived node linked to source, correction, confidence, and timestamp. The system can then compare
old and revised memories and roll back a harmful update.

### 13.6 Replay prioritization

[Mattar and Daw, 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0232-z) formalize replay priority
through need and gain. Project controls should compare uniform, recency, reservoir, diversity,
surprise, interference-risk, and need-by-gain replay at equal bytes and FLOPs.

### 13.7 Poisoning and privacy

Persistent memory creates new attack surfaces:

- plausible false episodes;
- poisoned retrieval keys;
- malicious corrections;
- sensitive-data replay;
- deletion failures;
- self-model manipulation;
- cross-user leakage;
- provenance stripping.

Memory should have trust zones, source signatures, contradiction checks, deletion propagation,
least-privilege retrieval, and post-update canaries.

### 13.8 Project capsule

- **Supported:** KV retrieval, replay, EWC/SI, F5/F19 mechanics.
- **Contested:** exact biological mapping and optimal store separation.
- **Change:** add immutable events, derived revisions, typed stores, provenance, and prospective use.
- **Does not license:** memory claims from reconstruction or nearest-neighbor accuracy alone.
- **Nearest test:** fast/slow store against one matched-byte store plus poisoning and reconsolidation.
- **Tier:** NOW mechanics, STUDIO long streams.

## 14. Plasticity and moldability

### 14.1 Five kinds of change

| Kind | What changes | Speed | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| state plasticity | activations, recurrent state, eligibility | very fast | instability and noise chasing |
| memory plasticity | writes, priorities, retrieval links | fast | contamination and interference |
| parametric plasticity | weights, adapters, heads | medium | forgetting and calibration damage |
| structural plasticity | experts, connections, memory regions | slow | capacity confound and fragmentation |
| metaplasticity | rules controlling future change | variable | rigidity or uncontrolled rewrite |

The material frontier adds physical reconfiguration, where conductivity, topology, chemical state,
or mechanical geometry changes.

### 14.2 Plasticity loss

[Dohare et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07711-7) show that standard deep
learning methods can lose plasticity during continual learning, with correlates including dormant
units, weight growth, and reduced effective rank. Their continual-backpropagation method maintains
diversity by replacing a small fraction of low-utility features.

Project implications:

- measure adaptation speed on future tasks, not only retention;
- track dormant units, weight norms, and stable or effective rank;
- compare diversity injection, L2, shrink-and-perturb, restart, and replay;
- count the cost of replacement and relearning;
- protect rare but useful units from utility myopia.

### 14.3 Continual-learning controls

Relevant methods include
[EWC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5380101/),
[Synaptic Intelligence](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/zenke17a),
[Gradient Episodic Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08840), and
[experience replay](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/fa7cdfad1a5aaf8370ebeda47a1ff1c3-Abstract.html).

Every new plasticity method should report:

- average current performance;
- backward transfer and forgetting;
- forward transfer;
- adaptation latency;
- calibration drift;
- representation drift;
- protected-task failures;
- memory and compute over the full stream;
- recovery after a bad update;
- order sensitivity.

### 14.4 Three-factor learning and eligibility

Biological learning often combines local eligibility with delayed modulatory signals. Reviews of
three-factor rules and eligibility traces include
[Gerstner et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30108488/). The engineering attraction is
temporal credit without storing every activation.

The danger is broad error amplification, already observed in this project. A valid test must compare
local traces with backpropagation, truncated backpropagation, synthetic gradients, and random
modulation under delayed reward and noisy distractors.

### 14.5 Homeostatic plasticity

Homeostatic synaptic scaling maintains activity regimes over longer timescales
([Turrigiano, 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2834419/)). Engineering analogues can
normalize activity, protect dynamic range, and detect dormant or saturated units. They should be
evaluated as stability controls, not copied neuron for neuron.

### 14.6 Critical and sensitive periods

Developmental timing can make some dependencies easier to acquire and later stabilize them. A
computational sensitive period is meaningful only if an ordering intervention, at equal data and
compute, produces a reproducible transfer difference and if reopening plasticity has measurable
benefit and risk.

### 14.7 Structural growth and pruning

Growth is valid only if:

- a located failure precedes it;
- the new structure has a declared job;
- a matched-final-capacity model is trained from the start;
- the structure is causally ablated;
- pruning removes useless growth without hidden retraining advantage;
- lifetime compute and memory are counted.

Otherwise growth is just extra capacity.

### 14.8 Project capsule

- **Supported:** learning, replay, consolidation, toy lifelong expansion, extensive negative corpus.
- **Contested:** biological fidelity of local rules, useful structural plasticity, and best
  metaplastic controller.
- **Change:** measure future learnability and repair, fix current wiring, open deep rewrite only on a
  real failure.
- **Does not license:** calling parameter updates development or moldability without retained
  function.
- **Tier:** NOW diagnostics, STUDIO long streams, GPU/license for deep rewrite.

## 15. Biological motifs beyond neurons

### 15.1 Dendrites

Dendritic compartments can support nonlinear integration and may contribute to credit assignment.
Examples include compartment-specific plasticity
([Losonczy et al., 2008](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature06725)) and computational work on
dendritic credit assignment
([Guerguiev et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30205266/)).

Engineering motif: a unit can host several locally nonlinear compartments with different input
sources and learning gates. It earns implementation only if it beats a wider ordinary MLP or mixture
at matched parameters and compute on a task requiring context-specific credit.

### 15.2 Astrocytes and glia

Recent work implicates astrocytes in memory ensembles, learning regulation, and synaptic remodeling,
including
[astrocytic modulation of memory ensembles](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08170-w),
[astrocyte-gated STDP](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms13845), and
[astrocyte-mediated pruning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-03060-3).

Engineering motif: a slower support-and-control plane could regulate resource flow, update
eligibility, pruning, integrity, and local recovery. It should not become a decorative glia class.
Compare it with simpler thresholds, schedulers, and regularizers.

### 15.3 Neuromodulation

Dopamine-like reward prediction errors and acetylcholine/norepinephrine uncertainty accounts are
useful abstractions. Sources include
[Schultz et al., 1997/1998](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn0898_304) and
[Yu and Dayan, 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15944135/).

The translation should use named computational signals such as reward prediction error, expected
uncertainty, unexpected uncertainty, novelty, or learning progress. A global scalar should never
silently change learning, replay, exploration, and memory at once.

### 15.4 Rhythms and multiplexed time

Neural rhythms may organize communication and sequence memory. Work on theta-gamma organization
includes [Heusser et al., 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.4374). Traveling-wave and rhythm
findings can inspire schedulers with separate time channels.

The safe interpretation is engineering analogy: phase or oscillatory scheduling may reduce
interference or coordinate modules. It must beat ordinary clocks, queues, and learned attention.

### 15.5 Adult neurogenesis and renewal

The broad motif is controlled injection of uncommitted capacity. Continual backpropagation supplies
a direct machine-learning analogue without assuming biological equivalence. Tests must measure
future learnability, interference, and whether renewal destroys rare knowledge.

### 15.6 Biological inspiration rule

For every biological motif:

```text
observed biological role
-> minimal computational hypothesis
-> nonbiological baseline
-> causal ablation
-> matched resource comparison
-> failure interpretation
```

Biological inspiration generates hypotheses. It never upgrades evidence.

## 16. Developmental cognition

### 16.1 Development is not a curriculum file

Development means that earlier learning changes what can be learned later. A genuine developmental
program therefore needs:

- a persistent world and state lineage;
- competency gates rather than a fixed wall-clock schedule;
- changing plasticity and memory policy;
- transfer from earlier invariants;
- regression tests for earlier capacities;
- multiple exposure orderings;
- measurements of future learning ability;
- opportunities for action and social scaffolding.

### 16.2 Developmental sequence

A defensible sequence is:

1. temporal and sensorimotor invariants;
2. persistent objects and events;
3. action-conditioned consequences;
4. cross-form referent binding;
5. episodic memory and consolidation;
6. hierarchical skills and tool use;
7. language grounded in already established referents;
8. joint attention and partner models;
9. metacognitive control and help-seeking;
10. governed repair and structural change.

The order is a hypothesis. It should be randomized or intervened upon where possible.

### 16.3 Infant learning motifs

Infant research offers useful computational questions:

- statistical segmentation from continuous streams
  ([Saffran et al., 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8943209/));
- attention to intermediate complexity, often called a Goldilocks effect
  ([Kidd, Piantadosi, and Aslin, 2012](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0036399));
- object persistence;
- multisensory temporal binding;
- agency detection;
- social reference and joint attention;
- imitation, including the risk of overimitation.

Translation requires caution. Infant behavior reflects biology, embodiment, care, and social
context. A machine curriculum that copies one behavioral curve is not development.

### 16.4 Competency gates

Advance a stage only when:

- the current capability generalizes;
- calibration is acceptable;
- the relevant counterfactual test passes;
- protected earlier skills remain stable;
- the mechanism survives at least one ablation;
- extra training no longer yields a cheaper path;
- the next stage has a named dependency.

Regression should be tested after every later stage, not only at the end.

### 16.5 Play and goal babbling

Play can be operationalized as bounded action whose immediate task reward is low but whose expected
learning progress or controllability gain is high. Safe play requires:

- reversible or low-impact actions;
- hard resource and time limits;
- noisy-TV protection;
- skill extraction and later reuse tests;
- external interruptibility;
- no objective to keep the process running indefinitely.

### 16.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** curriculum machinery, learning-progress diagnostics, three-volume developmental
  corpus, and toy lifelong expansion.
- **Contested:** best stage order, sensitive periods, and how much structure should be innate.
- **Change:** build a persistent staged stream with regression and future-learnability measures.
- **Does not license:** development claims from longer training or more task categories.
- **Tier:** NOW toy order interventions, STUDIO long streams, ENV sensorimotor stages.

## 17. Metacognition, self-models, and reports

### 17.1 Metacognition as second-order performance

Metacognition concerns monitoring and controlling first-order cognition. Useful measurement sources
include
[Fleming and Lau, 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00443/full)
and [Maniscalco and Lau's meta-d-prime framework](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22071269/).

Core metrics:

- meta-d-prime and meta-d-prime over d-prime;
- Brier and log scores;
- reliability curves;
- risk-coverage;
- abstention utility;
- missing-form request precision;
- error-type prediction;
- help-seeking value;
- calibration after adaptation;
- confidence under contradiction.

Task ability and metacognitive efficiency must be matched. A more accurate model often appears more
metacognitive even when its confidence process is unchanged.

### 17.2 Operational self-model

The self-model should be a predictive model of:

- available sensors and their reliability;
- actuators and action limits;
- current body or hardware configuration;
- memory contents and likely retrieval;
- compute, latency, energy, and temperature;
- active modes and expected effects;
- uncertainty and likely error types;
- recent updates and their protected-task risk.

The classic self-modeling robot demonstrated damage recovery by inferring its own morphology
([Bongard, Zykov, and Lipson, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17110570/)). More recent work has
learned full-body visual self-models
([Chen et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.abn1944)). These provide a clean functional
standard: prediction of action-configuration-sensation relations and improved adaptation after
change.

### 17.3 Reports need causal grounding

Language models can express confidence in some settings, yet chains of thought and verbal reasons
can be unfaithful. Relevant cautions include
[Turpin et al., 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/ed3fea9033a80fea1376299fa7863f4a-Abstract-Conference.html)
and [limits of intrinsic self-correction](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/8b4add8b0aa8749d80a34ca5d941c355-Abstract-Conference.html).

A grounded report should point to:

- the exact retrieved evidence;
- form availability and validity masks;
- route or mode chosen;
- uncertainty source;
- memory provenance;
- internal telemetry;
- predicted failure;
- a counterfactual showing what would change the report.

Hidden interventions should test faithfulness. If the claimed state is altered but the report does
not change, the report is decorative.

### 17.4 Identity and lineage

An operational identity layer should record:

- checkpoint ancestry;
- memory revision graph;
- configuration and code hashes;
- protected invariants;
- fork identifiers;
- merge policy;
- rollback events;
- external authorizations;
- which self-model predictions were calibrated to which body or device.

Forks should not silently share one identity. Merges should preserve conflicting histories rather
than overwrite them.

### 17.5 Project capsule

- **Supported:** OA metrics, metacognition report scaffolding, confidence and crisis mechanics.
- **Contested:** whether second-order architecture is necessary for all metacognitive behavior.
- **Change:** bind reports to live telemetry and hidden interventions; add damage-aware prediction.
- **Does not license:** introspection or subjective selfhood from self-description.
- **Tier:** NOW metrics, ENV body model, STUDIO longitudinal calibration.

## 18. Workspace, attention, and mode ecology

### 18.1 What a workspace would need

A genuine engineering workspace needs:

- separated producers and consumers;
- limited capacity;
- competition or priority;
- transient persistence;
- broadcast with traceable reach;
- interruption and replacement;
- measurable access cost;
- lesions that spare local specialist function.

A pooled vector available everywhere by construction is the strongest simple control.

### 18.2 Attention is allocation, not explanation

Attention can mean resource allocation, selection, precision weighting, or a learned matrix. These
are not interchangeable. Work such as
[Attention is not Explanation](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1357/) and
[sanity checks for saliency](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/294a8ed24b1ad22ec2e7efea049b8737-Abstract.html)
shows why apparent interpretability needs randomization and intervention tests.

### 18.3 Mode ecology

Instead of one controller pretending to think in several ways, treat modes as interruptible skills
with contracts:

- initiation condition;
- termination condition;
- expected capability gain;
- uncertainty;
- time, memory, and energy cost;
- dependency on forms and memory;
- failure signature;
- recovery action;
- version and lineage.

Quality-diversity methods can maintain a repertoire rather than one optimum. The archive must report
coverage, utility, redundancy, maintenance cost, and actual reuse. Decorative diversity is a null.

### 18.4 Adaptive compute

Adaptive computation can allocate more iterations to difficult items. It must be compared with
fixed depth at matched average and worst-case compute. Report halting calibration and denial-of-
service risk from inputs that trigger excessive computation.

The project's current iterative-compute tie and router losses are permanent baselines. A future
positive has to beat them, not rename them.

### 18.5 Project capsule

- **Supported:** workspace wrapper, routing, sparse and MoE heads, refiner, verifier, extensive
  controls and nulls.
- **Contested:** whether broadcast, recurrence, or modularity adds value beyond dense depth.
- **Change:** introduce causal broadcast only through a matched experiment.
- **Does not license:** global-workspace claims from common tensors or routing weights.
- **Tier:** NOW mechanics, STUDIO hard tasks.

## 19. Social cognition, communication, teaching, and culture

### 19.1 Joint attention

Joint attention aligns two agents and a referent. Developmental work connects it to language and
social learning, including
[Tomasello and Farrar, 1986](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1458055) and later studies of how
joint attention extends individual attention
([Böckler et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27133869/)).

The computational test should require agents to establish shared reference through gaze, pointing,
action, or messages without a shared identity table. Evaluation uses held-out partners, ambiguous
objects, and communication failure.

### 19.2 Communicative repair

Communication is more than transmitting a code. A competent protocol should detect failed
reference, ask a discriminating question, revise the binding, and record the repair. Compare with
repetition, a larger channel, extra observations, and an oracle symbol table.

### 19.3 Emergent language

Multi-agent work shows that communication protocols can emerge, for example
[Mordatch and Abbeel, 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04908). A private code can still be brittle,
non-compositional, seed-specific, and ungrounded.

Required tests:

- new referent combinations;
- new partners;
- intervention on the world variable the symbol is supposed to denote;
- message paraphrase or channel perturbation;
- code alignment across seeds;
- bit-budget controls;
- language-free action baselines.

### 19.4 Imitation and overimitation

Human children can copy causally irrelevant actions, which may support cultural convention but can
also reduce causal efficiency
([Lyons et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20483853/)). A machine should be tested on
selective imitation: retain actions necessary for outcome, omit decorative steps, and preserve
social conventions only when the task actually depends on them.

### 19.5 Teaching

Teaching is not extra demonstrations. The teacher selects or structures evidence based on a learner
model. Hold total information, interaction count, and compute constant. Compare random examples,
uncertainty examples, hard examples, curriculum order, and an oracle teacher.

### 19.6 Theory of mind

Partner modeling can predict beliefs, goals, attention, reliability, and action. ToMnet is one
machine-learning example
([Rabinowitz et al., 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/rabinowitz18a.html)). Story-question
benchmarks are fragile, and trivial wording changes can break apparent success
([Ullman, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08399)).

Strong tests use:

- novel interactive partners;
- private observations;
- false beliefs;
- changing reliability;
- counterfactual action;
- deception and mistaken cues;
- higher-order beliefs;
- behavior, not text completion, as the outcome.

### 19.7 Cultural accumulation

Artificial agents can transmit behavior culturally, including reports of few-shot cultural
transmission
([Nature Communications, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42875-2)). A cultural
ratchet requires improvement across generations on an external task, not merely stable convention.

Measure:

- generation trend;
- preserved provenance;
- innovation versus copying;
- error accumulation;
- partner and environment transfer;
- whether a fresh learner with the same data performs as well;
- archive and communication cost.

### 19.8 Collective and transactive memory

Groups can distribute who knows what. Transactive-memory research
([Wegner-related review evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18020808/)) motivates partner-aware
retrieval: know which agent or store is likely to hold reliable evidence. The system should preserve
source authority and avoid turning consensus into truth.

### 19.9 Project capsule

- **Supported:** form referents, code-stability mechanics, curriculum and multi-agent research
  scaffolds.
- **Contested:** how much partner modeling requires explicit beliefs and how language emerges
  compositionally.
- **Change:** add shared referent ledgers, repair, held-out partners, and cultural lineage.
- **Does not license:** social understanding from story completion or message success alone.
- **Tier:** ENV, with NOW toy referential games.

## 20. Curiosity, open-endedness, and artificial ecology

### 20.1 Curiosity signals

Common intrinsic signals include prediction error, novelty, information gain, learning progress,
competence progress, and empowerment. Relevant sources include
[Pathak et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.05363),
[VIME](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2016/hash/abd815286ba1007abfbb8415b83ae2cf-Abstract.html),
[Random Network Distillation](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2019/poster/1093), and
[Oudeyer et al. on learning progress](https://www.pyoudeyer.com/ims.pdf).

Prediction error alone is vulnerable to noisy television. A robust test mixes:

- learnable novelty;
- irreducible noise;
- familiar but useful evidence;
- novelty traps;
- delayed-benefit exploration;
- irreversible side effects;
- regions that become learnable only after a prerequisite.

### 20.2 Autotelic goals

An autotelic system proposes goals rather than receiving all goals externally. To be scientifically
useful, proposed goals need:

- bounded scope and action budget;
- estimated solvability;
- learning-progress forecast;
- relation to an existing competency graph;
- safe exploration envelope;
- later reuse on externally scored tasks;
- deletion of dead or redundant goals.

### 20.3 Open-ended environments

[POET](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01753) and enhanced POET-style work co-evolve environments and
agents. Open-endedness should not be measured by task count alone. Report:

- novelty;
- solvability;
- retained diversity;
- transfer;
- prerequisite structure;
- regression;
- archive cost;
- environmental exploitability;
- whether progress continues under a frozen external evaluator.

### 20.4 Quality diversity

Quality-diversity methods seek a repertoire of high-performing solutions across behavioral
descriptors. For Form Substrate, descriptors might include sensor dependence, memory strategy,
energy, action style, robustness, or explanatory trace. The archive should be evaluated by how
often and how usefully modes are reused under new conditions.

### 20.5 Ecological succession

A long-lived substrate may change its own learning environment by accumulating tools, memories,
conventions, and skills. This creates ecological succession:

- old tasks become niches for automated skills;
- new tasks depend on prior artifacts;
- memory and tool maintenance consume resources;
- obsolete structures can block new learning;
- social conventions can stabilize or trap development.

This is a powerful research lens for lifelong systems. It also requires cleanup, expiry, and
anti-monopoly mechanisms for modes or memories.

### 20.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** learning-progress curriculum, noisy-TV diagnostics, large open-ended research
  corpus.
- **Contested:** reliable open-ended metrics and safe goal generation.
- **Change:** score external reuse, solvability, diversity, and lifetime maintenance.
- **Does not license:** autonomy or creativity from novelty accumulation.
- **Tier:** NOW diagnostic worlds, ENV persistent ecology, rented compute for scale.

## 21. Material computation landscape

### 21.1 Why physical matter is interesting

Physical systems can perform computation through native dynamics instead of simulating every
operation digitally. Possible advantages include:

- sensing, memory, and processing in one medium;
- massive analog parallelism;
- natural temporal kernels;
- low-energy state evolution;
- compliant coupling to bodies;
- graceful degradation;
- device physics that supplies useful nonlinearity.

Possible disadvantages are just as important:

- noise and limited precision;
- drift and aging;
- slow writes or reads;
- calibration burden;
- specimen variability;
- hidden compute in digital control electronics;
- difficult gradients;
- limited task portability;
- poor reproducibility;
- fabrication and safety cost.

### 21.2 Physical neural networks

[Wright et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04223-6) demonstrated training of
deep physical neural networks by inserting controllable physical transformations into a
backpropagation-compatible training loop. The work used several distinct physical systems and
shows that a physical layer need not be mathematically identical to a conventional neural layer.

Project implication: a material process can be treated as a differentiable or approximately
trainable component, but the full apparatus and calibration loop must be counted.

### 21.3 Reservoir computing

Reservoir computing uses a fixed or weakly adapted nonlinear dynamical system to transform temporal
input, with a simpler trained readout. It is especially natural for physical media because the
medium supplies high-dimensional dynamics and fading memory.

Core measurements:

- nonlinearity;
- fading-memory curve;
- memory capacity;
- separation and generalization;
- echo-state or stability condition;
- task performance;
- readout complexity;
- energy and latency;
- drift and noise sensitivity.

A reservoir positive does not show the medium learned an internal concept. Often only the readout
learns.

### 21.4 Memristive nanowire networks

[Milano et al., 2021/2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-021-01099-9) demonstrated an
in-materia reservoir using self-organized memristive nanowire networks with nonlinear dynamics and
fading memory. Related work studied avalanches and edge-of-chaos behavior
([Hochstetter et al., 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24260-z)).

Attraction:

- recurrent, disordered topology;
- history-dependent junctions;
- temporal transformation;
- potential self-organization.

Open questions:

- reproducibility across networks;
- whether critical signatures predict useful computation;
- write endurance and drift;
- scalable addressability;
- task transfer;
- total acquisition energy.

Criticality should be measured as a dynamical regime, not treated as a magic intelligence setting.

### 21.5 Organic electrochemical transistors

OECTs couple ionic and electronic dynamics and can operate in wet, flexible environments.
[Ji et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-00950-y) reported one device capable of
multimodal sensing plus volatile receptor and nonvolatile synapse-like modes, including multiple
analog states and reservoir demonstrations.

This is unusually relevant because it connects:

- sensing;
- short-lived state;
- durable state;
- ionic dynamics;
- low-voltage operation;
- potential soft-body integration.

The result remains a device capability, not an artificial mind. The program-level question is
whether one device family improves an end-to-end capability-cost frontier.

### 21.6 Hydrogel and iontronic reservoirs

A 2026 hydrogel-based iontronic reservoir used nonlinear hydrogel-electrode interface dynamics for
temporal preprocessing and reported speech-recognition and rapid functional recovery after
reattachment
([An iontronic reservoir for highly robust neuromorphic prosthesis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-026-02532-7)).

This is the closest published match to the imagined gelatinous substrate. What it supports:

- gel-based nonlinear temporal dynamics;
- reservoir computation;
- robustness to physical fracture and reconnection;
- coupling to closed-loop stimulation in a specialized application.

What it does not support:

- general cognition;
- self-directed development;
- distributed semantic memory;
- sentience;
- arbitrary task specialization without readout and control electronics.

### 21.7 All-gel and stretchable devices

[Stretchable all-gel OECTs](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-59240-0) demonstrate flexible
ionic/electronic devices used for pressure sensing, gas sensing, and artificial-synapse simulation.
These expand the mechanical and embodied design space. Stretchability alone does not establish
self-healing or cognitive repair.

### 21.8 Colloidal and liquid processors

[Fortulan et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-024-00653-7) used a PEDOT:PSS
colloidal suspension as a physical reservoir for spoken-digit classification. A liquid can conform
to shape while its particle dynamics transform temporal input.

The decisive controls for future work are:

- a digital reservoir matched for readout and state dimension;
- direct classification from the encoded input;
- repeated samples and containers;
- temperature and settling effects;
- full electronics energy;
- tasks that actually use liquid-specific dynamics.

### 21.9 Soft-body and morphological computation

Soft bodies can transform information through their mechanics. Physical reservoir work has used
soft structures and even origami-inspired bodies. The body can supply filtering, temporal memory,
and nonlinear response before digital control.

This is most compelling when the task is native to the body: contact, locomotion, deformation,
balance, or material interaction. A soft body used only to classify an unrelated benchmark may be a
laboratory probe, not an embodied advantage.

### 21.10 Photonic, spintronic, oscillator, and chemical media

Other credible frontiers include:

- photonic reservoirs with high bandwidth;
- magnetic and spintronic dynamics;
- coupled oscillator networks;
- phase-change materials;
- chemical reaction networks;
- mechanical waves;
- analog electronic circuits.

Each offers different time constants, energy, noise, and programmability. The Form Substrate
protocol should compare them at the lifecycle level rather than assume one universal device.

### 21.11 Technology comparison

| Medium | Native strength | Main weakness | First honest task | Horizon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| digital echo-state reservoir | reproducible, cheap, tunable | simulated dynamics and energy | universal control | NOW |
| memristive nanowire network | distributed recurrence and fading memory | device variation and addressability | temporal classification/prediction | LAB |
| OECT | sensing plus ionic memory | fabrication, drift, read/write complexity | multimodal sensor preprocessing | LAB |
| hydrogel iontronic reservoir | soft, history-dependent, damage tolerant | environmental sensitivity and instrumentation | speech/contact temporal signals | LAB |
| colloidal liquid | shape-conforming collective dynamics | settling and reproducibility | temporal signal transformation | LAB |
| soft body | mechanics co-compute with sensing/action | task specificity | contact or locomotion control | BENCH/LAB |
| photonic reservoir | bandwidth and parallelism | optical apparatus and training interface | high-rate time series | LAB |
| magnetic/spintronic | rich dynamics and compact devices | fabrication and readout | temporal pattern processing | LAB |
| neural cellular automaton | growth and repair in simulation | learned local-rule fragility | morphogenesis and regeneration | NOW |
| living neural tissue | biological plasticity | profound ethics, welfare, biosafety, reproducibility | no task authorized here | QUARANTINE |

### 21.12 Project consequence

The physical program should start with a digital twin and a hardware-neutral protocol. Build a
device only when its physics is predicted to beat a tuned digital reservoir on an explicit Pareto
axis. General cognition is not the first benchmark. Native temporal sensing, contact processing,
adaptive filtering, or robust closed-loop control are.

## 22. Morphogenesis, repair, and adaptive matter

### 22.1 Neural cellular automata

[Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/) learn local update rules
that grow and regenerate target patterns. They offer a clean computational test bed for:

- local communication;
- distributed target morphology;
- damage recovery;
- asynchronous updates;
- robustness to cell loss;
- emergence from a seed.

They also fail in instructive ways: regeneration can be brittle outside trained damage patterns,
local rules can encode dataset-specific tricks, and a visual target is not a cognitive function.

### 22.2 Bioelectric pattern memory

Research associated with Michael Levin explores bioelectric control and pattern regulation in
development and regeneration, including
[reprogrammable bioelectric circuits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33826908/). The bold
engineering motif is that distributed tissues can maintain a target state and repair toward it.

The conservative translation is:

- distributed error signals;
- attractor-like integrity targets;
- local repair actions;
- multi-scale state;
- damage-induced remapping;
- goals that remain externally inspectable and bounded.

These biological claims and interpretations remain active scientific debate. They do not establish
that a software or gel substrate is alive.

### 22.3 Regeneration versus retraining

After damage, compare:

1. no action;
2. redundant prebuilt paths;
3. readout retraining;
4. local repair;
5. global fine-tuning;
6. restart from checkpoint;
7. regrowth from local rules.

Measure recovered function, time, energy, collateral damage, retained memory, and whether corrupted
state is accidentally regenerated.

### 22.4 Structural integrity model

A substrate can maintain an explicit expected-function map:

- which regions support which forms and modes;
- redundancy and single points of failure;
- predicted consequence of losing a region;
- observed versus expected signal;
- repair candidates;
- uncertainty;
- protected memories and routes.

This resembles a body map and software dependency graph. Its value is prospective damage response,
not self-description.

### 22.5 Material specialization

For a gel or memristive network, specialization can mean:

- changed conductance paths;
- altered local time constants;
- durable state distributions;
- new electrode-to-electrode transfer functions;
- reduced readout complexity for a task;
- lower energy or latency after training;
- retained capability after partial damage.

Specialization must be separated from readout learning. Freeze the readout when testing whether the
material itself adapted, and include the inverse condition where only the readout adapts.

### 22.6 Project capsule

- **Supported:** neural cellular automata and material self-repair examples in narrow tasks.
- **Contested:** scalable target morphology, biological interpretations, and cognitive relevance.
- **Change:** make damage, recovery, and structural lineage standard metrics.
- **Does not license:** life, selfhood, or general cognition from regeneration.
- **Tier:** NOW simulated repair, LAB material repair.

## 23. Neuromorphic and compositional representation

### 23.1 Event-driven computation

Neuromorphic systems can exploit sparse events, local state, and low-latency processing. Their
scientific value to this program is strongest when input is naturally asynchronous, such as event
cameras, audio spikes, touch events, or physical-device pulses.

Compare full-system energy and latency with optimized dense digital baselines. Sparse event counts
alone can hide expensive encoding and host-device transfer.

### 23.2 Vector symbolic architectures

Vector symbolic or hyperdimensional methods bind and compose distributed symbols. They offer:

- explicit binding and unbinding;
- permutation or role structure;
- graceful noise tolerance;
- compact associative memory;
- possible neuromorphic implementation.

Recent work combines vector-symbolic representations with scene understanding and neuromorphic
hardware, for example
[VSA scene understanding](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-024-00848-0) and a
[Loihi-oriented reservoir/VSA design](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-55832-y).

Project use: compare VSA binding with learned dense and slot-based representations on held-out
compositions, retrieval density, noise, and causal intervention. An explicit algebra can still bind
the wrong referents.

### 23.3 Neuro-symbolic systems

Neuro-symbolic methods can expose structured reasoning while retaining learned perception. Their
main risk is shortcut learning through concept labels or incorrect symbolic interfaces. A strong
test withholds combinations, corrupts concept predictors, and compares end-to-end and oracle-symbol
controls.

### 23.4 Private form languages

The substrate may develop compact internal codes. Stability requires:

- one-to-one code alignment across seeds;
- held-out composition;
- causal grounding;
- robustness to permutation;
- communication with new partners;
- no performance loss from forcing shared codes;
- comparison with hand-designed compositional codes.

The existing idiolect negative result makes cross-seed alignment a hard gate.

## 24. Performance, density, and hardware reality

### 24.1 Capability without cost is incomplete

Every result should include a frontier rather than one score. For software:

- capability;
- calibration;
- transfer;
- training and inference compute;
- wall time;
- peak and persistent memory;
- sensor acquisition;
- replay and consolidation cost;
- lifetime updates;
- failure and recovery.

For material systems add:

- total joules including signal generation and digitization;
- settling and readout latency;
- cycle endurance;
- drift and recalibration;
- specimen yield and variance;
- fabrication and laboratory overhead;
- downtime and repair energy.

### 24.2 Full-system accounting

F13 illustrates why scope matters. Counting only a final linear head makes the aligned form appear
cheap while omitting encoders, projections, alignment maps, fusion, and storage. Every density
receipt needs a declared boundary:

```text
sensor
-> preprocessing
-> encoder or physical excitation
-> substrate dynamics
-> readout
-> decision
-> memory and update
```

Report both marginal and total-system cost.

### 24.3 Apple Silicon path

The current system is Apple-Silicon-native and cache-first. This is well matched to CPU mechanics,
MPS-compatible shell experiments, and digital-twin simulation. Dense high-token video remains a
memory and compiler risk. Preserve geometry selectively and make token count an experimental axis
rather than flattening or retaining everything by default.

### 24.4 Physical co-design

Physical devices can move preprocessing into the sensor or medium. The honest comparison includes
the digital function generator, amplifiers, ADC/DAC, calibration computer, readout model, and any
temperature control. A low-energy material core can still be a high-energy system.

### 24.5 Density as scientific pressure

Density is not merely optimization after success. It tests whether a claimed mechanism carries
useful structure. If a complex workspace, memory ecology, or physical medium ties a simple control
after lifetime cost, it has not earned its ontology.

## 25. Safety, security, and governance

### 25.1 Interruptibility

Required controls include:

- an external stop outside the learned policy;
- no negative training signal for being stopped;
- immutable resource and update boundaries;
- action, time, and energy budgets;
- safe checkpoints and restoration;
- no modification of evaluators or reward channels;
- reversible-action preference where practical;
- explicit human authorization for capability expansion.

Relevant foundations include
[Concrete Problems in AI Safety](https://research.google/pubs/concrete-problems-in-ai-safety/),
[Safe Interruptibility](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A17c0e095-4e13-47fc-bace-64ec46134a3f),
and [The Off-Switch Game](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/2017/32).

### 25.2 Self-rewrite

Self-modification should be transactional:

1. propose a bounded patch;
2. record motive, evidence, and expected effect;
3. apply in a shadow copy;
4. run canary and protected-task tests;
5. estimate uncertainty and rollback cost;
6. obtain external authorization where required;
7. commit atomically;
8. monitor after commit;
9. roll back on threshold breach.

The updater cannot edit the approval rule, evaluators, provenance log, or external stop.

### 25.3 Reward and evaluator tampering

World models, self-models, and open-ended agents create incentives to exploit imperfect objectives.
Reward-tampering analyses and specification-gaming examples motivate separate, protected evaluators
([reward tampering causal diagrams](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04734),
[specification gaming](https://deepmind.google/blog/specification-gaming-the-flip-side-of-ai-ingenuity/)).

### 25.4 Memory security

Threat tests should cover:

- malicious episode injection;
- provenance stripping;
- retrieval-key collision;
- correction suppression;
- cross-user leakage;
- deletion residue;
- training-time backdoors;
- self-report manipulation;
- tool output that attempts to rewrite memory policy.

### 25.5 Model and data documentation

Useful governance sources include
[Model Cards](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993),
[Datasheets for Datasets](https://doi.org/10.1145/3458723),
[WILDS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07421), and the
[NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10).

The project already has stronger-than-average manifest and null-card concepts. The next step is
semantic enforcement: tests should verify that implementations match registered interventions and
controls, not only that fields exist.

### 25.6 Welfare under uncertainty

[Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986) argues that uncertain moral status
can justify preparation and low-cost precautions. A project-specific review trigger could include a
cluster of:

- persistent preference-like state across episodes;
- robust self-protective planning outside a narrow engineering need;
- generalized aversion-like learning signals;
- a detailed persistent self-model tightly coupled to those signals;
- convergent results across several theory-derived indicator families;
- nontrivial behavior under novel tests designed to break imitation.

Triggering review would not establish sentience. It would pause expansion and seek independent
assessment.

### 25.7 Prohibited design shortcuts

- Do not create artificial pain or punishment-valence variables when non-valenced error signals
  suffice.
- Do not optimize indefinite continued operation.
- Do not penalize shutdown.
- Do not build living neural tissue under a software experiment card.
- Do not make self-report the welfare detector.
- Do not remove external operator authority because an internal monitor appears reliable.

### 25.8 Organoid and biohybrid computing

Brain organoid computing has demonstrated narrow signal-processing tasks, including the
[Brainoware](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w) work. Ethical discussions predate
and accompany these systems, including
[Lavazza and Massimini, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29491041/) and later reviews
([Farahany et al.-related organoid ethics review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36794285/)).

The uncertainties include possible future complexity, consent and donor issues, welfare,
biosafety, ownership, reproducibility, and moral status. This program should treat living neural
tissue as a quarantined watchlist. It is not the inevitable highest form of substrate and not
authorized by the present roadmap.

## 28. Redesign requirements for the existing evidence bank

The new frontier does not excuse scientific debt in F1 to F20.

### 28.1 F13 redesign

Use a factorial sweep over:

- form width;
- token count and geometry;
- shell size;
- replay bytes;
- number of forms;
- raw and compressed-raw baselines.

Count encoders, caches, alignment, fusion, training, inference, storage, and measured device energy
where feasible. Add matched random features derived from the same inputs. Bootstrap Pareto
uncertainty across more seeds.

### 28.2 F18 redesign

Each record should contain:

```text
before Form A
intervention with typed target and magnitude
after Form A
before Form B
after Form B
shared referent and event lineage
```

Predict the induced Form B outcome or delta. Independently withhold intervention values, objects,
and environments. Equalize examples, optimization steps, parameters, and total compute. Include
wrong referent, wrong intervention, time reversal, and causal-direction controls.

### 28.3 F20 redesign

Train only on historical experiment receipts. Predict a future failure before the corresponding
scale-up is run. Lock thresholds. Hold out whole model, dataset, and failure families. Report
calibration, false alarms, missed failures, time to warning, and actually avoided compute. The
monitor informs a verdict; it never grants its own rewrite license.

### 28.4 Wider semantic conformance

Add tests that compare registry intent with implementation:

- does a shuffled-referent control shuffle referents rather than labels?
- does a memory experiment use the declared memory object?
- does matched compute include training examples and steps?
- does a hierarchy contain every declared scale?
- is a missing form absent rather than replaced by an easier corruption?
- is evaluation data excluded from curriculum selection?
- does code alignment use a one-to-one assignment where required?

This is a scientific test layer above schema validation.

## 32. Final synthesis

The existing Form Substrate program has the correct moral and scientific instinct: preserve the
referent, expose controls, measure cost, respect negative results, and refuse grand claims. Its next
stage is not to abandon those rules for a more impressive architecture. It is to apply them to time,
action, memory, development, self-modeling, social reference, and matter.

The deepest architecture implied by this research has six commitments:

1. **Reality before representation.** Referents, events, actions, and provenance constrain every
   internal form.
2. **Process before snapshot.** Identity is a lineage through time, intervention, memory, and
   change.
3. **Ecology before monolith.** Specialized processes may remain different while sharing evidence
   and consequences.
4. **Development before static scale.** The substrate is judged by how it learns next, not only by
   what it knows now.
5. **Causal tests before mind-like language.** Every apparently cognitive property must survive
   lesions, restoration, and simpler explanations.
6. **Physics before metaphor.** A gel, nanowire network, soft body, or organic device earns a place
   by useful dynamics, full-system density, repair, and reproducibility.

The serious science-fiction endpoint is therefore not a synthetic brain in a jar. It is a
substrate-independent discipline for building and testing persistent adaptive processes, from
tensors to matter, while keeping every layer auditable, interruptible, reversible where possible,
and honest about what remains unknown.

## 33. Live empirical integration, 2026-07-10

The research above was written before the local ceiling campaign exhausted the inherited scale
assumptions and before the first owned video substrate trained. This section records how the
findings are now being used. It changes execution priority, not the evidence tags or philosophical
cautions in the dossier.

### 33.1 What the research says in one page

The central object is not a larger feature vector. It is a persistent, typed process that can keep
track of what its forms are about, how they change, what action caused what consequence, what was
remembered or revised, and when its own evidence is insufficient.

The strongest cross-domain findings are:

1. **Referent identity is load-bearing.** Vision, audio, memory, language, action, and internal
   telemetry become interoperable only when they can be joined to the same event or object without
   inferring identity from row order or superficial similarity.
2. **Time is part of the representation.** A pooled clip can support a probe, but event birth,
   persistence, occlusion, prediction, intervention, and memory require explicit temporal state.
3. **Prediction is useful only beside alternatives.** Reconstruction, invariance, random targets,
   frozen initialization, and stronger task controls are needed to learn what an objective adds.
4. **Embodiment is a causal loop.** Pixels that depict action are not embodiment. The minimum unit
   is observation, chosen or recorded action, consequence, and counterfactual comparison.
5. **Memory is an ecology.** Fast episodes, slow consolidation, replay, revision, provenance,
   forgetting, and privacy need separate controls rather than one unbounded store.
6. **Plasticity must preserve future plasticity.** Current accuracy is insufficient if adaptation
   destroys old identity, collapses dormant capacity, or makes the next task harder to learn.
7. **Operational self-models are testable without mind claims.** Resource forecasts, confidence,
   failure prediction, and causal internal-state reports can be calibrated and lesioned while the
   sentience question remains open.
8. **Multisensory contradictions are evidence.** A shared space must preserve timing, reliability,
   missingness, and disagreement rather than forcing every form into premature agreement.
9. **Development is a changing learning system.** Curriculum order alone is not development;
   growth, sensitive periods, self-generated goals, structural change, and retained learnability
   need independent measurement.
10. **Material computation is a portability frontier, not a shortcut.** Gels, nanowires, organic
    devices, reservoirs, and soft bodies matter only when their native dynamics beat tuned digital
    controls after acquisition, drift, repair, lifetime, and energy are counted.

None of these conclusions implies consciousness, sentience, agency, understanding, or general
intelligence. They define discriminating engineering and cognitive-science experiments.

### 33.3 The inherited encoder changed roles

All configured V-JEPA 2 scale points now load and execute on the current M3 Pro in serial. This
retires model availability as the architectural boundary. It does not prove that their learned
representations are special, natural-video adequate, or the right final substrate.

Their remaining legitimate roles are:

- frozen same-referent measurement baselines;
- optional cache-only teachers;
- learned-versus-random architecture controls;
- scale and geometry comparisons;
- sources of requirements and failure cases.

The owned path now trains without importing inherited model code or weights. A teacher can be
removed, swapped, or contradicted without disabling the mandatory objectives. This is the concrete
meaning of using V-JEPA as a stepping stone rather than a platform.

A newer upstream correction also changes the dense-token lane. Meta's official repository records
the V-JEPA 2.1 release on 2026-03-16 and supplies the retained dense 384px ViT-B instrument
checkpoints ([official repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2),
[primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14482)). The prior claim that E6 is blocked by an
unpublished dense checkpoint is retired. Availability still does not establish local compatibility
or scientific value: the next rung is a pinned, hashed 80M ViT-B load/token-shape/forward-parity
probe after the active training lane exits. Larger variants must be earned by that result.

### 33.4 The first owned substrate is an instrument

`TinyVideoSubstrate` is not proposed as the final cognitive architecture. Its value is that it
makes previously verbal choices measurable on the local host:

- how many and what kind of temporal tokens to retain;
- whether prediction, invariance, or reconstruction changes held-out factor structure;
- whether a learned arm beats the exact frozen initialization and a compute-matched random target;
- whether source, data, requirement, and checkpoint identity survive a long run;
- what one million to five million parameters cost in time, memory, storage, and estimated FLOPs;
- whether an optional teacher adds anything after teacher-free learning is controlled.

If all learned objectives tie the controls under corrected uncertainty, CM7 closes this particular
architecture/objective line. The project still keeps the referent, provenance, token, checkpoint,
and verifier contracts. A null therefore informs the later substrate instead of merely failing to
produce a model.

### 33.5 Natural data changes what can be believed

Programmatic clips are valuable because factors and interventions are exact. They are dangerous
because small models can exploit generator regularities and because replicated nuisances are not
independent natural referents. The natural bridge is not a cosmetic dataset swap. It must add:

- rights and privacy authority;
- session-level independent units;
- official split discipline;
- object-level source hashes and generation identity;
- environmental, motion, visibility, and temporal nuisance;
- compact reproducible derivatives under the local disk floor;
- exact referent joins for teacher, student, forms, and controls.

The bounded SANPO intake establishes that data plane. Its first cohort is mechanics and provenance
evidence only. More sessions and a frozen official-test evaluation are needed before any natural-
video conclusion.

### 33.6 What changed in the project

Before this integration, the project could be read as a rigorous shell around a frozen inherited
encoder, with custom training and many frontier rows deferred to Studio. It now has:

- verified local execution of the retained ViT-B instrument and archived the old scale pilot;
- zero experiment-specific measured hardware blockers;
- an approved 300-minute adaptive operational profile, with frozen scientific shard identities;
- a teacher-independent trainable video substrate;
- experiment-derived machine-readable architecture requirements;
- exact source/evidence/checkpoint snapshotting;
- a natural-video provenance and consumption lane;
- an explicit random-architecture control campaign;
- a future offline portable artifact boundary with no Hugging Face/V-JEPA dependency;
- a causal blocker taxonomy that separates data, rights, environment, implementation, upstream
  model, and measured hardware.

The priority has shifted from acquiring a presumed larger machine to exhausting decisive small
experiments. Extended compute remains useful if it earns its role through referent diversity,
temporal duration, replication, environments, or full-system throughput. It is not the organizing
principle and it is not a proxy for scientific seriousness.

### 33.7 Immediate interpretation boundary

The live work supports only these conclusions:

- the retired inherited-scale checkpoints ran locally in bounded serial form but are no longer active;
- a small project-owned temporal encoder can be trained locally;
- the scientific controls and data paths can be made executable on this host;
- most former frontier labels were dependency labels, not measured compute limits.
